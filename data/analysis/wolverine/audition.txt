You saw a rescue. But Jean Grey freed Logan from Apocalypse by erasing his past, and Marvel's Wolverine told you the cost before she did it.

It is easy to read the ending as a sad rescue. Logan survives and walks away with a clean slate. This video argues the other side. The ending is an erasure, and the game sets it up long before the final fight. So did Jean rescue Logan, or did she erase him? Fair warning: full spoilers for the whole game, including the post-credits scene.

The answer comes down to one warning Jean gives and four words Logan says back. The four words sound kind. By the end of this video they'll sound like the saddest line in the game. Getting there takes a story Jean tells about the 1800s, and a villain who spent the whole game reaching into Logan's head too.
