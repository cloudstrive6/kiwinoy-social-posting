# GTA 6 footage facts (verified from KG's own footage)

Facts agents verified in KG's recordings, so the footage log and the claims check can name people, places and
vehicles without asking Bryce (Bryce, 2026-11-23: "You/agents should be able to know this by reviewing the
footages"). Add a fact only with the clip and time that shows it.

## Characters
- **Jason Duval**: short blond hair, beard, muscular. Playable. Seen at his wardrobe in
  `cutscenes/gta6/GTA 6 (2026-11-21 21-42-41) p02.mp4` 3:00 to 3:28, and in the mod shop session of the same
  recording (p06 to p08).
- **Lucia Caminos**: long dark curly hair. Playable. Uses the motel ATM in `GTA 6 (2026-11-21 23-37-28) p01.mp4`
  about 3:00 to 3:30; stops the robbery at Tucker's in p05 0:37 to 1:04.
- **Moses**: runs the Watkins garage and tire yard, buys and registers cars. On-screen notices call him "Moses";
  his surname is never shown or said in KG's footage. `GTA 6 (2026-11-22 01-00-16) p11.mp4`.
- **Linda**: the motel manager (objective "SEE LINDA, THE MANAGER").
- **Brian**: the fence at Brian's Lockup; his payouts show on the bank statement as "Brian".

## Places
- **Jason's house**: a stilt house on a canal. Jason wakes there, dresses from his own wardrobe and saved outfits,
  and his black Ganado is parked outside next to a jet ski (`21-42-41 p02.mp4` 2:35 to 3:48).
- **Watkins (Moses's yard)**: tire service and car yard where cars are scanned, sold and registered (`01-00-16 p11.mp4`).
- **Tucker's Citrus Farms**: farm stand; the robbery Lucia stopped (`23-37-28 p05.mp4`).
- **Coastal Auto Care**: mod shop; parts are paid from the bank, the $60 repair from cash (`21-42-41 p06 to p08`).

## Money and HUD
- The top-right HUD shows the bank (purple "b" icon) above the cash (green wallet icon).
- Jason and Lucia share one bank account: Lucia's ATM statement lists Jason's Coastal Auto Care charges and Brian's
  payouts (`23-37-28 p01.mp4` about 3:10).

## Dates
- KG's first GTA 6 recordings are dated 2026-11-19 (launch day); the file times are 24-hour local times (for
  example 12-47-53 is 12:47 pm).
