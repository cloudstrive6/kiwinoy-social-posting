Tip number one, and it is the most important tip in this whole video. Bank your cash. Whatever you carry is lost when you die. Guides say the same goes for getting busted. I had five hundred and thirty-one dollars on me on launch night. One plane crash and one shootout later, my HUD said zero. Guides also report a small fee from your BuckMe bank app when you go down. So every time you pass an ATM with real money on you, stop and deposit it. Cash in the bank survives. Cash in your pocket is only on loan. Every robbery fills your pocket. So bank it before the next one. Tip number two. You deposit at an ATM, in person. A lot of new players think the phone app can bank their cash. It cannot. Guides agree your phone will not move pocket cash into the bank. ATMs sit in places you already visit. Lucia's motel has one in the lobby. The convenience store has one by the magazines. Find the ATM before you find the action.
