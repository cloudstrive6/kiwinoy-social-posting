Phantom Blade Zero got the spotlight during a recent PlayStation State of Play, treating viewers to about 20 minutes of new gameplay footage and story cutscenes. While many fans were hoping for a playable demo—especially after supposed sightings on the PlayStation Store—there was no official announcement or release of one during the broadcast, as reported by PushSquare.

Presented in part by martial arts legend Donnie Yen, the deep dive covered a wide range of features, including the game's narrative, character lineup, atmospheric world, and progression mechanics. The most attention-grabbing moments explored the fast-paced combat and multiple difficulty modes. These range from the accessible "Wayfarer" cinematic experience to the punishing "66 Days" challenge, which imposes a strict 66-life limit. Lose them all, and it's game over for good.

Developer S-GAME also shed some light on the unique enemy AI that will adapt to each player's tactics on the higher difficulty settings. This twist demands players stay on their toes, preventing familiar patterns from making fights too predictable or easy.

The showcase wrapped up with a quick look at how Phantom Blade Zero will utilize the PS5's hardware features such as adaptive triggers, haptic feedback, and 3D audio—an encouraging sign given Sony's backing of the game as a timed console exclusive. However, fans still have some questions, especially regarding a potential physical edition and whether a demo will arrive in the future.

Phantom Blade Zero continues to draw interest thanks to its stylish presentation and action-packed gameplay, though some viewers noted the footage occasionally suffered from choppy frame rates. For now, eager players will have to wait for more news—and for that anticipated demo to finally make its debut.
