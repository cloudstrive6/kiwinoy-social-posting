Arc Raiders players are in for a longer wait before their next chance to reset and prestige their Raiders. **Embark Studios has announced that all future Expeditions will be delayed until early 2027**, with the development team seeking to implement major improvements in response to player feedback. The confirmation arrives just as **Expedition 5 is set to run September 8–29**, marking the last active run before the extended hiatus begins.

## Why Expeditions Are Being Paused
According to Embark Studios, the decision follows an in-depth review of community comments and experiences with Expeditions—a late-game mechanic that encourages replayability via reset progression. While the studio has made "ongoing improvements," some issues raised by players, such as **tight departure windows, reward structure, and newcomer accessibility**, require more substantial changes rather than small tweaks. Instead of sticking with incremental updates, Embark is banking on a significant overhaul, with more details promised in September.

During the pause, buffs earned from previous Expeditions will be preserved. Additionally, players who finish every Expedition to date will unlock the **Zenith outfit**, while those with incomplete Caravans will see their progress replaced with Ermal Vouchers of equivalent value, tradeable for various in-game items or currency.

## Other Live Service Updates and Community Reaction
The delay isn’t the only change on the horizon. Embark also revealed that **weekly Trials will go on hold from September 22 to October 8**. Although Arc Raiders continues to attract a steady—if smaller—community after its 2025 debut, some fans have voiced frustration with the live service cadence, noting sparse updates and the risk of diminishing long-term progression. The upcoming **Frozen Trail** update, scheduled for October, will bring a new map and some of the "biggest" changes yet, but many players remain cautious, hoping the next chapter delivers on promised improvements.
