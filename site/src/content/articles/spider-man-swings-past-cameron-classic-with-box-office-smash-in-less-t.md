Spider-Man has proven once again that when it comes to box office dominance, the friendly neighborhood hero is in a league of his own. According to a new report from Collider, the latest Spider-Man movie has surpassed James Cameron’s most successful film at the global box office—doing so in record time, clocking the achievement in just under 20 days since its release.

While James Cameron holds two of the biggest cinematic successes in movie history—Titanic and Avatar—it was his most lucrative offering that Spider-Man overtook this week. The report didn’t specify which title, but Cameron’s Avatar has long maintained a stronghold on all-time highest-grossing records. Now, Marvel Studios’ iconic wall-crawler has swung firmly into the lead, turning heads across the industry with both its critical acclaim and financial success.

Analysts credit the film’s lightning-fast momentum to a combination of fan anticipation, positive word-of-mouth, and the enduring popularity of both the character and Marvel’s storytelling. Box office watchers say Spider-Man’s cross-generational appeal and its integration into the wider MCU have helped drive exceptional turnout both domestically and worldwide.

Official tally details remain closely watched, but early reports indicate that Spider-Man could keep climbing as the year’s box office titan, possibly setting up more records as the weeks go on. Marvel fans can rest assured: with feats like this, Spidey continues to soar, both on screen and in the history books.
