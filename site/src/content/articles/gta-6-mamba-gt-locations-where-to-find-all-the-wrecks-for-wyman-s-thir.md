Tracking down the **GTA 6 Mamba GT locations** is how you restore the third classic car **Wyman** asks you to help with. After you finish the **Transgressor** and then the **Riata**, he sends a text with details about fixing this classic 60s roadster, and this one is a little more involved than the earlier cars.

## How the Mamba GT hunt works

There are **four Mamba GTs** to scan with the **WAiNK It** app in the counties around **Port Gellhorn**. That's more legwork than the previous two restorations, so it helps to know where each wreck is before you set out.

## The first three locations

**Farmhouse garage north of Leonida State Prison:** The first Mamba GT sits in a farmhouse garage north of **Leonida State Prison**, southeast of Gellhorn. Head north up the main road east of the prison until you spot a private property gate on the left, opposite a building with **Gruppe6** written on it. Smash the gate and follow the track to the house. The wreck is in the garage to the left of it, and you can enter through the back door. There's also a little cash in a box inside.

**Garage near the Ambrosia gas station:** The second wreck is in the town of **Ambrosia**, east of Port Gellhorn, near the **Xero Gas - Ambrosia** station and the purchasable **Wyatt Motors** garage. Head down the side of the gas station, turn right into the alley and continue until you see a white garage door on your right, next to a big blue garage door. Interact with the white one to open it and scan the Mamba GT inside.

**Pool at the Thrillbilly Mud Club:** The third Mamba GT is sunk in a pool at the **Thrillbilly Mud Club**. You'll have visited this spot during the story with Wyman, when you met Billy and had a race, east of Port Gellhorn.

The fourth location, in the wreckage of a bridge near **Bonanza**, is listed in the guide's contents, but the details were not included in the source text, so we can't confirm exactly where to find it here.
