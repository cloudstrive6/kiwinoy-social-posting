The third chapter in what’s being called **Wanda’s trilogy of Disney+ shows** is nearly here. **VisionQuest** arrives on October 14, set to continue the psychedelic and emotional journey that started with WandaVision and was followed by Agatha All Along. This time, Vision takes center stage, as the new series aims to unravel what became of the character after the dramatic finale of WandaVision.

## Vision’s Search for Self

VisionQuest promises to resolve the enigmas left by WandaVision, especially around the two Visions: the one conjured by Wanda and the White Vision introduced in the Hex. The newly released trailer hints that the show will pick up exactly where audiences last saw Vision, now in possession of his original memories and searching for his true identity. The series is also set to bring back **Ultron**—the infamous villain from Avengers: Age of Ultron—as well as Wanda and Vision’s son Tommy, further deepening the character’s ties to his MCU origins.

## A Mind-Bending New Setting

In a recent interview, Vision actor Paul Bettany described VisionQuest’s setting as a wild and imaginative "mind space" reflected as a mansion within Vision’s own consciousness, featuring a series of interchangeable rooms and a host of AI characters, including Ultron. Bettany highlighted that these different AI avatars each bring something unique to help Vision analyze what it means to be alive. Thematically, VisionQuest is being billed as a "massive swing"—much like WandaVision—a show unafraid to embrace the bizarre and the eccentric.

The strongest connection between VisionQuest and WandaVision may ultimately be on a thematic level, exploring identity and existence more than direct narrative continuation. Still, with Tommy returning and such a surreal premise promised, fans are watching closely for any possible appearances by Wanda herself. Whether or not that hope is fulfilled, VisionQuest looks set to offer a fresh, ambitious perspective on one of the MCU’s most complex heroes.
