**Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4’s** signature DMZ extraction mode just received a notable change: **Story Missions are now private for solo players—at least on their first attempt.** While Infinity Ward hasn’t made any formal announcement, fans have observed the update, sparking discussion across the community about the future of PvE and PvPvE gameplay in the popular mode.

## The Solo Story Mission Experience

The DMZ mode has always emphasized a mix of PvP and PvE action, much to the frustration of players hoping for a purely PvE adventure. Until now, Story Missions in DMZ weren’t truly private—even with fewer players during these tasks, solo operators could still stumble upon unwanted PvP encounters. The latest adjustment changes that, as solo players engaging in a Story Mission for the first time now appear to receive a genuinely private instance. In testing, players have noticed no other human operators, no activity on the Recent Players social tab, and an absence of telltale skirmishes like activated UAV Towers or SAM Sites. It’s a significant quality-of-life upgrade for those seeking the narrative without the chaos of open-world PvP.

## Still Not a True PvE Mode

There are caveats—the new privacy only applies to your first attempt at a Story Mission, with any repeat runs returning to the more hazardous, shared matchmaking experience. And while most evidence points to complete privacy during that initial run, there are hints that some overlap with other players might still occur, so caution remains wise. The change highlights an ongoing split in the DMZ community: a growing contingent longs for PvE-only servers, especially as new players enter the game expecting less risk and more story-driven content. Infinity Ward’s tweak offers a partial answer, but the underlying debate about the fundamental design of DMZ continues.
