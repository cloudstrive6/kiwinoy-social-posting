**Insomniac’s next Marvel project may be assembling sooner than expected.** Fresh details have surfaced about the studio’s long-rumored X-Men video game, after months of speculation following the release of Marvel’s Wolverine. While the team has not officially spoken about this upcoming title, a new staff listing from Insomniac offers concrete hints — and the first tease of what fans can expect when they finally get their hands on the controller.

## Multiple Heroes, Multiple Abilities

According to a newly discovered job posting, Insomniac is seeking a **Staff Hero Designer** who will be responsible for creating new playable characters for an unannounced project. The listing stops short of naming the X-Men directly, but the timing, right on the heels of Marvel’s Wolverine, strongly suggests mutant business is underway. The most exciting nugget: the X-Men game looks set to include **multiple playable characters**, each likely bringing a unique set of powers and skills. This stands apart from Marvel’s Wolverine, which focused solely on the adamantium-clawed hero, and aligns with what fans would hope for from an X-Men experience.

## X-Men Still a Ways Off

Even with these new hints, Insomniac’s X-Men project isn’t expected to debut anytime soon. Marvel’s Spider-Man 3 is widely assumed to be the studio’s next big title, and reports point to a potential new Ratchet and Clank game also in development. This means X-Men probably won’t launch until much later, with fans possibly waiting well into the next console generation before seeing an official reveal or gameplay.
