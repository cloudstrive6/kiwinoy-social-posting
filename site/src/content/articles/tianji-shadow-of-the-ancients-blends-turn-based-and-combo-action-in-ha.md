A fresh RPG out of Taiwan has just been revealed for PlayStation 5, and it’s turning heads with its striking visuals and inventive combat mechanics. **Tianji: Shadow of the Ancients** from developer **Justdan** is set for a global release next year, and its first trailer already sets it apart in a crowded genre.

## What Makes It Stand Out

Tianji: Shadow of the Ancients introduces a combat system that aims to bridge the best of both worlds: **classic turn-based commands combined with real-time combo chaining**. Players can launch a standard attack and then extend their assault by linking additional moves, keeping enemies pressured and allowing for greater tactical depth. According to the official press release, these battles are designed to deliver “the satisfying impact of an action game” while still rewarding strategic play, promising an experience that could appeal to fans of both action and traditional RPGs.

## Hand-Drawn Art and Gorgeous Maps

Visually, Tianji sets itself apart with a **fully hand-drawn art style**. This approach brings not just character models, but also the overworld map, to life in a way that’s hard not to notice. Early footage showcases a world rich in detail, helping Tianji carve out its own identity among PS5’s upcoming RPG lineup. The lush visuals already have fans talking, especially with the map itself turning heads as a standout element.

## Release Outlook

With a **worldwide launch planned for next year on PS5**, Tianji: Shadow of the Ancients is shaping up to be one of the more intriguing original RPGs on the horizon. While details on the story and deeper mechanics are still under wraps, the combination of expressive art and strategic-yet-punchy combat has certainly piqued our interest.
