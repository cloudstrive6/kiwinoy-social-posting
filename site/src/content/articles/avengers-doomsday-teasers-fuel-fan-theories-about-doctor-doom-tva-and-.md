**Avengers: Doomsday** continues to generate buzz and speculation with its tantalizing teasers and new promotional art, ramping up fan theories across the MCU community. With **Robert Downey Jr. confirmed as Doctor Doom** and **Chris Evans returning as Steve Rogers**, the movie is already loaded with mystery—especially as both characters’ returns raise major questions about the fallout of Avengers: Endgame and the nature of their roles in Doomsday’s unfolding plot.

## Returning Faces and Multiverse Teases
The return of Downey Jr. and Evans has fans questioning whether Doctor Doom might be a variant of Tony Stark or if Steve Rogers' timeline journey is set to become even more complex. Theories are running wild as viewers try to piece together how the events of Endgame may be fueling the next major conflict. Meanwhile, fans still have no confirmation on which other familiar faces might appear—there’s hope for a Scarlet Witch return, but details remain unconfirmed.

Adding even more intrigue, new art from Doomsday merchandise has ignited debate online. Eagle-eyed fans believe they've spotted the **Time Variance Authority (TVA)** in the background between **Sam Wilson** and Doctor Doom. This piece of visual evidence has sent speculation into overdrive, with some suggesting that the TVA could play a central role in the plot, especially given Loki’s confirmed presence in the movie.

## Theories and Rumors In Overdrive
With so much about the actual story still under wraps, every small detail is being analyzed. The idea that Doctor Doom may have a grievance with the Avengers—and potentially a vendetta against the TVA—fits well within the teasers where Doom accuses the Avengers of living “stolen lives.” If the TVA is indeed involved, it could shake up the entire MCU status quo, introducing many heroes to the existence of the TVA and changing how they perceive the multiverse. However, as of now, all of this remains **unconfirmed** and firmly in the realm of rumor, based on one intriguing piece of art.
