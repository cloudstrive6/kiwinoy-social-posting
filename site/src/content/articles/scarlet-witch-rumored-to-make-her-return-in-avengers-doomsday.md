With fan buzz over Spider-Man: Brand New Day settling down, **MCU fans are now laser-focused on three major upcoming movies: Avengers: Doomsday, Avengers: Secret Wars, and X-Men**. While projects like Ryan Gosling’s Ghost Rider and a possible Shang-Chi sequel have their own followings, these three films are already being seen as potential milestones for the franchise’s future. In particular, **Avengers: Doomsday** is shaping up as the film that may set the tone for everything that follows—especially Secret Wars.

## Key Characters and Unanswered Questions
MCU devotees have a lot riding on Doomsday. **Questions swirl around the returns of Chris Evans as Steve Rogers and Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom**, both of which could have significant impacts on fan sentiment. Any disruption to Steve’s Avengers: Endgame ending, for example, is likely to be controversial. Meanwhile, Downey Jr.'s return as Doctor Doom has also sparked intense discussion, though official details remain scarce.

## Wanda Maximoff’s Possible Comeback
Perhaps the most exciting rumor involves the fan-favorite Scarlet Witch. According to recent reports, **Elizabeth Olsen is expected to reprise her role as Wanda Maximoff in Avengers: Doomsday**, following new reshoots for the film. While this news remains unconfirmed and hasn’t been officially announced by Marvel, Disney, or Olsen herself, it’s fueling fresh speculation. Wanda’s on-screen journey has felt incomplete since her apparent demise in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, especially after recent reveals in Agatha All Along and VisionQuest that her children may still be alive in her universe. In the comics, Wanda and Doctor Doom share a storyline that could easily fit within this new phase. Whether the MCU will touch on those comic arcs is yet to be seen, but Wanda’s rumored return offers hope for closure – or at least the next chapter.
