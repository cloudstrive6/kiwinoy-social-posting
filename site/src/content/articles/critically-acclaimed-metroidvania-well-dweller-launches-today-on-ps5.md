A surprise hit has landed on PS5 today: **Well Dweller**, a dark fairy-tale Metroidvania crafted by solo developer Kyle Thompson, is now available and already boasts **some of the highest review scores of 2026**. With a **Metacritic rating of 91** and an **Opencritic score of 90** after 16 reviews, Well Dweller has officially joined the ranks of the most acclaimed new releases this year.

## Critical Success for a Solo Dev

Best known for his previous indie gem, Crypt Custodian, Thompson’s latest project is winning over critics with its **polished gameplay, rewarding exploration, and unique approach** to the genre. Well Dweller places players in the talons of **Glimmer, a tiny bird armed with a matchstick**, embarking on a quest to face down a wicked queen and save his family. The game stands out thanks to tight platforming, fluid movement, and meaningful customization, crafting an experience that’s both challenging and satisfying.

According to reviewers, combat feels responsive, platforming continually innovates, and exploration is engaging without being overwhelming. Notably, 100% of critics on Opencritic recommend the game, earning it the site’s fourth highest rating for 2026. The PC version also holds a top-tier Metacritic score, cementing Well Dweller’s reputation as a must-play for fans of the genre.

## A Dark Twist on the Metroidvania Formula

Well Dweller isn't just another nostalgia trip—it injects its fairy-tale world with quirky humor and approachable design. From useful tools to helpful hints that make completion less daunting than many Metroidvanias, the game manages to strike the right balance between difficulty and accessibility. Priced at **$19.99 (£17.99)**, it offers an exciting new adventure for PlayStation 5 owners—and is already being hailed as one of the best indie releases in recent years.
