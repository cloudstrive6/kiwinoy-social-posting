Marvel Studios continues to shake up its roster, with new casting developments for upcoming films—and now, rising star **Emma Myers (Wednesday, A Minecraft Movie)** is reportedly being considered for a major new role in the **Marvel Cinematic Universe**. The news surfaced after several notable casting announcements at recent events, including the next Black Panther (David Jonsson) and new X-Men members like **Maya Boyd (Storm)** and **Christopher Abbott (Professor X)**.

## Who Could Emma Myers Play?

According to well-circulated rumors, **Myers is being eyed for a "huge" MCU role**, sparking a wave of speculation among Marvel fans online. While official details haven't been released, commenters are already placing bets on potential characters—including **Gwenpool** and **Spider-Gwen**. While Spider-Gwen's rights reportedly remain with Sony and her future in the MCU is uncertain, Gwenpool (Gwendolyn Poole) could be a strong fit, thanks to her unique comic book origins and direct ties to existing MCU heroes like Kate Bishop and America Chavez.

Other fans have thrown alternative picks into the mix, naming **Valeria Richards** (possibly joining a newly imagined Fantastic Four family), **Kitty Pryde**, **Jubilee**, **Mary Jane Watson**, and **Black Cat** as possible roles for Myers. The actress is best known for her performance as Enid Sinclair in Wednesday, a break-out part that propelled her to wider attention.

## The Big Picture for Marvel's Upcoming Slate

These rumors emerge as Marvel's new era takes shape, with key roles already cast for the X-Men reboot and other major properties. **Adam Driver** has signed on as Mister Sinister, Sadie Sink is set for Jean Grey, and Samara Weaving joins as Emma Frost. With fan speculation at a fever pitch, Emma Myers’ potential entry could add another dynamic presence to the expanding MCU lineup—though nothing has been officially confirmed yet.
