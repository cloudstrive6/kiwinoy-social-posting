**Toy Story 3 Complete Edition** is making a grand return, with Digital Eclipse set to release the remastered fan-favorite platformer **on October 15**. Dropping alongside **Toy Story: Retro Roundup**, a compilation of classic Pixar-inspired games, the remaster brings new life to one of the most beloved movie tie-ins in gaming history, and fans have something to look forward to with a brand-new set of Trophies revealed just ahead of launch.

## What’s New in the Complete Edition

According to early details on PSNProfiles, the **Trophy list in Toy Story 3 Complete Edition mirrors the original PlayStation 3 version**, but with an important twist: **three all-new Trophies** have been added for returning fans and completionists. While the main objectives remain — completing every story chapter, hunting down collectibles, and tackling unique challenges like reaching Zurg’s base unscathed — these updates add extra incentive to jump back in.

A major addition is the wider availability of **Zurg as a playable character**. Previously exclusive to the PS3, Zurg can now be used on all platforms, and **two of the new Trophies** involve completing objectives with this iconic villain. The third new Trophy centers on collecting every customization pack from the in-game Toy Store, giving Toy Box mode veterans another reason to dive into the game’s creative sandbox.

## Why This Remaster Matters

With its blend of story-driven platforming and the acclaimed Toy Box mode, **Toy Story 3** remains a standout among movie-based games — and the Complete Edition aims to recapture that magic for both nostalgic fans and new players alike. Whether you loved building out your own custom town, unlocking quirky props, or just want an accessible game for family co-op, Digital Eclipse’s remaster ensures the essential experience is preserved with a little something extra for those chasing 100% completion.
