The PS5 shooter Saros has rolled out its significant Zenith update, introducing several features geared toward making the game both **more accessible for newcomers** and **more replayable for experienced players**. Key additions include a new quickfire boss-rush mode, a New Game Plus option, an array of gameplay modifiers, and a highly-requested slow-motion feature that lets players fine-tune the game speed to their preference.

## New Modes and Modifiers

The update's standout features are the **Overlord Rush boss-rush mode**—ideal for short, challenging play sessions—and a New Game Plus mode called **Eclipse Layers**. Eclipse Layers lets you replay the game with altered rules and increased intensity, unlocking additional story content as you go. Players can modify their runs with a whole new layer of challenges or advantages: **Protection modifiers** offer helpful boosts, such as the powerful Corruption Melee Enhancement, while **Trial modifiers** ramp up the difficulty, with options like Hostile Regeneration forcing you to adapt your combat strategy.

## Accessibility Gets a Major Boost

A highlight of the Zenith update is its **Slow Game Speed option**. This feature lets players control exactly how much to slow down the frenetic action, with manual or automatic activation. The developer, Housemarque, emphasizes that this addition is about providing space to think and react without diluting Saros’s combat intensity—so the "bullet ballet" underpinnings remain firmly intact.

Progressing through Eclipse Layers not only amps up difficulty but also changes up the look of the protagonist, Arjun, with a glowing crown and golden armor that become more prominent as you activate more modifiers. This crown is also the key to unlocking **12 new story visions** lurking within the expanded game.

## Replayability For the Long Haul

For those hungry for a true challenge, **Overlord Rush’s Totality set** unleashes all eight bosses in one gigantic gauntlet, while regular sets offer more bite-sized boss battles. Saros originally launched to solid reviews but had a modest start sales-wise. However, Housemarque appears committed to evolving the experience for some time yet, with Zenith as a major step in that ongoing journey.
