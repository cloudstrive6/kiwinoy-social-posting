Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is shining a spotlight on its revamped DMZ mode this week, generating a frenzy of speculation after glimpses of a mysterious 'Single Squad Mission' option surfaced during official behind-the-scenes footage. As players dissect every detail, the question of whether DMZ will support a **PvE-only playlist** remains unanswered.

## Hints from Official Footage
In the seventh episode of Infinity Ward's development series, viewers spotted a deployment screen featuring **four options**, with three described in detail. The fourth, "Single Squad Mission," stood out for its lack of explanation, leading players to hope it signals a private, PvE-focused experience limited to a single squad. The mode's name has fueled theorizing among the community, with some believing it means only invited players—potentially sheltered from PvP threats—can participate. Others suspect it's tied to DMZ's Lieutenant Hunts, where teams extract with unique tokens, but there's been no official word to settle the debate.

## Theories and What We Still Don’t Know
Adding to the intrigue, a subsequent DMZ trailer released just a day later failed to display the Single Squad option at all. The absence led fans to speculate on whether this feature is limited to early builds, has been delayed, or perhaps serves as an onboarding tool for new players. Previous shooters have provided low-pressure experiences or bot-only matches for newcomers, fueling theories about DMZ taking a similar path. While Infinity Ward is keeping details close to its chest, the community's interest highlights persistent demand for a dedicated PvE mode in extraction shooters.

DMZ will be available as part of Modern Warfare 4 when the game launches on **PC, PS5, Xbox Series X/S, and Switch 2**. Whether PvE fans will get their wish at launch—or further down the line—remains to be seen.
