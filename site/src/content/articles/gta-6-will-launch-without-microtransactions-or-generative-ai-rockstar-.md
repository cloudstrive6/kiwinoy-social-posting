**Rockstar Games has officially confirmed that Grand Theft Auto 6 will launch without microtransactions or generative AI integration**. The announcement came during a group interview ahead of the GTA 6: Extended Look event, where the development team addressed mounting questions about the highly anticipated open-world sequel’s design philosophies and monetization plans.

## A Different Approach

When pressed on the possibility of paying real money for in-game purchases, Rockstar stated there are **“no monetisations in this game”**. The studio also clarified that **no generative AI technology is present anywhere in GTA 6’s development**. This stance comes amid a wider industry shift toward implementing AI tools and in-game spending options, with various studios either investing heavily in AI or introducing new forms of DLC and microtransactions. Rockstar’s parent company, Take-Two Interactive, previously laid off its AI team earlier this year, and CEO Strauss Zelnick has been outspoken about his skepticism regarding AI-generated creativity in games.

## What’s Included (and What’s Not)

While GTA 6 won’t feature microtransactions, there **will be gated content in the game’s Ultimate Edition**, which costs more than the standard edition. The **Ultimate Edition** includes exclusive cosmetics, vehicles, weapons, and access to shops and services—such as the Electric Fang tattoo parlour and Stock 305 clothing store—not available in the base game. Players with the standard edition will be able to upgrade later if they choose. Rockstar notes that this content is “threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia’s story” and unlocks incrementally as you play.

**Grand Theft Auto 6 is set for release on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S on November 19**. This week’s information blowout also brought details about a revamped Wanted system, new carjacking mechanics, a romance system, and a massive, activity-packed game world—all reinforcing that Rockstar is aiming for ambitious realism this time around.
