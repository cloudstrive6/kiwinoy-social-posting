The **Golden Joystick Awards** have officially opened voting for their **Ultimate Game of the Year**, marking the 44th consecutive year of one of gaming's most beloved honors. With an impressive list of nominees—including some of the most talked-about releases of the year—fans have until **8 pm ET / 12 am GMT on October 30** to cast their votes and have their say in this hotly contested showdown.

## This Year’s Heavy Hitters
There’s no shortage of competition this year, with several standout titles vying for the crown. Among the nominees are games like **Crimson Desert**, lauded for its massive technical ambition and a flood of updates that have kept players engaged throughout the year. Other favorites making waves include **Star Wars: Galactic Racer**, a title so engrossing it’s inspired actual gaming calluses, and **Dispatch**, a superhero game noted for its memorable, tightly crafted story. The full official list of nominees has been published, representing a packed field of 2026’s finest interactive experiences.

## Award Show Details
The main event will be hosted by **Alix Wilton Regan**, the new voice of Lara Croft and a familiar voice actor in games such as Veilguard and Inquisition. She promises a night that celebrates not only the games themselves but the development teams who bring fantastic worlds to life—complete with special guest appearances, standout titles, and plenty of fun along the way. The Golden Joystick Awards show will debut across all major streaming platforms, including **YouTube, Twitch, and X** on **Wednesday, November 11**. With voting now open at goldenjoysticks.com, fans have just a few days left to help determine which game claims the year’s top honors.
