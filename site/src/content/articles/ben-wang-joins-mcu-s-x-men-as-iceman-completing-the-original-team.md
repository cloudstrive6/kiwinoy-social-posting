Marvel’s X-Men movie has officially found its Iceman, with **Ben Wang** reportedly entering talks to play **Bobby Drake** in the highly anticipated MCU reboot. With this casting, the MCU’s take on the X-Men is set to feature all five founding members — Cyclops, Jean Grey, Beast, Angel, and Iceman — together onscreen for the first time.

## Bringing the Original Five to the MCU
The X-Men movie’s casting announcements began at D23 Expo, revealing **Sadie Sink as Jean Grey**, **Kit Connor as Cyclops**, and **Maya Boyd as Storm**. Since then, more actors have joined the project, including **Asa Germann as Angel** and **Peter Claffey as Beast**. With Wang’s addition, the team mirrors the classic X-Men lineup from the comics, finally uniting Cyclops, Jean Grey, Beast, Angel, and Iceman in a single film. While previous films included these characters across different entries, the core team has never shared the screen as a unit until now.

## Who Is Ben Wang—and What’s Next for Iceman?
Ben Wang is known for recent roles in projects like **The Long Walk** and **Karate Kid: Legends**, alongside his lead performance in Disney+’s "American Born Chinese." He’ll soon appear in "The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping." Several other actors reportedly auditioned for Iceman, including **Gabriel LaBelle**, **Jackson Kelly**, and **Rhenzy Feliz**, but Wang ultimately landed the role. Notably, while Bobby Drake is often the youngest member of the original team in Marvel lore, Wang is actually among the older cast members (besides Peter Claffey).

## What Could This Mean for Iceman’s Story?
One area of speculation surrounds how the MCU will handle Iceman’s character. While Bobby Drake was later revealed to be gay in the Marvel comics, it is still unknown whether the upcoming film will address this part of his identity or how it might impact his storyline on screen. The MCU has yet to confirm how closely it will follow this version of the character’s journey.
