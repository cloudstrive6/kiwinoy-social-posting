In a major blow for fans of innovative game design, **Liquid Swords—the Swedish studio behind Samson—has been declared bankrupt just six months after its debut title launched**. The news comes on the heels of persistent financial and development troubles, closing the book on the ambitious crime sim.

## Behind the Shutdown

Liquid Swords was established in **2020** by **Christofer Sundberg**, best known for his earlier work at Avalanche Studios on the Just Cause series. The studio's first project, **Samson**, set players loose as a getaway driver in an open-city underworld, blending high-speed action with a distinctive run-based progression. Despite an inventive concept, the company faced challenges from the beginning. The initial vision for Samson was a larger action RPG, but the team struggled to secure publishing support—at one point facing **five rejections from a single publisher**. Without necessary backing, the studio laid off half its staff last year and pivoted Samson’s gameplay structure into its final format.

## What Happens Next?

The situation grew increasingly dire in recent months, with approximately **30 employees going unpaid for at least two months** before they themselves petitioned for bankruptcy. Although Liquid Swords managed to release a major patch in October, mixed critical reception and ongoing bugs meant Samson never found the audience it needed to sustain the business.

Now, an administrator has placed Liquid Swords’ assets—including Samson—up for sale, with a bidding process open until **October 21**. For affected employees, Sweden’s state wage guarantee will cover unpaid salaries, offering some relief. The fate of both the studio’s intellectual property and its distinctive take on the open-world crime genre will depend on whether a buyer comes forward.
