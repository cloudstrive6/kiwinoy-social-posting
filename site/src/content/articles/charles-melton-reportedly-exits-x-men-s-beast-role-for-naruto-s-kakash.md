**Charles Melton is no longer expected to play Beast in Marvel's new X-Men film, with unconfirmed reports pointing to a major role switch: Melton has apparently joined Lionsgate's live-action Naruto movie as Kakashi Hatake, leaving the iconic mutant spot open for another rising star.** Industry insider Knight Gambit (Caleb Williams) ignited the speculation, suggesting this casting shakeup brings a new face into the X-Men fold and upends earlier expectations around Melton’s Marvel involvement.

## Who Might Replace Melton?

**With Melton seemingly out, all eyes turn to Peter Claffey as the likely frontrunner for Beast.** Claffey drew critical acclaim as Ser Duncan the Tall in A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms—a performance that helped net the series a slew of Emmy nominations and earned him plenty of buzz as a versatile actor. While some fans question if Claffey can capture Beast’s signature intelligence and wit, others point out that the character’s brilliance comes as much from writing as actor delivery. Ultimately, Claffey’s casting hinges on schedules not conflicting with his ongoing role in A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms, which has already been renewed for more seasons.

## The Next-Gen X-Men Cast Takes Shape

**The current rumored MCU X-Men cast includes Sadie Sink (Jean Grey), Maya Boyd (Storm), Kit Connor (Scott Summers/Cyclops), Samara Weaving (Emma Frost), Christopher Abbott (Professor X), Inde Navarette (Rogue), Adam Driver (Mister Sinister), and Asa Germann (Angel).** If Claffey officially steps into the role of Hank McCoy, he joins a powerhouse ensemble clearly aimed at redefining Marvel’s mutant heroes for a new era. Fans continue to debate whether actors can juggle blockbuster projects, though precedent exists for stars balancing multiple major roles. For now, the X-Men lineup appears headed for even greater ensemble strength, with Claffey’s potential Beast raising both excitement and questions about the film’s future direction.
