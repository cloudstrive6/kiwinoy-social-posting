With the release of **Marvel’s Wolverine** just days away, the game has landed in the crosshairs of fan debate after the official launch trailer gave a quick look at villain **Lady Deathstrike**, wielding a striking purple katana. This brief moment led to confusion across social media, with some viewers mistakenly identifying her as Psylocke—a classic X-Men character known for her iconic psychic blade.

## Insomniac Clarifies Character Identity
Shortly after the trailer’s debut, **Insomniac Games** posted a clarifying video clip to ensure fans understood that the character shown was indeed Lady Deathstrike. While the design departs somewhat from her classic comic book appearance—introducing new elements like the purple energy sword—core features such as her signature shoulder pads and deadly finger claws are still present. This update, however, didn’t end the discourse; a vocal segment of the audience criticized the look as generic, with some suggesting Insomniac had to explicitly spell out her identity due to these design changes. The reason behind Lady Deathstrike’s new weapon remains unknown, leaving players to speculate on Insomniac’s unique spin for this adaptation.

## Fan Reaction and Studio Response
The discussion around Lady Deathstrike seems to be part of a broader wave of scrutiny aimed at Insomniac, reminiscent of debates that followed other major releases like The Last of Us 2. While some fans are open to fresh takes on established characters, others remain skeptical—particularly regarding character art and design direction. Some critics believe the new approach is intended to align with the real-life actor portraying Lady Deathstrike. Despite the criticism, these kinds of fan reactions are becoming increasingly common for high-profile PlayStation exclusives. Insomniac has acknowledged the relentless nature of online discourse and hinted at potentially stepping back from social media engagement in the future.
