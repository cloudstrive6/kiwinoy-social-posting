Nominations for the **2026 Steam Awards** are now open. To mark the event's **10th anniversary**, Valve has brought back some categories from years past and added a new **Hall of Fame** category. Players have until **6pm UK (10am Pacific) on 30th November** to nominate their favourite games of the year across **14 categories**.

## What's new for the 10th anniversary

Returning throwback categories include **Just 5 More Minutes**, **Defies Description**, and **This is My Job Now**, which recognises games that simulate mundane real-life jobs.

The new **Hall of Fame** category will appear when voting opens. It will let players choose their favourite from Steam's previous Game of the Year winners, crowning the **best game of the past decade**.

Most categories are only open to games released on Steam since last year's Autumn Sale. The exception is the **Labo(u)r of Love** award, which goes to older games that developers are still supporting with new content. Previous Labo(u)r of Love winners, including **Warframe**, **Grand Theft Auto 5**, **Cyberpunk 2077**, and **Baldur's Gate 3**, can't be nominated again.

## What happens next

**Five finalists** will be revealed in each category when voting opens alongside the **Steam Winter Sale on 17th December**. Voting closes at **10am Pacific on 2nd January**, and the winners will be announced straight afterwards.

Nominating in at least one category also earns you the first level of Steam's **Nomination Committee badge**.

In other Steam news, Valve released a new update earlier this month that adds **Steam Deck framerate data** to a game's store page, giving potential buyers an indication of how well a game runs on the handheld.
