Marvel is set to celebrate the impending arrival of Avengers: Doomsday, the highly anticipated first part of the Marvel Cinematic Universe’s two-part adaptation of Jonathan Hickman's Secret Wars, with a series of special tie-in comics. One of the biggest highlights is the upcoming "Incursions" series—one-shot issues designed to showcase epic clashes between legendary alternate realities. Among them: a long-awaited crossover battle between the colorful world of X-Men '92 and the fan-favorite cast of X-Men: Evolution.

## Animated Eras Collide

The new "X-Men '92: Incursions" one-shot will see the beloved '90s throwback team—made famous through X-Men: The Animated Series and its comic spinoff—directly face off against the teenage-driven lineup from X-Men: Evolution. While X-Men '92 has remained a nostalgic staple, inspiring projects like X-Men '97, Evolution carved its own legacy by reimagining mutants as teens navigating both public high school and their mutant identities, introducing enduring characters such as Laura Kinney/X-23. This crossover isn’t just a fan dream: it marks the first official meeting of these two iconic animated teams, penned by Evolution creators Craig Kyle and Christopher Yost.

## Why This Crossover Matters

Marvel’s recent multiverse stories, particularly those involving mutants in the MCU, have leaned into nostalgia for classic X-Men adaptations. Avengers: Doomsday and its associated comics are set to follow suit, with promotional art promising plenty of familiar costumes and musical nods to fans’ favorite eras. The Incursions series—while a smaller suite of extras compared to the comic deluge seen during Secret Wars—continues Marvel’s strategy of revisiting past hits while paving new ground. This special clash between X-Men '92 and Evolution serves not only as a tribute to the animated series that defined generations, but could also hint at the direction Marvel may take with mutants in upcoming films.
