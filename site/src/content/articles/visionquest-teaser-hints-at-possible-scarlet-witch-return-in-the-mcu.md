Marvel fans have been buzzing about the potential return of **Wanda Maximoff/Scarlet Witch**, and the hype has reached a new level thanks to a teaser for **VisionQuest**, arriving on Disney+ on **October 14**. While many had been holding out for her comeback in the upcoming **Avengers: Doomsday**, new footage suggests viewers might catch a glimpse of Wanda's story sooner than expected—possibly even extending beyond simple flashbacks.

## Clues in the VisionQuest Teaser
The latest trailers for VisionQuest have already confirmed that **Wanda will appear in flashbacks** or in sequences where Vision revisits memories of his past, possibly while watching old footage. However, things take a mysterious turn as the new teaser introduces an "error" message just before flashing to scenes featuring Wanda. This glitch seems to specifically target memories related to her, raising questions about why these files would be corrupted and whether it’s a sign of something deeper lurking beneath the surface.

The emotional weight of the teaser is heightened by Vision declaring that he, Wanda, and their twins, Billy and Tommy, were never a real family—a sentiment colored by the fact that this is a very different Vision: an amalgamation of the original, the Hex Vision, and White Vision. VisionQuest looks set to explore his struggle to untangle his own identity and connections, especially those with Wanda. The unique error message, marked out in red, separates Wanda's memories from the rest, hinting that her fate may be more complicated than audiences were led to believe after her apparent self-sacrifice in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness.

## What This Could Mean for Wanda's Fate
Fans have long pointed out that **Wanda's death in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness left room for her to return**, especially since no body was shown and she's a fan-favorite character. In Marvel Comics, Wanda and Doctor Doom have a significant storyline together, and rumors indicate that Avengers: Doomsday could pick up threads from there. Some viewers now believe that the corrupted memories in VisionQuest could be a clue to Wanda’s true fate within the MCU, suggesting she may not be gone after all.
