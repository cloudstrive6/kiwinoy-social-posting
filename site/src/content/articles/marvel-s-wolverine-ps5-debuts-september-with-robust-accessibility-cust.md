**Marvel’s Wolverine** is just weeks away from slashing onto PS5, bringing a fresh superhero action adventure from Insomniac Games. The game is set for release on **15th September**, and the latest trailer showcases a host of features designed to elevate both gameplay and accessibility. From deep combat upgrades to optional side activities and a robust suite of player customization tools, Wolverine is poised to be one of PlayStation’s most feature-rich exclusives this year.

## Deep Customization and Side Activities
Players can look forward to **unlocking techniques and special skills** that enhance Logan’s signature brawling style. The new system of "Adaptations" allows upgrades directly to Wolverine himself—improving aspects like **Rage build-up**, parrying ability, and even the number of bullets he can weather in combat. Side activities, such as **Nightmare Door challenges**, collectible whisky bottles, and crafting suit materials, add to the replayability, while new suits also provide a max health bonus, incentivizing exploration and collection. Fans of completion will find plenty to chase with these varied objectives.

## Accessibility and Quality of Life
Insomniac continues its strong emphasis on accessibility, packing the game with a wide array of **options to support players of all abilities**. Filters can toggle off language, blood, and dismemberment, while gameplay can be tuned with manageable inputs and adjustable difficulty settings. As with past Insomniac releases, these features ensure that Marvel’s Wolverine can be enjoyed by the widest possible audience. The game will also offer **Photo Mode** from day one (via a launch patch), **New Game+**, and the ability to replay missions whenever you like—making it easier to dive back in for more.
