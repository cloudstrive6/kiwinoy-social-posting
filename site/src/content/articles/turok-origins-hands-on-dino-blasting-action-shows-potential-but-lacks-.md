**Turok: Origins** is set to bring prehistoric chaos to PS5 in **Spring 2027**, letting players team up with friends to take on sentient dinosaurs in a sprawling campaign. We spent some hands-on time with the game at Gamescom, and while the premise channels that Saturday morning cartoon energy, our early demo left us mixed on whether the gameplay can live up to its dino-sized promise.

## Classic Turok Vibes With Modern Co-Op

Whether you play in **third-person or first-person**, Turok: Origins invites you and up to two friends to battle waves of intelligent dinos. Each mission starts with selecting a playstyle—**assault, defense, or healer**—which determines your loadout of weapons, from assault rifles and miniguns to shotguns and plasma rifles. Unique abilities, such as healing zones and time-slowing bubbles, recharge over time, urging players to mix up their approach. The ability to swap between perspectives on the fly felt smooth, with solid controls in either mode.

Despite the variety on offer, our early combat encounters felt a bit too predictable. Most dino skirmishes ended quickly, lacking the tension or surprise you’d hope for in a co-op shooter. Even the much-hyped boss fight—a hulking, armored T-Rex unleashing rockets and shockwaves—looked impressive thanks to sharp visuals, but the battle played out in familiar cycles of shooting, swapping weapons, and waiting for cooldowns. Limited ammo management added some stakes, but the fight could have used mechanics that pushed weapon-switching or co-op abilities to the forefront.

## Hoping for More From the Full Release

The good news: you can play Turok: Origins solo or in co-op, online or offline. However, the demo didn’t showcase any major reasons to coordinate or communicate with your squad, with little need for teamwork or strategy beyond shooting what’s in front of you. While the promise of mindless, turn-your-brain-off fun is there, fans hoping for deep tactics or inventive enemy encounters might want to temper expectations until the full game’s release.
