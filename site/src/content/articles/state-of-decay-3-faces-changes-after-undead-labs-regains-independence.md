Undead Labs has spoken out about the future of **State of Decay 3** after buying back its independence from Xbox last month. The studio's head, Phillip Holt, confirmed that while the separation has prompted some painful layoffs, the team is still on track to deliver the anticipated co-op zombie survival game—and that its decision-making now responds only to its audience, not a corporate parent.

## Studio Restructuring and Scope
While **State of Decay 3** is "pretty close to finishing," Holt revealed that the recent layoffs will mean "we've got to make some changes." Despite losing a significant number of staff, Holt emphasized that the game's overall scope remains intact and that alpha players and Gamescom attendees have already seen what the new installment brings to the table. The game now carries a general release date of **"2027"**, giving the studio some flexibility as it enters its home stretch. Holt also addressed the community’s concerns about the game's lengthy development, clarifying that meaningful progress didn't ramp up until **2022 or 2023**, even though the project was first teased back in 2020.

## Independence and Clearer Focus
Holt traced the early announcement of State of Decay 3 to the uncertainty that followed Microsoft's acquisition spree in 2018, aiming to secure the game's public identity before any more corporate shifts. After being earmarked for divestiture during Microsoft's recent restructuring, Undead Labs' management succeeded in reacquiring the studio, following the model seen at other game developers. Holt declined to specify how many roles were affected by the layoffs, but made it clear that the decision was a heartbreaking one for everyone involved. Freed from the priorities of a larger company, Undead Labs is now focused on a single directive: making the best game possible for its fans.

## What’s Next for State of Decay 3?
Despite changes, **State of Decay 3 will still launch day one on Game Pass**, a move Holt credited with helping the series reach a reported 25 million lifetime players. Thousands remain active in the community every day, a testament to the franchise’s ongoing appeal. Early hands-on reports suggest the new game is shaping up well, and more details are expected as development advances.
