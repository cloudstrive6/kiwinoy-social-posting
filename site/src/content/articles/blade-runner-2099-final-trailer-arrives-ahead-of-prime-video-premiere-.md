Blade Runner 2099 has revealed its big final trailer, just weeks before the series premieres on Prime Video. All **eight episodes** will be available on **November 25, 2026**, bringing the sci-fi franchise to television for the first time.

The trailer teases the next chapter of the Blade Runner universe and features **Michelle Yeoh** (Everything Everywhere All at Once) and **Hunter Schafer** (Euphoria) in the lead roles. Yeoh plays **Olwen**, a Replicant days from dying. She recruits **Cora** (Schafer), a human fugitive making a final bid to stop running. Cora must take on the role of a Blade Runner in order to hunt down a runaway.

## A very different Blade Runner world

The series is set **fifty years** after the last film, and two things immediately set it apart from what came before. First, by this point in the franchise timeline the Replicants have become the main population of the Earth, while humans are now the minority. Second, the show is set against a sunny landscape rather than the rainy world Ridley Scott established in the original.

The wait between projects is also shorter this time. Ridley Scott's 1982 Blade Runner, which wasn't a hit on release but changed the sci-fi genre, was followed 35 years later by Denis Villeneuve's Blade Runner 2049 in 2017. The new series arrives less than a decade after 2049.

## Cast and creative team

Alongside Yeoh and Schafer, the series stars **Dimitri Abold** (The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes) and **Lewis Gribben** (Somewhere Boy). Recurring guest stars include Katelyn Rose Downey (The Nun II), Daniel Rigby (Renegade Nell), Johnny Harris (A Gentleman in Moscow), Amy Lennox (Only Child), Sheila Atim (The Woman King), Matthew Needham (House of the Dragon), Tom Burke (Furiosa) and Maurizio Lombardi (Ripley).

**Silka Luisa** serves as showrunner, creator and executive producer.
