If you've somehow missed out on one of the most celebrated RPGs of all time, now is the perfect time to join Geralt of Rivia's adventure. **The Witcher 3: Complete Edition is currently 50% off on the PlayStation Store**, dropping the price to **$24.99/£17.49** for a limited time. Even better, PlayStation owners who purchase this edition will **automatically receive a free upgrade to the remastered version** launching on **September 29**.

## All Expansions and a Major Remaster for One Price
The Complete Edition includes every story expansion—from **Hearts of Stone** to **Blood and Wine**—along with all previously released DLC. But the big news is the imminent next-gen overhaul tailor-made for the **PS5 Pro**. This remaster brings **ray tracing at a 60fps target**, leverages **PSSR technology** for clear image quality, and features a robust slate of improvements spanning everything from visuals to gameplay mechanics. Existing PS5 players who already own the Complete Edition will automatically receive these upgrades at no extra charge, offering immense value and new reasons to revisit the Continent.

## Limited-Time Deal and Upgrade Path Details
**The 50% sale runs through October 8**, so there’s time to grab the game at its best price in years before the remaster drops. For those coming from PS4, a free upgrade to the PS5 version has been available for some time, but this new remaster is built specifically for PS5 hardware. Note that **digital ownership may be required** for the auto-upgrade, and all major DLC is now available at no additional cost with the Complete Edition.

If you've never experienced The Witcher 3, this is a rare chance to own the definitive, next-gen ready version at a fraction of the usual cost.
