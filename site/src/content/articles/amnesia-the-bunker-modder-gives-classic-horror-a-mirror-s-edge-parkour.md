**Amnesia: The Bunker**, already infamous for its suffocating horror and relentless monster, just got a genre-bending mod that throws expectations out the window. Modder **TiMan** has reimagined the game with all the wall-running, mantling, and bright cityscapes of **EA’s Mirror’s Edge**, swapping out the series’ signature dread for a surprisingly fresh (and disorienting) take on survival-horror action—all as part of Frictional Games’ 2026 modding jam themed ‘What is reality?’

## A Shocking Change of Pace

In the aptly named **Bunker's Edge**, players are whisked away from the claustrophobic, crumbling French bunker and dropped—via portal—into a vibrant city skyline straight out of Mirror's Edge. What’s particularly impressive is seeing the traditionally shadowy HPL engine render such a sunny, colorful environment. But the transformation isn't merely cosmetic. TiMan faithfully recreates the parkour toolkit: mantling, sliding, wall-running—even the tricky 180-degree wall-jump—come along for the ride, giving the protagonist Henri a whole new way to evade danger.

## Still a Horror Game at Heart

Despite the breezier setting, **Bunker's Edge** stays true to its roots: the original Bunker's monster follows Henri through the portal, chasing him just as aggressively across the city rooftops as in the original’s dark corridors. The mod, while short (about 15 minutes), serves up a weird and compelling twist ending you won't find in either source material. That said, don’t expect flawless parkour; some mechanics feel clunky, and players might bump into geometry bugs, but it's an impressive showcase of what’s possible with dedicated modding.

Frictional Games' ongoing support for modding—along with their next horror project Ontos in development—signals a bright future for fans of inventive twists on classic game formulas.
