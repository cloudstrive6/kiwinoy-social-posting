**A fresh wave of rumors suggests Xbox Game Pass could see another big revamp, with the possible removal of some of its most sought-after features—including access to new games on launch day and the cloud streaming service.** According to an unconfirmed leak posted by ResetEra user Slayven, who previously revealed accurate Game Pass changes, Microsoft is reportedly planning to rework its Game Pass subscription tiers, potentially impacting how players access upcoming games and multiplayer experiences.

## What's Reportedly Changing

Slayven's latest leak claims **Xbox could drop the day-one game releases from the main Game Pass offering**, and may also eliminate **cloud streaming entirely** from the service. While prices might drop slightly, these changes would mean that subscribers would lose access to several of the benefits that helped establish Game Pass as a standout gaming subscription. The details suggest that **a new ad-supported tier** could be introduced too, further shaking up how gamers experience the platform. The rumored changes are said to be targeted for implementation **between February and April 2027**, but with no official confirmation from Microsoft, these details remain speculative.

## Official Response and Community Buzz

When reached for comment, **Microsoft stated it has no changes to announce or plans that are finalized**. The company acknowledged that it regularly tests different Game Pass offerings to find out what works best for players, but assured that there are no changes affecting previously announced day-one releases for Game Pass Ultimate. Uncertainty remains regarding whether **online multiplayer will be part of the new tiers** and what impact these adjustments might have on PC Game Pass—a service previously rumored to be going away, which has yet to happen. Noted leaker NateTheHate has also vouched for the legitimacy of Slayven's sources, but noted that the timeline could still shift.

**Earlier this year, Microsoft adjusted Game Pass pricing and restricted some content** (notably removing Call of Duty as a day-one release), further fueling speculation about continued changes. As Xbox continues to evolve its services, all eyes are on what these rumored transformations could mean for the Game Pass community and the shape of Xbox gaming in the years ahead.
