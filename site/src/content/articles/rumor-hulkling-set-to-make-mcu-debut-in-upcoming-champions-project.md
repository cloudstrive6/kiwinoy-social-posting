The Marvel Cinematic Universe may be expanding its roster of young heroes even further, as a new rumor suggests **Hulkling is set to debut in the MCU**, potentially through the long-rumored **Champions** project. This development is generating buzz, especially as more members from Marvel's younger teams continue to appear on screen.

## Champions Expands The MCU's Young Lineup
In recent years, the MCU has introduced a slew of young superheroes who have ties to either the **Young Avengers** or **Champions** in the comics. On the Young Avengers front, we've already seen **America Chavez, Kate Bishop (Hawkeye), Cassie Lang (Stature), Billy Kaplan (Wiccan), Eli Bradley (Patriot)**, and most recently **Thomas Shepherd (Speed)**, who appeared in VisionQuest. Meanwhile, Champions representation includes **Kamala Khan (Ms. Marvel), Joaquin Torres (Falcon),** and **Riri Williams (Ironheart)**, with rumors swirling around an upcoming introduction for **Sam Alexander (Nova)**.

Now, the latest rumor claims that Hulkling will appear in Champions, a title that was initially planned as a Disney+ series but could become a movie. If true, this could mark a significant moment for fans eager to see more comic-accurate team-ups and relationships.

## The MCU's First Big Young Romance?
Hulkling's arrival may also pave the way for one of Marvel Comics' most beloved—and sometimes controversial—relationships. In the comics, Hulkling (a half-Kree, half-Skrull shapeshifter) is best known for his romance with **Wiccan**. The MCU has already introduced Wiccan as Billy Maximoff in WandaVision and later as Billy Kaplan in Agatha All Along, with his brother Tommy (Speed) joining in VisionQuest. While the Young Avengers in the comics came together following the Avengers' disbandment, Hulkling and Wiccan quickly became a central duo and were even married before a major cosmic event.

If Champions brings both characters into focus, their legendary relationship could become a major highlight for the franchise. For now, details about Champions remain unconfirmed, and the project is still reportedly in development for a future MCU phase following Avengers: Secret Wars.
