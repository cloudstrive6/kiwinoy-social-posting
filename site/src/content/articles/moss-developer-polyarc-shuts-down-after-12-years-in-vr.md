**Polyarc, the Seattle-based studio known for the beloved VR series Moss, has officially closed down after nearly 12 years of game development.** The announcement was made through a farewell post on the studio’s official LinkedIn account, signaling the end of an era for a team that brought immersive storybook adventures to virtual reality and beyond. The message expressed gratitude to both the team and fans, calling the studio’s journey a "joyous rollercoaster" and reflecting pride in their work. 

## The Fallout and Polyarc’s Legacy

While never a large company—**Polyarc’s headcount reportedly ranged from 11 to 50 employees**—the impact of its Moss franchise was sizable within VR circles. The studio had already experienced layoffs back in March, after struggling to secure funding for a project that was ultimately canceled. **Moss: The Forgotten Relic**, its final release, arrived in July and notably brought the core Moss games to traditional (flatscreen) platforms for the first time, expanding the studio’s reach beyond VR.

To support affected staff, Polyarc shared a reverse recruiting spreadsheet, encouraging other game studios to consider hiring its former team members. Polyarc’s back catalog, including both Moss titles and The Forgotten Relic, is still available for players on major platforms.

## Another Blow to the VR Gaming Space

Polyarc’s closure is part of a broader contraction in VR development during 2026. Major industry player Meta has made substantial cuts within its Reality Labs division this year, laying off staff, shutting down three studios, and pulling its commercial Quest headsets from the market as it shifts focus to AI and smart glasses. The VR market has also seen staff reductions and studio closures elsewhere, including cuts at nDreams.

Despite announcing ambitious plans in recent years, including an unfulfilled vision for a VR competitive multiplayer title, Polyarc’s chapter in the industry has come to a close. Their acclaimed Moss series, however, remains a touchstone for what narrative-driven VR experiences can achieve.
