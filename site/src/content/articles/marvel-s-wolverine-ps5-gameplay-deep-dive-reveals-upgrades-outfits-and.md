Sony’s next PS5 exclusive, **Marvel’s Wolverine**, is almost here, and Insomniac has dropped a feature-packed trailer offering a fresh look at what’s in store. Launching on **September 15**, the action adventure will put players in control of the adamantium-clawed hero, with plenty of systems and surprises waiting beneath the surface.

## Deep Customization & Collectibles
Wolverine’s gameplay is all about brawling and progression. Players can master a variety of techniques and special skills to boost Logan’s combat prowess, with unlockable moves letting you tailor your fighting style. Adaptations serve as upgrades that tweak core abilities like Rage build-up, parrying, and even how much damage Wolverine can shrug off — familiar territory for action veterans, but delivered with Insomniac’s polish.

The world is filled with optional side activities and collectibles, including Nightmare Door challenges, scattered whisky bottles, and materials to craft new outfits. Each new suit isn’t just cosmetic: unlocking outfits gives max health boosts, adding a strategic layer to your fashion choices. As confirmed, fans will have access to a **New Game+ mode at launch**, along with a Photo mode available via a day-one patch. Want to replay your favorite missions? You can, at your own pace.

## Next-Level Accessibility Options
Wolverine takes a big leap on the accessibility front, bringing back and expanding features from previous Insomniac titles. Players will find a wide range of options for those hard of sight or hearing, plus the ability to simplify controls or adjust difficulty in a slew of ways. The full menu is extensive, even allowing you to dial back (or disable) blood, gore, and strong language with a filter system, letting you shape the tone of your experience as you see fit. These tools aim to welcome even more fans into the fold, whatever their needs or playstyle preferences may be.
