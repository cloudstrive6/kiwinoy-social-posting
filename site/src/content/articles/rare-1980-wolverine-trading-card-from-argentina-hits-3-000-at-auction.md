Every now and then, the world of trading cards steps beyond sports and into the realm of comic book legends—and this time, Wolverine is taking the spotlight. A rare **1980 Terrabusi Marvel Coleccion Superheroes #86 Wolverine card**, released exclusively in Argentina, has recently surfaced at Heritage Auctions and is already being bid up to **$3,000** (including buyer’s premium), with plenty of time still left in the auction.

## The Origins of Wolverine’s Rookie Card
What makes this card so special isn’t just its **PSA NM 7 grade** or its age—it's the unique place it holds in Marvel collectibles history. Released as part of a promotional campaign with Argentina’s beloved **Tita chocolate-covered sweet** by Terrabusi, this card beat Wolverine’s American trading card debut by several years. For U.S. collectors, this card is especially elusive, as it was never officially released stateside, making any copy in North America a true rarity. The card features Wolverine in his unmistakable yellow, black, and blue suit, reminiscent of the classic '90s X-Men animated look, striking a pose with his hands behind his back against what appears to be the backdrop of the X-Men's Danger Room. The reverse side even offers a throwback to the original Tita packaging, which hasn’t changed much since 1980.

## Why It’s the Ultimate Marvel Trading Card Find
Few comic book collectible cards reach such high valuations, but this one checks every box for rarity and desirability. It’s recognized as Wolverine’s "rookie card" by collectors—a status bolstered by its position at the top of PSA’s official Wolverine Master Set checklist. Its combination of a sought-after character, a vintage release date, a high grade, and a unique non-U.S. origin has the card on track to exceed its current five-figure bid as the auction nears its close. For fans and collectors, its timing couldn’t be better, with anticipation running high for upcoming Marvel video game releases featuring the iconic X-Man.
