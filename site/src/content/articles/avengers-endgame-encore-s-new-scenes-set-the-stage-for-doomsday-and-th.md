**Avengers: Endgame Encore** may only be about four minutes longer than the original cut, but the rerelease’s new scenes serve a bigger purpose—kicking off the destruction-heavy stakes set to define **December’s Avengers: Doomsday**. The standout new mid-credits scene goes all-in on multiversal chaos, tying together characters and lore from both the Loki Disney+ series and Deadpool & Wolverine while channeling the dread that fans of the comics will recognize from the Secret Wars storylines.

## The TVA Faces a Multiversal Collapse

The new scene reunites fans with TVA agents **Mobius M. Mobius (Owen Wilson)**, **Ouroboros “O.B.” (Ke Huy Quan)**, and **Casey (Eugene Cordero)** as they return to TVA headquarters. With the TVA now focused on preserving, rather than pruning, the multitude of timelines—a consequence of Loki’s finale—the stakes are instantly raised. Panic erupts when the TVA team detects numerous incursions, spelling disaster as multiple universes begin to collide and self-destruct with alarming speed. O.B. breaks the news: “The multiverse is collapsing.” On one monitor, viewers can spot Earths 10005 and 1127 at war, with advanced weapons being fired as both universes fight to survive in the wake of their impending annihilation.

## What This Means for Doomsday and Secret Wars Fans

While the original comics’ “Secret Wars” and “Time Runs Out” arcs built toward multiversal destruction through years of layered storytelling, the Encore’s mid-credits moment fast-forwards the devastation. Fans will note that **Earth-10005**—home to the X-Men as seen in the Deadpool trilogy and Logan—takes a central role, as its onscreen conflict echoes Marvel’s comic tradition of ultimate-universe showdowns. This aggressive escalation hints that the X-Men’s world will be pivotal in **Avengers: Doomsday**, likely serving a role similar to that of the Ultimate Universe’s clash with Earth-616. With so many universes obliterated in rapid succession, and familiar heroes scrambling to hold the multiverse together, Encore’s tease is clear: the stakes have never been higher for the MCU.
