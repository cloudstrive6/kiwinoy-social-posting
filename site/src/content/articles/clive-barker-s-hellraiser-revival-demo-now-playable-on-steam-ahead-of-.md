**Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival**, the highly anticipated horror game, has released a brand new playable demo on Steam just weeks ahead of its full launch on **October 8, 2026**. Alongside the demo, fans can check out the latest trailer, titled **Scarlet Church**, offering a grisly glimpse into the game’s dark themes and the cult at its center. Viewer discretion is definitely advised—the trailer, even in its censored form, is loaded with gore, violence, and mature content true to the Hellraiser legacy.

## Dive Into the Labyrinth

In the demo, players are tossed straight into the terrifying world of Clive Barker's Hell, where they’ll navigate a shifting Labyrinth and tangle with the cult known as the Scarlet Church. The group has claimed the **Genesis Configuration**, the mysterious puzzle box that’s central to the story. The demo introduces core gameplay elements, including combat, crafting, main characters, and puzzle-solving mechanics, offering a solid taste of what’s to come—even though progress won’t carry over to the final release.

## Pre-Orders, Editions, and Platforms

**Hellraiser: Revival** is now up for pre-order at £35 for the base edition and £45 for the Deluxe Edition. Early adopters will score exclusive skins, including a **Labyrinth Pistol Skin** and a **Martyr Sawblade Skin**, usable at launch. For collectors, two premium editions are available for pre-order, each packed with official licensed collectibles and all Deluxe Edition in-game bonuses. The game is set to unleash its horrors across **Nintendo Switch 2, PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X/S**.
