Thor has long been one of the MCU's most malleable heroes, adapting to drastically different tones as various directors have taken him in new directions. Now, with Avengers: Doomsday on the horizon, fans can expect a major course correction—Thor will be returning to the more serious, determined version last seen in Infinity War, according to the Russo brothers.

## A Hero Reforged in 'Doomsday'

From Kenneth Branagh's dramatic, Shakespeare-inspired **Thor** to Taika Waititi's wildly comedic spins in **Thor: Ragnarok** and **Love and Thunder**, the God of Thunder's on-screen persona has swung wildly. Even in **Avengers: Endgame**, Thor underwent yet another transformation, visibly weighed down by loss—a direction that drew mixed reactions from fans. While some appreciated the portrayal of grief, many were unhappy with seeing Thor depicted as worn down and defeated, especially when compared to his triumphant, battle-ready presence in previous films.

The good news for longtime fans: the Russo brothers recently confirmed that their version of Thor in Doomsday will align closely with the character’s Infinity War portrayal. Joe Russo described Thor as a role that is “fascinating” in its range and adaptability, but stated that their vision is to move forward with "the Infinity War Thor"—the same powerhouse who famously charged into battle, wielding Stormbreaker and demanding, "Bring me Thanos."

## Why This Change Matters for the MCU

The trailers for **Avengers: Doomsday** have hinted at darker stakes, and it appears Thor will be reclaiming a leadership role that calls for gravitas and conviction. This shift, bringing back a more serious and focused Thor, resonates with fans tired of the recent comedic approach and aligns with the epic scale of the upcoming film. However, this change also highlights the ongoing challenge of consistency in Thor's characterization—a trait that has made his journey unpredictable, for better or worse.

Still, for many, this return to form is welcome news. With Avengers: Doomsday promising high stakes and fierce battles, the MCU’s embrace of "Infinity War Thor" could set the stage for one of his most memorable outings yet.
