Arc System Works has officially revealed the final three DLC characters set to join the roster for **Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls’ Year 1** lineup, wrapping up the game’s ambitious first wave of post-launch content. **Jubilee, Jane Foster, and Venom** will be joining the fight, with each character debuting alongside new Peach Momoko artwork that captures their unique Marvel flair. Rounding out the previously announced Phoenix Cyclops, these picks add new dimensions to the game’s ever-expanding cast.

## Fresh Faces and Fan Favorites
Jubilee is making a highly anticipated debut for X-Men fans, marking a rare instance of the character taking center stage in a fighting game lineup. Jane Foster offers a fresh spin on the Thor archetype, stepping away from the traditional version’s move set for a new take on Asgardian brawls. Venom rounds out the trio, bringing his iconic symbiote powers to the mix—ideal for players who enjoy aggressive, transformation-heavy playstyles. Notably, while some fans have questioned Venom’s inclusion alongside Carnage, his enduring popularity ensures plenty of excitement for his arrival.

## New Stage and Ongoing Speculation
Accompanying Venom’s launch, a new **Klyntar stage**—the fabled planet of the symbiotes—is set to release in **Spring 2027**. The timing syncs perfectly with Venom’s debut in the game and is sure to provide dramatic backdrops and lore-rich visuals for symbiote showdowns. Additionally, the announcement has sparked speculation among the community regarding potential connections to Insomniac’s unconfirmed Marvel’s Venom project, rumored to be tied to future PlayStation hardware releases, though nothing official has been announced.
