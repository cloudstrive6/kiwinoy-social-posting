**Monster Hunter Wilds** fans can look forward to their first major expansion, **Ascendance, set for 2027**, but Capcom isn’t waiting for the Tokyo Game Show to start dropping new details. This week, the company launched a series of daily videos showcasing the game’s fourteen weapon types, spotlighting a powerful new mechanic that’s set to shake up the hunt: Boosted Actions.

## Boosted Actions: The Next Evolution in Combat

The heart of **Ascendance** lies in the introduction of a new resource and state for each weapon — the Boost Bracer system. By aggressively attacking, players build up Boost Bracer energy, activating the new **Boosted Actions** via special attacks tailored to each weapon. Once triggered, Boost unlocks new combos, amplified moves, and unique abilities, changing up playstyles across the arsenal. The rollout started with the Great Sword, which now boasts new aerial moves and versatility, and teases have hinted at everything from a hammer-based perfect guard to 'counter' actions for the Charge Blade.

Three main ways to trigger the boosted state have been shown: slow but high-damage Boost moves, quick stance shifts, or seamless combo transitions. This promises not just flashier moves but a more aggressive, dynamic combat rhythm — something many players have been asking for as monster encounters ramp up in difficulty.

## What’s Next for Monster Hunters

This daily weapon showcase will continue until around the start of Tokyo Game Show, where Capcom is expected to reveal more about **Ascendance**. Until then, fans can look forward to breakdowns for all fourteen weapon types — with each video packing new mechanics and gameplay hints. **Monster Hunter Wilds** is currently available on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam, with a Switch 2 version in the works. With Capcom pushing for a faster-paced, offense-heavy style, longtime hunters may need to rethink their favorite loadouts and tactics.
