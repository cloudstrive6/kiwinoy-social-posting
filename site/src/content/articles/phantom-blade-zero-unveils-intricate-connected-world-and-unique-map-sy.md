S-Game has finally lifted the veil on Phantom Blade Zero’s game world, offering fans a first proper look at its atmosphere and design following a reveal during PlayStation’s State of Play. For the first time, players have been shown snippets of eight individual maps that will “seamlessly connect into a single world,” offering a rich variety of detailed environments.

During past demos, S-Game emphasized that even the apparently major areas and bosses experienced were sidetracks from the core storyline, hinting at the game’s expansive nature. The new showcase spotlights several distinct locations, including bandit-infested valleys, an eerie abandoned warehouse with puppets and rituals, a steam-powered tower, and a wide lake navigable via an unusual vessel. Although the footage previewed only brief flashes of each zone, the narration made clear that each area will contribute to a varied and surreal overall journey.

One standout feature is Phantom Blade Zero’s use of eight hand-painted Chinese ink wash maps to guide player exploration. Unlike traditional open world titles that reveal every last secret or soulslike games that forego maps almost entirely, these maps provide only a general sense of direction, encouraging players to uncover hidden paths and secrets through exploration. Fast travel will be possible between discovered locations via bells placed throughout the world.

S-Game specifies that while Phantom Blade Zero does not fit into the standard definitions of either open world or strictly linear action games, the experience is designed to blend structure with a sense of mystery. The short glimpses of gameplay demonstrate the ability to use weapons to break through obstacles, hinting at a light metroidvania style of progress where new abilities or items may unlock previously inaccessible areas. As more details emerge, fans remain eager to see how Phantom Blade Zero’s distinct approach will stack up against the giants of the action RPG genre.
