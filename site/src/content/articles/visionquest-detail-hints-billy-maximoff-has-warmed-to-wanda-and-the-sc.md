A small costume detail in **VisionQuest** may say a lot about where **Billy Maximoff** stands with his mother. Eagle-eyed fans have spotted that Billy's jacket features **the Scarlet Witch's crown**, and because Billy drew the symbol onto the jacket himself, it suggests he has come around on **Wanda Maximoff**.

## A detail that is easy to miss

Most of the attention in Billy's **VisionQuest** debut went to the reunion itself: how emotional it was to see **Tommy and Billy** back together, and the moment **Vision** pulls Billy in for a hug. That made the markings on his jacket very easy to overlook. The find has been credited to a post from costume designer **sarahyoung_costumedesign** on Instagram, which showed Wiccan adding the crown to his jacket.

The symbol already had a link to Billy. A crown appeared on his head in **Agatha All Along**, but the fact that he chose to put it on his own clothing is a more deliberate step. That matters because **Agatha All Along** established that Billy, who lacks most of his memories from the Hex beyond the existence of his brother, did not accept Wanda as his mom. He was raised by the **Kaplans** for about **three years** and sees them as his parents, and the show also hinted at some animosity toward Wanda. Many fans disliked that, and this detail suggests the complaint may be fading.

There are other signs of a thaw. Billy first calls Vision "sir" before being pulled into a hug, and **VisionQuest** ends with Tommy and Vision heading to dinner with Billy, presumably with Agatha as well, in **New Jersey**.

## Why a reunion is still far off

The series also confirmed in a post-credits scene that **Wanda is alive**. Wanda has not yet reunited with her sons or Vision, and it is unclear when or if that will happen, though it currently seems more likely than not. The complication is that **Doctor Doom** has come to collect her, saying he will take the Scarlet Witch's power for himself. That suggests Wanda could end up a villain under Doom's control in **Avengers: Doomsday**.

So even if Billy is more open to Wanda than before, the Maximoff family is still far from a happy ending, if one is coming at all.
