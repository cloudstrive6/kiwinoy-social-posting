Marvel is pulling out all the stops for **Avengers: Doomsday**, dropping an exclusive trailer during the MLB World Series that’s packed with both superpowered surprises and a clever nod to baseball history. The spot shines a light on both real-life baseball legends—**Bryce Harper, Freddie Freeman, Jacob Misiorowski, Junior Camiero, and Randy Arozarena**—and the Fantastic Four, with alternate universe stakes front and center. In this fresh take, viewers meet the team’s Earth 828 counterparts, where fan favorites like **Reed Richards (Pedro Pascal), Ben Grimm (Ebon Moss-Bachrach), and Johnny Storm (Joseph Quinn)** take the field for a Dodgers team that famously never left Brooklyn.

## Fantastic Four and a Baseball What-If
While Earth 616 in the Marvel Cinematic Universe mirrors real-life history, with the **Brooklyn Dodgers relocating to Los Angeles before the 1958 season**, Earth 828 asks: what if the Dodgers stayed in New York? The teaser leans into this alternate reality, mixing Marvel’s superhero flair with a retro baseball aesthetic and raising eyebrows about the fate of other historic teams. Given the Dodgers’ 1950s success and the real-world pressure for a modern stadium, this change suggests New York found a way to keep the club—potentially altering decades of sports history with ripple effects across the city, from tourism to sports fandom. And if the Dodgers stayed, what became of the Giants—baseball and football alike? The MCU’s attention to these details could tease further multiversal twists down the road.

## MCU, Sports, and the Multiverse Ripple Effect
The choice to showcase this new sneak peek during a major sports event initially seemed surprising, but the marriage of baseball and the Fantastic Four is fitting—especially with well-known sports fan Ben Grimm in the mix. The trailer’s playful universe-bending could even pay off as an inside joke in Doomsday or an after-credits scene, especially if the team has to choose new allegiances (Yankees? Mets?). The ad hints that shifting the Dodgers’ fate impacts New York beyond the ballpark, and fans are left to wonder: what other changes lurk in Earth 828’s version of Marvel history?
