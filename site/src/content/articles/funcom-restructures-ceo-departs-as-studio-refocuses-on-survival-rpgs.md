Funcom, the studio best known for **Dune: Awakening and Conan Exiles**, is undergoing a significant **restructuring process** that will see layoffs and the departure of current CEO **Rui Casais** once the reorganization is finished. The company revealed it will pivot fully toward supporting its existing **survival RPGs** rather than pursuing new projects in the immediate future.

## The Impact on Funcom's Staff and Leadership
Funcom’s CEO acknowledged the challenges of moving from peak development of Dune: Awakening into a long-term support phase. As the studio transitions away from the resources and staffing required during initial production, the team stated it cannot sustain its previous size or structure. Casais emphasized the importance of treating laid-off employees with respect and transparency during this transition. The restructuring is being described as a necessary step as Funcom focuses on the ongoing development and operation of its signature titles.

## Shifts for Dune: Awakening and Conan Exiles
After a fast start upon release, Dune: Awakening experienced a notable **decline in player counts** and mixed community feedback around its PvP and endgame systems. This led to earlier rounds of layoffs as well as a major overhaul just last month, in which the game pivoted away from its MMO roots to highlight solo play and dedicated PvE modes—though PvP gameplay remains available. Meanwhile, Conan Exiles received substantial upgrades this year, including a new engine, a paid expansion, and even more character customization features. With both titles having completed major updates and reworks, Funcom’s leadership has indicated that the studio is **not ready to start a new game**, with future plans remaining nebulous for now.
