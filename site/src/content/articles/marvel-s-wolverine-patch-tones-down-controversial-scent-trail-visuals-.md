Marvel’s Wolverine might be racking up decent sales since its **September 15** launch, but the news cycle has been dominated by passionate debate—and frequent complaints—about one feature in particular: the game’s omnipresent blue gas scent trails, which guide players through its world. Now, developer **Insomniac Games** has responded with a new patch designed to make those trails less intrusive.

## The Hotfix: Scent Trails Get a Makeover
The **1.001.005 update**, released late on **September 18**, reduces the **opacity of both Scent Trails and Audio Sense indicators**, making them subtler during gameplay. While the blue gas effect is still in place, it appears less overpowering, as demonstrated by side-by-side gameplay comparisons already circulating online. This visual tweak comes after extensive online criticism, with some players calling the hand-holding integration distracting and difficult to ignore. It’s clear that Insomniac is listening—but for now, the blue trails remain a core element.

## What’s Next? More Customization Could Be Coming
Insomniac has acknowledged ongoing feedback and says it’s evaluating further adjustments to the Sense system, including the possibility of **toggleable options in a future update**. However, the team cautioned that such changes are complex, as these features are tied to other gameplay systems and require careful testing to avoid new bugs or game-breaking issues. No timeline for this update has been given yet, but the studio has thanked fans for their patience while they continue to fine-tune the experience.

The backlash against Marvel’s Wolverine’s guidance system taps into a larger trend: growing resistance among players toward overly directive mechanics in big-budget games. While other PlayStation hits like God of War Ragnarok and Horizon Forbidden West have also been critiqued for heavy-handed guidance, the blue scent trails in Wolverine have drawn particularly vocal criticism, partly because of how visually prominent they are.
