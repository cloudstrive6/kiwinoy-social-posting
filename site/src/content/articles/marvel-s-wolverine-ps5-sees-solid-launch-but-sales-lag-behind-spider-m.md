Insomniac’s hotly anticipated action game **Marvel’s Wolverine** saw a respectable launch on PS5 last month, moving **around 2.16 million copies in September**. That’s enough for the game to claim the number two spot on the month’s PS5 sales charts, trailing only behind the inevitable powerhouse—**EA Sports FC 27**. But while strong, these numbers are drawing extra scrutiny given the hype and the reported scale of the project.

## Not the Blockbuster PlayStation Hoped For?

At first glance, 2.16 million units in roughly two weeks would seem like a solid debut for most games. But industry watchers have noted that Wolverine’s launch appears **“soft” compared to PlayStation’s usual hits.** Projections from Alinea Analytics put it at approximately 1.9 million by mid-September, so the growth since then has been modest. Officially, PlayStation and Sony have remained silent about the numbers—adding to speculation that Wolverine hasn’t quite met expectations. That silence contrasts with industry peers: earlier this month, Bandai Namco openly celebrated Ace Combat 8 clearing a million sales faster than any previous entry.

## Costly Project, Room to Grow

Wolverine reportedly came with a **production price tag as high as $300 million**, fueled in part by a significant licensing fee to Disney. With a spend of that magnitude, Sony will be looking for more than just a respectable launch—long-term sales will be necessary for the game to turn a profit. The publisher traditionally holds back on sharing sales milestones outside of financial calls, so a clearer picture is likely to emerge in PlayStation’s next earnings report. 

While most AAA releases are frontloaded, Wolverine still has untapped potential: **holiday bundles, Black Friday deals, and price promotions** could help extend the game’s sales runway. As for the product itself, early reactions vary—some players praise its action and performances, even as critics note it lacks the breakout appeal of Insomniac’s Spider-Man games. Whether it can climb to blockbuster status, or settles as a solid performer, will be one of PlayStation’s key stories to watch as 2026 unfolds.
