Grand Theft Auto 6 didn't just sell in huge numbers on launch day. It dominated how people spent their time. New data from Circana's Player Engagement Tracker shows that on **19th November**, **61 percent** of US PlayStation 5 players and **56 percent** of global Xbox players who were active that day played GTA 6.

Analyst Mat Piscatella shared the figures on Bluesky. Players on PS5 averaged **513 minutes** in the game that day, or more than eight and a half hours. Xbox players averaged **494 minutes**, around eight and a quarter hours. Piscatella wrote that both the daily active user and minutes played engagement rates are "absolutely bonkers."

## How it compares

For context, **Fortnite** had the second-highest engagement rate that day, with **10 percent** of active players across both platforms playing it. That puts GTA 6 well ahead of the rest of the field.

Piscatella also listed the top five games that GTA 6 players had been playing in the two weeks before launch, from **6th to 19th November**: Fortnite, Call of Duty, Grand Theft Auto 5 (Remastered), Roblox and NBA 2K27.

## The big picture

The numbers follow Rockstar's confirmation that GTA 6 became the **biggest entertainment launch of all time**. It sold **32.5 million** pre-release copies and made **$3.79 billion** by the end of its first day. The game launched on Thursday, 19th November, for PS5 and Xbox Series X/S.

Critics have been kind. GTA 6 is one of the best-reviewed games of all time, though its Metacritic user score tells a different story. Players have review bombed it over its limited frame rate and a purported "woke" agenda.

There's also a cautionary tale for anyone sharing screenshots. One GTA 6 player was reportedly hit with a **30-day PlayStation Network ban** after sending a friend a photo taken in the game's nudist resort.
