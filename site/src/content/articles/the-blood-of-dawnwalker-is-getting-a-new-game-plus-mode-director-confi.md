**The Blood of Dawnwalker** is one of September’s biggest RPG releases, and fans hoping for a good reason to dive in again are in luck: the director has confirmed **a New Game Plus mode is on the way**. While the story already packs plenty to explore, this new feature aims to boost replayability for dedicated players eager to experience every corner of this dark fantasy adventure.

## What We Know About New Game Plus

Director **Konrad Tomaszkiewicz** announced the New Game Plus mode during a recent stream, but kept further details under wraps. For now, there’s no word on a release timeline or exactly which features the mode will include. Still, the confirmation alone has sparked a wave of anticipation. Typically, New Game Plus lets players carry existing gear, skills, or progress into a fresh playthrough—perfect for a game that’s already built around tough choices and time management.

## Why This Mode Makes Sense

The Blood of Dawnwalker’s structure is tailor-made for replayability. Players have **30 in-game days** to accomplish the main objective—saving the protagonist’s family. Since time advances through quests and upgrading skills, every decision matters; you can’t do it all in a single run. The addition of New Game Plus could let fans push further, gradually maxing out a character or chasing unfinished quests by preserving some progress between playthroughs. It’s a setup that could turn each run into a uniquely strategic experience, and that prospect alone has players speculating on how deep the new mode might go.

## What's Next for the Franchise?

Developer **Rebel Wolves** is clearly planning for the future. The team is already hiring for its next major project, believed to be a sequel to The Blood of Dawnwalker—potentially set in a different era or location. The studio envisions the series stretching across centuries, promising plenty of fresh stories and settings down the line. For now, though, players can look forward to replaying the original game with new twists, once New Game Plus arrives.
