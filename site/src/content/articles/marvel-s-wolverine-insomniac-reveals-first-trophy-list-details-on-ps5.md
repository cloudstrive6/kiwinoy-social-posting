Insomniac Games has given fans an early peek at the **Trophy list for Marvel's Wolverine**, the highly anticipated PS5 exclusive launching soon. The studio shared several non-spoiler Trophies on social media, offering a glimpse at what completionists can expect as they work toward that coveted Platinum Trophy. 

## Trophy Challenges: Parrying, Collectibles, and Upgrades

From what’s shown so far, most of the Trophies appear to be **straightforward and in line with Sony’s recent first-party offerings**—so you shouldn’t expect anything brutally tough or over-complicated. The most notable standout is a Trophy requiring players to **parry 500 times** in combat, making it a potential hurdle depending on your timing and parrying skills. With parrying likely to play a significant role in Wolverine’s fights, making it a consistent part of your playstyle could be the key to success for those looking to go all the way.

Other Trophies highlighted include goals like **collecting all the Whisky Bottles**, **maxing out health by crafting suits**, and taking down enemies in specific ways. There’s also a nod to strategy in how you approach combat and upgrades, but overall the list suggests a game focused on action and collection rather than punishing difficulty spikes.

## What to Expect When Marvel’s Wolverine Launches

With **Marvel’s Wolverine releasing on September 15**, players can look forward to a familiar blend of combat, collecting, and suit upgrades, rather than overly grindy or esoteric Trophy requirements. Insomniac is known for keeping Trophy lists accessible, so longtime fans of games like Spider-Man, Miles Morales, or Ratchet & Clank should feel right at home. If parrying isn’t your strong suit, it might be time to sharpen your reflexes—or get comfortable with the rhythm of Wolverine’s battles.
