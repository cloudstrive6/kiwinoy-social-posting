Marvel Studios has released a new **Avengers: Doomsday** promo that quietly reveals the name of Doctor Doom's late child: **Malachi Von Doom**. The 30-second teaser places Steve Rogers in Latveria at a circular memorial structure inscribed "Beloved Son Malachi Von Doom."

In the promo, Steve interacts with the site, summons **Mjolnir** and channels its power, seemingly trying to destroy the memorial to get inside Doom's headquarters. Why he is breaking it down remains unclear, but the monument itself may be the secret entrance to Castle Doom. **Avengers: Doomsday** arrives in theaters in the United States on **Friday, December 18**.

## A tragic backstory takes shape

Doom's wound has been a pillar of the film's marketing. The Russo Brothers have described Robert Downey Jr.'s version of the character as "ambition and fear woven together," and earlier promos hinted at his grief through Sue Storm's reflection on how broken he is and visuals of him mourning his wife and child. In an interview in early November 2026, co-director **Joe Russo** called Victor von Doom "a father who's a victim of trauma" who had "lost a child," which sparked speculation about the child's identity.

Malachi appears to be a new creation. Across Earth-616 and alternate timelines, Doom has had several children in the comics, including **Kristoff Vernard**, **Caroline le Fay** and the alternate-reality **Qadira**, but none are named Malachi. That gives the filmmakers freedom to shape Victor's Earth-828 tragedy without the weight of dense comic continuity, though Malachi seems to carry the same narrative DNA as Kristoff, whom Victor raised as his prized successor.

## Why this matters

Malachi's death also recontextualizes the post-credits scene of **The Fantastic Four: First Steps**, in which Doom kneels before Reed Richards and Sue Storm's son, Franklin. Franklin may represent the miracle Victor could not achieve for his own son. As a grieving father, Doom likely sees Franklin's cosmic power as a key to bringing Malachi and his wife back, though that is interpretation rather than confirmed fact.

The promo also suggests Malachi's resting place will become an active battleground between Doom and the alliance of **Steve Rogers and Thor**. Steve, himself a father in the film, directly confronting the grave of Doom's child sets up a deeply personal collision. Because the memorial sits at the heart of the assault on Latveria, Doom will presumably defend it fiercely.
