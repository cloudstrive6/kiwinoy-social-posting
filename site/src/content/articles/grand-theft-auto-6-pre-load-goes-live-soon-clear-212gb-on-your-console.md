Rockstar Games has set the stage for the highly anticipated launch of **Grand Theft Auto 6** by revealing key details about its pre-load schedule and file size. If you’re planning to hit the streets of the newest GTA from day one, now’s the time to make room: the digital download will require a **staggering 212GB** free on your console. 

## How to Pre-Load GTA 6

Players can begin pre-loading **Grand Theft Auto 6 on November 10**. On **PlayStation 5**, the pre-load is set to become available at midnight in your local time. For those on **Xbox Series X/S**, the download unlocks at **12am GMT** on the same day, so be sure to check your equivalent time zone. Getting started is straightforward—simply pre-order the game on your platform’s digital store to trigger the pre-load option. Rockstar’s support site also offers a step-by-step guide for both consoles for those who want the smoothest possible launch day experience.

## The Big Picture

This announcement lands as the gaming community is still buzzing about GTA 6’s massive install size and some recent headlines surrounding Rockstar Games. With **212GB** required, space will be at a premium, so it might be time to say goodbye (at least temporarily) to some of your other installed games. As always, a little preparation now could save you from a stressful launch night scramble.
