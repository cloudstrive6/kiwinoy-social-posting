Marvel is heading into the holidays with a frosty one-shot. **Marvel Winter Break Special 2026 #1** goes on sale **December 30, 2026**. It collects **four stories** and includes a Spider-Man adventure co-written by hockey player **Carly "CJ"** of the **Women Professional Hockey League**.

The book is the latest of Marvel's 2026 specials. It has a strong creative lineup, and Marvel shared exclusive pages from several of the stories.

## The four stories

**"The Infinity Bell"** is a superhero New Year's Eve party from **Ryan North and Rachel Stott**. North is currently writing *Fantastic Four* and is also known for comedic books like *The Unbeatable Squirrel Girl*. The preview page shows Stott's clean line work giving the party a realistic feel, with standout fashion design for the guests.

**"Hope You Survive the Ice-Perience"** comes from **Christina Strain and Pete Woods**. The X-Men settle their differences on the ice, and the preview shows **Logan and Scott** taking their rivalry to the rink. Strain first worked with the mutants on her 2017 *Generation X* run.

**"Spider-Man on Ice"** is written by **Stephanie Phillips**, who writes *Daredevil*, with artist **Jamie Jones** and the hockey star. It takes the Wall-Crawler somewhere he rarely goes. The hockey player said, "Spider-Man has been my hero for as long as I can remember! From reading the comics my dad handed down to me to putting on my own mask, Spidey inspires me in a way best summed up by Aunt May: 'I believe there's a hero in all of us.'"

**"Wilbur Has His Day"** is a **Stilt-Man** story from **David Harper and Javier Pulido**. The villain has long been something of a joke among fans. This story looks set to finally give him a win.

## Why this matters

Marvel has marked the season many ways over the years, from the twisted Christmas of *Uncanny X-Men* #143 to Spider-Man skating in Central Park. This special continues that tradition. It pairs a comedy-minded writer with a party setting and puts sports rivalries on the ice. It also gives a lesser-loved villain a spotlight. The mix of tones should make it easy reading for the winter break.
