Arkane Studios—the acclaimed team behind Dishonored and Deathloop—may be forced to close its doors, putting the much-anticipated Marvel's Blade game in jeopardy. **A new report claims Arkane has until November 13 to find a buyer** or face a complete shutdown, potentially scrapping years of development work on the superhero project.

## Studio Faces Deadline, Blade Game at Risk
After Xbox announced a wave of internal studio closures and sales earlier this year, Arkane’s Lyon studio has been searching for a new owner. **According to recent reports, Arkane is running out of time to secure a deal**, threatening the jobs of all current employees and the future of Marvel’s Blade. Multiple buyout offers have reportedly surfaced, but the studio is seeking more time to finalize any potential agreements. 

The stakes are particularly high for Marvel’s Blade, which is said to be **nearing completion** despite no official release date. Early development progress suggested a potential launch as soon as 2027, making the threat of cancellation especially devastating for fans and the team alike.

## Blade: A Troubled Marvel Property
Marvel’s Blade hasn’t had much luck outside of comics in recent years. Not only is the video game adaptation now at risk, but a previously announced Blade movie starring Mahershala Ali also encountered significant internal setbacks. These troubles have led to the perception that the Blade property is almost cursed in non-comic media, with multiple promising projects meeting unfortunate ends.

For now, both Arkane and Xbox remain silent on the details of the negotiations. Fans and industry watchers can only wait to see if last-minute deals can save both the studio and the near-finished Blade game.
