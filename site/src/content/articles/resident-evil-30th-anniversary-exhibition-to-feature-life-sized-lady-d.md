Capcom is giving dedicated Resident Evil fans something to scream about for the series’ 30th anniversary. The company announced it will unveil **life-sized statues of iconic antagonists Lady Dimitrescu and Albert Wesker** at a special exhibition in Tokyo. The showcase will run at the **Shibuya BEAM Gallery from October 30 to December 24**, immersing visitors in a real-world celebration of the legendary horror franchise.

## What to Expect from the Exhibition

This limited-time event is set to feature **real-life recreations of items, creatures, and villains** from across Resident Evil’s storied history. Lady Dimitrescu, one of the most viral antagonists from Resident Evil Village, will be immortalized in a statue that reportedly measures **290 centimeters tall**—about nine and a half feet—though since she’s depicted seated on an ornate chair, the actual display height isn’t fully confirmed. Albert Wesker, the ever-enigmatic series villain, will also appear in his classic sunglasses and trenchcoat, standing tall in another meticulously crafted sculpture.

Concept art reveals how these statues will be presented: Lady Dimitrescu commanding attention from her plush throne, and Wesker exuding menace in a distinct pose. Both statues are going on display in the exhibition’s so-called “Evidence of Horror” zone, with each villain further categorized by their unique threats—Wesker within a section themed around parasites and biological dangers, and Lady Dimitrescu in an area dedicated to fungi and hallucinations.

## Fan Reactions and Exhibition Hype

Resident Evil’s community is already buzzing over the announcement. Social media exploded with jokes, memes, and tongue-in-cheek heist plans centered on the Lady Dimitrescu statue, with many fans humorously calling for extra security. The reaction echoed a similar response to the news of a life-sized Leon Kennedy statue, which will also be present at the exhibition. As excitement builds, it’s clear this anniversary event is hitting the right nostalgic—and mischievous—notes among longtime fans.
