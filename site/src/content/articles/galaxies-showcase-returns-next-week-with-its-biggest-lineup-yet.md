**The Galaxies Showcase is officially back on October 15**, and this year it’s pulling out all the stops with its most ambitious line-up so far. Featuring **over 40 studios and publishers**, the annual digital event aims to highlight some of the most exciting new projects across the gaming landscape, from blockbuster franchises to innovative indie gems. The stream will be hosted by Jane Douglas, well-known for her work with Outside Xbox, and promises a packed evening of reveals and announcements for dedicated fans and newcomers alike.

## How and Where to Watch

The Galaxies Showcase will air at **8pm BST / 9pm CEST / 12pm PT / 3pm ET**. Viewers have several options for tuning in, including streaming directly on Eurogamer's homepage. Returning fans and first-timers can expect a polished, professional presentation, with Jane Douglas steering the show and a large roster of participating studios.

## What to Expect

**Game makers both big and small will be showing off their work.** Notable names include the Square Enix Collective, Krafton (the creators of PUBG), Embark Studios (behind The Finals and Arc Raiders), Focus Entertainment, Kinetic Publishing (the new publishing arm from the Phasmophobia studio), Team17, Saber Interactive, 4Dvinity, Raw Fury, Wired Productions, Frontier Development, ZA/UM, and many others. While specific game announcements are being kept under wraps, organizers promise a mix of “blockbuster franchises, anticipated sequels, and brand-new announcements.”

The Galaxies Showcase distinguishes itself with a mission to give all kinds of games their moment in the spotlight, whether they’re from massive publishers or small, passionate teams. Fans can look forward to discovering both familiar series and as-yet-unknown projects once the event goes live.
