Hideo Kojima was deep in development on **Physint**, a PlayStation project seen as a spiritual successor to his acclaimed Metal Gear Solid series, when Sony unexpectedly ended its support. In his first interview since the dramatic studio shakeup, Kojima explained that the termination of funding left him "stunned" and forced to quickly reassess the future of his studio and staff.

## A Sudden Shift for Kojima Productions

Sony’s decision caught Kojima Productions off guard, especially as Kojima had been juggling both **Physint** and Xbox game **OD**. Kojima recounted that he brought his top executives into the meeting, only to learn that PlayStation would no longer fund Physint. The news was so shocking, Kojima said, that he "couldn’t process" what was happening and was immediately faced with concerns over staff layoffs and the studio’s financial stability.

With developers, scanned actors, and bank loans all depending on Physint’s progress, Kojima feared for the future of his independent studio—a fear heightened by the wave of cancellations and closures across the broader games industry. Despite these worries, Kojima harbored no resentment toward Sony, acknowledging the tough times facing many in the business.

## Xbox Steps In, Project Saved

Determined to keep his team together, Kojima acted fast to keep Physint alive, successfully convincing Microsoft and its new Xbox leadership to take over the project. What started as a PlayStation exclusive is now at home with Xbox, and there’s a real chance it won’t release for PlayStation at all. Kojima Productions insists the switch has not undermined the studio’s health: the team is "in a healthy, profitable state," with development of Physint moving forward and maintaining positive relations with PlayStation.
