Marvel's newest X-Men crossover, **'DNX,'** is underway, shaking up the mutant landscape with fallout from the dramatic 'Age of Revelation' storyline. In this new arc, fan-favorite **Magneto**—who has spent recent months powerless and on the brink—receives a profound reminder of who he truly is, just as a harrowing new scheme threatens mutantkind.

## The Fallout of Age of Revelation

'Age of Revelation' saw **Douglas Ramsey** unleash the devastating X-Virus, killing most humans and mutating many survivors. Although this timeline was avoided thanks to Cyclops’ intervention, **Beast**—now known as the Chairman after surviving his supposed death—wants to bring that catastrophic future to reality. While a cloned, resurrected Beast now leads the main X-Men, the original is back as an imposing antagonist.

Magneto, meanwhile, was left dealing with what was believed to be "Resurrection-Linked Degenerative Sickness" after losing his powers. In **X-Men #38** by Jed MacKay and Tony S. Daniel, the Chairman tries to extract vital genetic information about the X-Virus from Magneto, putting the Master of Magnetism in mortal danger. During this ordeal, Magneto faces a vision of **Charles Xavier**—or perhaps just a figment of his mind—who forces him to confront his failures, his worth without power, and the unique role he plays in mutant destiny.

## Magneto's Turning Point

This mindscape encounter is a defining moment for Magneto, as Xavier reminds him that Beast embedded crucial future data in Magneto’s DNA precisely because of who he is. Various alternate paths, including those where Magneto falls and chaos reigns, underline his importance. Magneto ultimately accepts his charge, refusing to back down—even powerless.

On top of this, recent storylines clarify that Magneto’s illness likely isn’t tied to Krakoan resurrection protocols. Instead, his current state was shaped by mystical resurrection through the Waiting Room, with medical experts like Reed Richards intervening but refusing to restore his powers.
