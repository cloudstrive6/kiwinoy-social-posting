Marvel's Wolverine for PS5 is already making waves ahead of launch, but not just for its gritty gameplay or fan-favorite mutant. A short new trailer — released during Sony's recent State of Play — set social media abuzz after viewers mistook a mysterious villain wielding a glowing purple energy sword for a surprise reveal of Psylocke. Discussion quickly erupted across platforms like YouTube, Instagram, and X, sending speculation into overdrive.

## Who's Actually in the Trailer?

Despite the resemblance triggered by the purple sword, **Insomniac Games has officially identified the character as Lady Deathstrike**, putting an end to the confusion. While the trailer’s character sports some design changes from her comic roots, Insomniac had previously confirmed Lady Deathstrike’s presence, and past teasers already showcased her iconic finger claws and menacing style. The sword, however, is a new element for the character — its origin remaining a mystery — but isn't intended as a nod to Psylocke. The developer’s social posts cleared things up, stunning some fans who thought the visual difference was obvious but causing a stir among those who insisted the sword meant Psylocke had joined the fray.

## What Else Do We Know?

For now, there’s no evidence that Psylocke appears in Marvel’s Wolverine, though other X-Men regulars like Jean Grey and Sabretooth have already been confirmed. Insomniac hasn’t commented further on Psylocke’s potential involvement, meaning speculation will have to wait. In the meantime, fans can expect **Wolverine to face off against Lady Deathstrike** when the game launches exclusively for **PlayStation 5**. The much-discussed trailer joins earlier reveals, including July’s "Ain’t No Hero" video, deepening excitement — and clearly, some confusion — among X-Men faithful.
