Something odd is happening on the **official Starcraft website** just days before BlizzCon kicks off, and fans are taking notice. As of this week, visitors are greeted by **visual glitches and distorted backgrounds** as soon as they access the landing page, which currently features Starcraft Remastered. Embedded gameplay videos are also affected, showing the same jittery effects. While the site offers no direct announcement or obvious hints—no flashing 'Starcraft 3' here—the deliberate nature of these glitches has set the Starcraft community abuzz.

## Fan Theories and Community Reactions

Speculation is running rampant on the Starcraft Subreddit, where longtime players are split between excitement and cautious pessimism. Reactions range from cautious optimism for a major reveal to more cynical jokes referencing Blizzard's mobile game announcement missteps. Some users hope for expanded support for Starcraft 2, including potential updates, a remaster, or a Terran-focused expansion. Naturally, anticipation for a hypothetical Starcraft 3 is also front and center among the community’s wishes, though many wonder if Blizzard would ever greenlight such a project.

## What Could Be Coming at BlizzCon?

These website teases are especially intriguing with **BlizzCon slated to begin on September 10th**. Notably, there’s no dedicated Starcraft panel announced, which suggests that any potential Starcraft announcements would appear during the high-profile opening ceremony. Adding another layer to the mystery, recent reports indicate the **next Starcraft game may not be developed by Blizzard, but by Nexon**. This would mark a significant shift for the franchise, though the potential new direction has some fans cautiously optimistic, particularly given Starcraft's enduring popularity in South Korea.
