The upcoming action RPG **Phantom Blade Zero** from S-Game is turning heads not just with eye-popping visuals, but with its unusually large storage requirements. Ahead of its release on **October 28**, the studio has revealed that while the game's **install size clocks in at a massive 145GB**, players will need **at least 300GB of free space** on their SSDs to complete the preload process.

## System Requirements Revealed
For those hoping to jump into this Unreal Engine 5-powered adventure, the official minimum and recommended PC specs have also been announced. At minimum, you'll need an **Intel Core i7 8700K or AMD Ryzen 5 3600**, paired with a **Nvidia GeForce GTX 1660 (6GB) or AMD Radeon RX 5500 XT (8GB)**, and **16GB of RAM**. This setup is aimed at 1080p resolution and 30 fps, using upscaling and with ray tracing options disabled.

Players looking for a smoother and sharper experience at 1440p and 60 fps will need to step up to the recommended specs: **Intel Core i5 10600K or AMD Ryzen 5 5600X** CPU, with a **Nvidia GeForce RTX 3060 Ti (8GB) or AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT (12GB)**. In both cases, an **SSD is required** for installation and play, and the developers are urging users to have the **latest graphics drivers** ready before launch.

## Why the Big Download?
While the system requirements aren't extraordinarily steep for an Unreal Engine 5 title, the biggest hurdle might be the enormous storage demand during preload. S-Game clarified that although the final install is 145GB, **you must have 300GB free** on your SSD to allow for download and decompression. That temporary space requirement may be a challenge for anyone with a packed drive, making Phantom Blade Zero one of the largest PC game launches in recent memory for sheer disk footprint.
