Insomniac has rolled out a surprise update for **Marvel's Spider-Man 2** — patch **v1.005.000**, released **July 28** — adding two new suits at no extra cost alongside a set of PS5 Pro visual upgrades.

## Two free suits

The headliner is the **"Fresh Start"** suit, inspired by Peter Parker's new look in the **Spider-Man: Brand New Day** film arriving in cinemas that same week. Joining it is a suit based on Spider-Man's appearance in **Marvel Tōkon: Fighting Souls**, the Sony-published fighting game, complete with a cel-shaded aesthetic. Both are **free** to all players.

## Sharper on PS5 Pro

For PS5 Pro owners, the update adds **Enhanced PSSR** upscaling for cleaner image reconstruction, and introduces a new **Power Saver mode** on PS5 that scales back some performance or resolution to reduce the console's energy draw.

Free post-launch content like this is exactly the kind of support that keeps a community locked in long after the credits roll — and with the **Brand New Day** movie tie-in, it's a smart moment to pull lapsed players back into New York. If you shelved your save, now's a great excuse to swing back in.
