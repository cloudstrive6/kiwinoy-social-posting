Insomniac has rolled out a surprise update for **Marvel's Spider-Man 2**, adding two new suits — the **Brand New Day** look and the **Marvel Tōkon** suit — at no extra cost, alongside a **PSSR** sharpness boost for PS5 Pro owners.

Free post-launch content like this is exactly the kind of support that keeps a community locked in long after credits roll. The Brand New Day suit taps into the current movie moment, while Tōkon brings a sleeker, more modern silhouette for players who want a fresh drip in New York.
