**The first post-launch DLC fighter, Phoenix Cyclops, has officially entered the arena in Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls as of November 2, 2026.** Arc System Works isn’t just rolling out a fresh character — a major update is also live, ushering in improvements across online play, game balance, and more. 

## Phoenix Cyclops Arrives
Phoenix Cyclops headlines the game’s new content drop and is available now to anyone who owns the Year 1 Pass. He can also be picked up separately for **$7.99/£6.49**. As a nod to classic Marvel fans, a "Classic" blue and yellow Cyclops costume is available as a premium add-on for **$4.99/£3.99**. Hulk fans can also grab a new Joe Fixit look at the same price. These outfits offer stylish new ways to customize your brawlers in the ring.

## Major Update Hits Gameplay and Online
Beyond the DLC, all players benefit from a substantial free update. **Online multiplayer and cross-play with PC have seen significant stability improvements**, aiming to create a smoother match experience across platforms. Training mode, certain character lessons, and various fighter balances have been tweaked as well.

Notably, **the assist system has been overhauled**. Players will now summon and tag in allies less frequently, meaning matches emphasize one-on-one action and cleaner, more tactical play. This change could redefine strategy for both casual and competitive players.

Extensive patch notes detail every tweak and fix, highlighting Arc System Works’ ongoing commitment to refining the Marvel Tokon experience.
