Formula 1 is about to get a little more horse-y. Cygames' horse girl gacha game **Umamusume: Pretty Derby** has been revealed as a **founding partner** of the Japanese Grand Prix leg of the **2027 F1 World Championship**, and it's bringing a corner renaming along with it.

## What the partnership means

According to the press release, founding partners are "the highest tier supporters of the F1 Japanese Grand Prix," who "bring additional value to the event while helping to shape the direction of its growth." Through marketing, promotional activities and new fan experiences, Umamusume will contribute to the long-term growth of the race.

In practice, that means a tidy sum of money invested in the Grand Prix. It also means Umamusume gets its name on one of **Suzuka Circuit's** iconic corners: the **130R** will become **Umamusume 130R** for around a year. Expect some Umamusume marketing and branding on a banner or two, though Cygames says none of it will be on display during the actual Japanese Grand Prix itself.

## Why Umamusume?

For anyone unfamiliar, Umamusume is an anime and gacha game about anthropomorphised horse girls, who are also based on real-life race horses. The game's global release last year brought plenty of attention to the franchise, and its fans have made a real-world impact. They've donated so much grass to the horse girls' real-life counterparts that it broke the donation website, and raised hundreds of thousands of dollars towards renovating stables.

It's a fun crossover between two very different worlds, and a reminder that F1 is, at heart, very fast mechanical horse racing.
