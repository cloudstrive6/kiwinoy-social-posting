Marvel’s Wolverine has stormed out of the gate, proving the old adage about adamantium resilience true. **In only its first three days, Insomniac Games’ latest superhero outing has moved approximately 1.9 million copies**, shrugging off mixed reviews and meme-fueled debates along the way. This early haul already makes Wolverine one of PlayStation Studios’ biggest launches this year, leaving titles like Saros, Marathon, and Marvel Tokon trailing behind.

## Digital Sales Pull Ahead, But Physical Copies Hold Strong

Of the game’s impressive numbers, a **sizable portion—over a fifth—came from physical sales**, according to new data. That means plenty of fans still want a disc for their collection, even as Sony plots an end to physical PlayStation game production in 2028. Still, **Marvel’s Wolverine skews more digital than some major recent PS5 releases**: only 22.9% of the game’s revenue stemmed from physical editions, a lower ratio compared to Insomniac’s Marvel’s Spider-Man 2 (35%), Astro Bot (46%), and Ghost of Yotei (37.6%). It’s worth noting Wolverine has been on shelves for only a short time, and sales splits could shift heading into the holiday season.

## Spider-Man Players (and Marvel Rivals Fans) Sharpen Their Claws

The data also points to a strong overlap between Wolverine’s new fans and those who’ve swung through New York with Spidey: **82% of Wolverine buyers played Marvel’s Spider-Man, and 70% played Spider-Man 2**. There’s even a notable crossover with Marvel Rivals, with 43% of Wolverine players having also played the hero shooter. While online chatter has focused on everything from the game’s divisive design to some comical “fart scent” memes, it seems criticism hasn’t slowed Logan’s march—or sales—one bit.
