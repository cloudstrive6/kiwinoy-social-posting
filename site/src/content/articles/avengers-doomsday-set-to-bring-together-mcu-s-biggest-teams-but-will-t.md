**Avengers: Doomsday** is primed to rival Endgame as the biggest live-action superhero movie yet, setting the stage for an even larger sequel in Secret Wars. While its title hints at disaster, the film is confirmed to be far more than just another Avengers team-up — with the **X-Men, Fantastic Four, Black Panther’s Wakandans, and the Time Variance Authority** all joining the fight alongside both classic and new Avengers rosters. But the Multiverse Saga’s recent focus on Marvel’s teenage heroes has fans watching for one more pivotal group to join the fray.

## The Young Avengers Rumor Mill Heats Up

Throughout the Multiverse Saga, the MCU has introduced and developed several popular young heroes, fueling expectations for an on-screen team-up of either the **Young Avengers or Champions**. While there’s been no official word on a standalone project, rumors persist that a version of these young crimefighters could debut in **Avengers: Doomsday** and its sequel. At CinemaCon earlier this year, it was confirmed that **Kathryn Newton’s Cassie Lang/Stature** will return in Doomsday, and she’s been briefly shown in trailers alongside Paul Rudd’s Ant-Man. Cassie’s inclusion has sparked speculation that she could team up with **Kamala Khan/Ms. Marvel (Iman Vellani)** and **Kate Bishop/Hawkeye (Hailee Steinfeld)** — both teased together at the end of The Marvels in a scene reminiscent of the original Avengers Initiative pitch. Hints from recent MCU projects and off-screen comments from cast members only amplify the hope that the Young Avengers — or a variation — might finally assemble on the big screen.

## The Maximoff Family, Secret Wars, and What’s Next

Another lingering question is whether other young heroes — like **Wanda Maximoff’s twin sons, Tommy and Billy** — could join the team. The Maximoff family has remained scattered across various MCU projects since WandaVision, with new series like VisionQuest possibly setting up further reunions. Despite talk that Wanda herself might not return fully until the next major film, rumors suggest she could appear in Doomsday to set up bigger events in Secret Wars. If true, the Young Avengers’ most famous comic storyline, Avengers: The Children’s Crusade — featuring their search for Wanda — could provide the perfect inspiration for what’s ahead.
