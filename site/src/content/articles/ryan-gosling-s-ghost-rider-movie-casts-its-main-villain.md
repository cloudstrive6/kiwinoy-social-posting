Ryan Gosling’s upcoming debut as Ghost Rider has just taken a fiery new turn: **Connor Storrie is officially in talks to play the film's main villain**. Announced during Marvel Studios’ San Diego Comic-Con panel, the Ghost Rider movie will see Gosling reunite with director Shawn Levy and is slated to hit theaters in **2028**. While plot details remain tightly under wraps, this latest casting news gives fans their first clues about what kind of dark threats the Spirit of Vengeance may face when he arrives in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

## Who is the villain?

While Storrie’s casting as the movie’s primary antagonist has been confirmed, exactly **which Ghost Rider villain** he'll embody is still a mystery. The Ghost Rider rogues’ gallery isn’t particularly large, and Marvel has already ruled out some options: **Mephisto is off the table** thanks to his recent appearance elsewhere in the MCU. However, one of the leading theories points to **Blackheart, Mephisto’s demonic son,** as a strong candidate. Blackheart’s complex motivations and history with both Daredevil and Ghost Rider could make him a compelling mirror to Gosling’s anti-hero. Other potential possibilities include **Nightmare**, known for his connection to supernatural chaos, or **Zarathos**, the entity responsible for Ghost Rider’s fiery powers. For now, speculation is running wild as to which foe Storrie will bring to life.

## The Ghost Rider mystery deepens

Beyond the villain, plenty of questions remain about the movie’s direction—starting with **which Ghost Rider Gosling will portray**. While many expect him to take on the mantle of Johnny Blaze, Marvel has left the door open for Danny Ketch or another version entirely. One thing we do know: this project came together quickly, with Marvel Studios and Gosling reportedly sharing a unified vision fueled by Gosling’s passion for the character. With Shawn Levy at the helm and this latest casting reveal, anticipation is only mounting for what promises to be an explosive MCU debut for the Spirit of Vengeance.
