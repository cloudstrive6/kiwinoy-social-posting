A major upgrade is on the way for **Metro 2033 Redux and Metro Last Light Redux**, with a free next-gen update launching **22nd October for PC** and **29th October for consoles**. This renewal comes as the Metro series marks a significant milestone, with over **50 million copies sold worldwide** since the original Metro 2033 made its debut sixteen years ago.

## Next-Gen Features and Platform Enhancements

4A Games emphasizes that this isn't a remake or full remaster—the original Metro Redux experience remains intact. However, players can expect a suite of enhancements including **4K textures across all platforms**, improved animations, upgraded audio, and richer environmental detail. Community-requested bug fixes and quality-of-life tweaks are also part of the package.

Platform-specific bonuses arrive for **PlayStation 5, PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X/S**: PS5 Pro sees PSSR, Xbox Series X gets TSR upscaling and anti-aliasing, and Xbox platforms offer spatial audio support. Both consoles support either native 4K at 60fps or upscaled 4K at 120fps (on compatible displays), along with better lighting, shadows, particle effects, and controller-specific immersive feedback like DualSense Adaptive Triggers and Xbox Impulse Triggers. Additional features include hardware tessellation, displacement, and power-saving modes. Xbox users get Play Anywhere and handheld support, while **PC players gain DLSS**, spatial audio improvements, and other quality-of-life fixes.

## Metro’s Future and What’s Next

The full patch notes for the update will be released at launch, but the key takeaway is that the update is **free for all existing owners** on Steam, Epic Games Store, Windows Store, GOG, PS5, and Xbox Series X/S. This update not only refreshes the current Metro experience, but also lays the groundwork for **Metro 2039**, which is scheduled to arrive early next year. Early hands-on impressions suggest Metro 2039 is intensifying the series' trademark sense of atmosphere and danger, while pushing graphical and narrative boundaries further.
