FromSoftware’s next ambitious project, **The Duskbloods**, has officially shifted its release window from **2026 to an unspecified date in 2027**. The action game, which blends elements of Bloodborne, Sekiro, and Dark Souls while introducing jetpacks and dinosaurs, is now targeting a later launch exclusively for **Nintendo Switch 2**.

## Why the Delay?
According to an update from the studio, The Duskbloods is currently in its "final adjustment stage". However, the developers say they need extra time to refine the experience. FromSoftware explained that the decision came after reviewing feedback from recent **network tests**, with developers aiming to further polish combat, strategic depth, and player expression. The team emphasized its gratitude to the community for their ongoing patience and support during this extended development period.

## Early Testing and High Expectations
Appetite for The Duskbloods appears particularly strong. During closed network tests, FromSoftware noted **higher player interest than Elden Ring Nightreign**, even though The Duskbloods is only set for Switch 2. While the network test initially hit connectivity issues—forcing the first test session to end abruptly—these problems were reportedly resolved in later phases. Despite players’ enthusiasm, the lack of recent updates from both publisher Nintendo and FromSoftware left fans questioning the feasibility of the 2026 window, especially after a previously rumored September launch quietly passed without news.

FromSoftware’s willingness to delay The Duskbloods signals a desire to deliver a polished and innovative experience when it does finally arrive. As the studio takes extra time for balancing and final tweaks, anticipation for this experimental title continues to grow.
