**MCU fans are buzzing with fresh speculation after eagle-eyed viewers claim to have spotted Billy Maximoff lurking in the background of VisionQuest’s opening episodes.** While the new Disney+ show has mostly kept its focus on Vision and Tommy, fans are convinced the mysterious character trailing Tommy might be none other than Billy, further tying together the WandaVision, Agatha All Along, and VisionQuest trilogy.

## Familiar Faces—and Familiar Theories

The theory started after audiences spotted a figure with dark curly hair and a similar build to Joe Locke’s Billy in scenes with Tommy. **Though the appearance is not confirmed**, the overlap is hard to ignore, especially since the last time Billy appeared onscreen, he was leaving with Agatha to search for Tommy. It would make narrative sense for Billy to be watching over his brother from a distance, especially given Tommy’s ongoing identity struggles. For now, however, it’s still unconfirmed whether this mystery character really is Billy—and if so, whether Joe Locke has actually returned to the role.

## Could Billy Be Playing a Bigger Role?

Some fans have also speculated that Billy might be involved in a larger way, possibly as the enigmatic character known only as the Client, whose distorted voice has been heard in the series. So far, this remains purely a rumor, but with VisionQuest set to conclude the trilogy, it would be an odd choice for the story to end without resolving Billy and Tommy’s arc. Whether the shadowy figure is Billy keeping his distance, or the Client pulling strings from behind the scenes, fans are watching closely for any sign of a reunion.
