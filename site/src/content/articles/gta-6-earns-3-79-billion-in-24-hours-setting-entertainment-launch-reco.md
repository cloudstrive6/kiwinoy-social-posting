Rockstar Games has confirmed that **Grand Theft Auto VI** earned **$3.79 billion** in retail sales during its first **24 hours**, making it the **biggest entertainment launch of all time**. Parent company **Take-Two Interactive** announced the figure today.

The number is unheard of in any medium. It means the game did not just beat the launch sales of titles like **Red Dead Redemption 2**, **Cyberpunk 2077** and any **Call of Duty** game. It also topped every blockbuster movie release ever.

## Pre-sales far beyond earlier reports

The launch figure follows a report from just yesterday that GTA 6 had sold more than **20 million** pre-order copies. Take-Two has now confirmed that figure undersold reality: the game moved **32.5 million pre-sale units** alone. That is more copies than the vast majority of games have ever sold.

How many non-pre-ordered copies have sold since launch is unclear. GTA 6 arrived on **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X/S** on **November 19**.

Rockstar founder and president **Sam Houser** shared a statement on the milestone. "It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI," he said. "We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn't be more excited to share this moment with you." Take-Two said it was "proud" to help GTA 6 take its new crown.

## The big picture

The result comes after the game quickly emerged as the highest-rated video game of all time, broke records across streaming sites and caused spikes in internet traffic. IGN gave the game a **10/10**.

GTA 6 still has a way to go before it can claim titles such as best all-time sales, but this is a start unlike any in the history of gaming. After 13 years, Rockstar has delivered a launch the industry has never seen.
