After a seemingly non-stop summer for PS5 players, the onslaught of releases isn't slowing down just yet. **More than 20 new PS5 titles** are set to launch next week, keeping the PlayStation Store lively and giving gamers plenty of fresh content to dive into. The week’s lineup has some major standouts: fans of high-octane racing will want to keep an eye on the **new Hot Wheels game**, while indie followers can finally check out the quirky **Mewgenics**. Perhaps the most anticipated is the console debut of **Valheim**, a beloved survival adventure that has already made waves elsewhere.

## The Big Highlights

This batch of **20+ new games** continues the recent trend of busy weeks for PS5, delivering a mix of genres and styles. The new **Hot Wheels** entry is sure to attract racing and nostalgia fans alike, just in time for those itching for arcade action. **Mewgenics** brings its own distinct blend of mechanics for indie enthusiasts, while **Valheim’s** launch marks a long-awaited moment for players wanting to experience its Norse-inspired realms on console.

## What Else Is Happening on PlayStation

As the new releases roll in, it’s also worth noting that **September’s PS Plus Essential games are available to download now**, with redemption running until early October. On top of that, Sony’s recent State of Play event dropped even more news for PS5 owners, while a handful of titles are set to be delisted or disabled soon—so it’s worth keeping an eye on your digital library if any of those games are on your radar.
