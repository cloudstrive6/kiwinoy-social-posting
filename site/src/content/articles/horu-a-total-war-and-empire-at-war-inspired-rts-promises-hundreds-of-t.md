A new space strategy game is aiming for scale on an apocalyptic level. **Horu**, an in-development real-time strategy game, has revealed a trailer promising **"hundreds of thousands of units"** on screen at once, with clouds of spaceships trading laser fire and oceans of infantry being obliterated by artillery.

Horu is inspired by **Total War** and **Star Wars: Empire at War**, and it aims to blend the colossal battles of the former with the galaxy-scale strategy of the latter. Players are challenged to build their own **interstellar empire**, constructing vast space fleets and huge land armies in a bid for ultimate dominion of the cosmos.

## Fully real-time warfare

Unlike the games that inspired it, Horu aspires to be **fully real-time**, with battles playing out simultaneously across the solar system. That covers both land and space combat, so you could punch your dropships through an enemy's orbital fleet to land an army on the surface while the battle in space is still raging.

Keeping track of all that sounds like a challenge, and Horu promises some tools to keep the scale manageable. Chief among them is a **"drop zone system"**, which lets you assign a drop zone on a planet and queue up a set of orders that the assigned army will then carry out automatically.

## What else is planned

Beyond the spectacle, the game has a number of other features on its list:

- **World colonisation and development**
- **Spy networks** and espionage missions
- **Space magic** that lets you cast devastating spells on the battlefield
- A fully simulated eco... (the source text cuts off at this point, so we can't say more about that feature)

Horu is still in development, and no release details were included in the trailer coverage we've seen. For now, it's the sheer size of the battles that stands out, and for fans of big armies and bigger explosions, it's one to watch.
