A new **Blade Runner** video game may be on the horizon, and it could be a big one. **Andrew Kosove** and **Broderick Johnson**, executive producers on the upcoming **Blade Runner 2099** TV miniseries and co-CEOs of **Alcon Entertainment**, which owns the Blade Runner IP, say they have considered adapting the franchise into a game. Should it happen, they say it would target **"triple-A"** scope.

The comments came in a new interview with IGN. Beyond that headline, the pair appeared keen to keep any further details to themselves.

## What we know so far

Blade Runner 2099 is an upcoming miniseries for **Amazon Prime Video**, acting as a sequel to **Blade Runner (1982)** and **Blade Runner 2049 (2017)**. It is set to release on **25th November**.

Kosove and Johnson appear to be in talks about a game rather than in any active development, so if anything comes of the idea it would likely be a long way off. Their aim seems to be getting players to **"interact"** more with the world of Blade Runner, which was recently fleshed out by the 2017 film and is set to be developed further in the upcoming series.

## Blade Runner's game history

This wouldn't be the franchise's first time in games. **1997's Blade Runner**, developed by **Westwood Studios** and published by **Virgin Interactive**, was a point-and-click adventure that captured the mood of Ridley Scott's original film. An **Enhanced Edition** arrived in **2022**, and is more of a remake than a simple enhancement.

In **2023**, Annapurna Interactive unveiled its first internally developed game, **Blade Runner 2033: Labyrinth**. It was set to sit between the original film and Blade Runner 2049, taking place in LA after the Blackout hits, and was promised for PC and consoles. Few details emerged before the game went quiet, and it has since been removed from Annapurna's website and delisted from Steam.

For now, a triple-A Blade Runner game remains an idea rather than an announced project. Fans wanting more of the cyberpunk world sooner can turn to Blade Runner 2099 later this month.
