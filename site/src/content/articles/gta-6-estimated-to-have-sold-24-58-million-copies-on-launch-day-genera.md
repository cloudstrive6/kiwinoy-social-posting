Grand Theft Auto VI may have just delivered one of the biggest single-day launches in gaming history. Market intelligence firm **Sensor Tower** estimates that **GTA 6** sold about **24.58 million units** on day one, generating roughly **$2.36 billion** in revenue. Neither **Rockstar Games** nor **Take-Two Interactive** has confirmed those figures, so treat them as estimates for now.

## Why This Matters

If Sensor Tower is right, that revenue is **76% more** than the **$1.34 billion** GTA 5 earned in its entire launch quarter, and it lands in the **$2 billion-plus** range analysts had been predicting. The firm splits the sales at roughly **75% PlayStation** to **25% Xbox**. Its figures do not account for refunds: Take-Two CEO **Strauss Zelnick** said GTA 6 pre-orders were **"far in excess of expectations"**, but cautioned shareholders not to celebrate too soon, since pre-orders can still be refunded.

## The Big Picture

Take-Two's own console numbers have shifted ahead of launch: a **February 2026** presentation counted **138 million** current-generation consoles as of December 2025, while a presentation this month put the figure at **130 million** as of June 2026. Official sales figures may arrive in stages, with Take-Two possibly sharing 24-hour, launch-weekend and year-end milestones.
