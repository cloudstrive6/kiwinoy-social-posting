Bulkhead’s tactical first-person shooter **Wardogs** has become an instant sensation, moving **over 2 million copies within five days** of its release in Early Access on Steam. Launching on **September 10**, the game immediately became one of Valve’s most-played titles, topping out at 428,666 concurrent players. The game sold **1 million copies in its first day** and **1.25 million by day two**, cementing its status as a breakout hit.

## Wardogs’ Meteoric Debut

Wardogs’ Early Access rollout saw a quick surge in popularity, fueled by players flocking to its squad-based-action and the chance to dive into a new tactical shooter with friends. **Bulkhead**, the studio behind the game, expressed gratitude to the community, posting that they are “beyond humbled” as they watch fans enjoy the very experience they set out to create. Initial launch hiccups were met with some criticism, but user reviews have since climbed to a **'Very Positive'** rating as issues settled and player excitement has kept up. Viral gameplay clips and in-game antics—such as the ongoing meme around the Lonestar faction—have only added to the buzz.

## Looking Forward: Content, Community, and Consoles

Bulkhead has indicated Wardogs will remain in Early Access for **1-2 years**, with ongoing content expansion and continuous tweaks based on player feedback. The team promises a "broader and more polished" full version, noting that the price is set to rise at launch to reflect the upgraded experience. Early players benefit from a lower price and the chance to help shape the game’s direction.

Planned additions include new maps, weapon categories, vehicles (including fighter jets), and a richer variety of gameplay objectives. Bulkhead is also working on more substantial progression systems, a stronger seasonal metagame, and rewards for impactful play across all in-game roles. A **console version is planned for 2028**, signaling a long-term vision for the growing franchise. Above all, the studio is committed to refining balance, performance, and stability throughout Early Access and beyond.
