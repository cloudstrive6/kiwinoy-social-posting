**The Blood of Dawnwalker** has made a powerful entrance, selling **over a million copies worldwide in only three days** since release. Rebel Wolves, the team comprised of ex-Witcher developers, shared the milestone on social media over the weekend, thanking fans for their early support in building the game's growing community. The RPG’s launch has quickly become a standout moment for new single-player experiences in 2026, highlighting the excitement around this ambitious debut title.

## Strong Steam Debut and Community Buzz
Over the launch weekend, The Blood of Dawnwalker hit an impressive **70,692 concurrent players on Steam**. This figure marks a significant achievement for a single-player RPG — a genre where such numbers are rarely seen outside the biggest blockbusters. The game’s early traction suggests a rapidly forming fanbase and high player engagement, factors that bode well for its longevity and future updates. Rebel Wolves has visibly embraced its growing community, emphasizing gratitude and promising to stay responsive to player feedback.

## Listening to Player Feedback
The development team is clearly paying attention to what players want. After showing the first detailed look at the game’s console version, fans expressed clear interest in performance options, especially regarding a 60fps mode on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. In response, Rebel Wolves confirmed they have prioritized this feedback, stating that performance testing results were positive enough to ensure a **Performance Mode will be available from launch day**. 

Eurogamer’s review also praised The Blood of Dawnwalker, awarding it four out of five stars and calling it “an RPG daring to do things differently,” pointing to the game’s fresh approach to the genre.
