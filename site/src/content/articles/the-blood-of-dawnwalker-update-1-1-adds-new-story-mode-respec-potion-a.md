A massive new patch is rolling out for **The Blood of Dawnwalker** next week, promising to enhance the experience for both newcomers and seasoned players. **Update 1.1** introduces a wealth of features, including a freshly designed Story mode with enhanced combat assists and the much-requested ability to pet dogs and cats. The update also brings sweeping technical improvements and over a thousand additional fixes.

## What’s New in Update 1.1

The headline addition is a **brand-new Story mode** designed to provide players with extra combat support, making the game more approachable. The former Story difficulty isn’t gone—it’s now relabeled as **'Forgiving'** difficulty, while the new mode takes its place for those seeking a less punishing experience. 

Players looking to shake up their playstyle will welcome the **Draught of Mind Rebirth**, a potion that lets you forget all your Swordmastery, Witchcraft, and Vampirism abilities. This effectively allows for full ability respecs, enabling even more experimentation with different builds. And for animal lovers, the update introduces an interactive feature—the ability to **pet dogs and cats** within the game world.

## Over 1,100 Improvements and Technical Enhancements

Beyond new features, **Update 1.1** brings a host of under-the-hood tweaks. Expect updates to combat targeting, camera, and traversal, plus improvements to FrameGen technology for better performance on supported systems. Players in Japan will notice that the **Steam version now includes uncensored blood and gore**. All in all, the developers are delivering an update packed with **more than 1,100 improvements and fixes**.

The Blood of Dawnwalker, which arrived in early September, has already received praise for its inventive structure and engaging quests. This comprehensive update aims to smooth out the edges and expand the options for every type of player.
