**Grand Theft Auto 6 unlocks at midnight local time on Thursday, November 19, and Sony says the rollout begins in New Zealand.** The game goes live at 12:00 AM NZDT, which is 11:00 AM UTC on Wednesday, November 18, then rolls out country by country as each time zone reaches midnight.

The PlayStation Blog confirmed the timing on November 10, describing a "rolling midnight launch by country" on PlayStation 5. Rockstar's own support page lists the same midnight local unlock, according to TechTimes. GTA 6 launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S.

## Still on track for November 19

Take-Two Interactive kept the date in its November 9 earnings release. "The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI is ten days away," CEO Strauss Zelnick said, adding that pre-orders were continuing at what the company called "unprecedented levels". Take-Two held its full-year net bookings outlook at $8.0 to $8.2 billion.

Sony said on November 10 that GTA 6 is the most pre-ordered game in PlayStation Store history.

## Two small updates are already out

Pre-loading opened on November 10. Since then, two small pre-launch updates have arrived: version 01.002.000 on November 13 and 01.002.001 on November 16, according to Vice and TechTimes. The second update was about 602.5 MB on PS5 and about 1.6 GB on Xbox, based on figures from dataminers. Rockstar has not published patch notes for either update.

If you have not pre-loaded yet, Rockstar lists an install size of up to 212 GB.

## What is not confirmed

Two launch-week details are circulating without official confirmation:

- **Review embargo.** Insider Gaming reported on November 17, citing leaker NateTheHate and its own sources, that reviews are set to go live at 6:00 AM ET on November 18, which is the same moment as the New Zealand unlock. Rockstar has not announced an embargo time, and no critic review scores had been published when this article went live.
- **United States unlock time.** A PlayStation Store listing reportedly points to midnight ET, which would be 9:00 PM PT on November 18 on the West Coast. Sony and Rockstar have not published a US time table.

There is also no official word yet on launch sales or a PC version. Treat any day-one sales figure as an estimate until Take-Two or Rockstar publishes one.
