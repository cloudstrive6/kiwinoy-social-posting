Owlcat Games has announced that its tactical role-playing sequel **Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy** will launch on **28 January 2027** for **PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series S/X**. A **Switch 2** release date will be provided "at a later date", according to a press release.

## A new role in the 41st century

Dark Heresy follows **Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader**, which released a couple of years ago. In Eurogamer's review of that game, Caeyln Ellis wrote that it "nails the 40k setting and provides an appropriately massive narrative filled with meaty tactical combat, though some bugs and poor performance hold it back."

This time, you play as an **Inquisitor**, tasked with hunting down heretics and purging the world of dark influences. Owlcat has also apparently overhauled many of the systems underpinning the adventure.

## Beta ends 7 January

A beta has been running for a while and offers a **complete first Act**. You can access it by buying a **Founders edition** of the game. The beta will disappear on **7 January**, as Owlcat prepares the game for launch.

## What else Owlcat is working on

Owlcat is also making a substantially different RPG based on **The Expanse** license, a game described as much more closely inspired by **Mass Effect**. That project recently brought in former Dragon Age writer **David Gaider**. It will no doubt hope to fill the gap left by the recent cancellation of **Mass Effect 5**.
