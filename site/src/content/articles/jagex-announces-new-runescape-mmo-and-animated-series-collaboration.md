Jagex has unveiled a major expansion for its iconic MMO universe, announcing a **fourth MMO set in the RuneScape series** and a **new animated adaptation** during RuneFest in Birmingham. The new game, tentatively referred to as RS4, marks the next era for RuneScape fans, while the animated project is being developed in collaboration with YouTuber Charlie "MoistCr1TiKaL" White and LA-based Lyrical Media.

## A New RuneScape Adventure in Gielinor

According to Jagex, RS4 aims to "bring the world of Gielinor to life in Unreal Engine," fusing the classic depth, freedom, and community-driven spirit that defined RuneScape's 25-year history with modern technology. While still in early development and years away from release, the new MMO will not replace existing games—instead, it’s designed to stand alongside titles like Old School RuneScape and RuneScape: Dragonwilds, both of which remain operational with dedicated support teams. RS4 will be set in Gielinor’s Sixth Age, starting in Ashenfall, a region introduced in Dragonwilds, and will expand beyond that area as the game develops. The narrative will take place years after the defeat of Dragon Queen Kuldra, opening up fresh storylines for players to explore.

## Animated Series Expands the RuneScape Universe

Alongside the new game, Jagex announced a **RuneScape animated adaptation** in partnership with Charlie "MoistCr1TiKaL" White and Lyrical Media. This series seeks to draw on the world, characters, and stories built over more than two decades and will tap into RuneScape's rich history and massive player base.

Jagex is emphasizing transparency and community collaboration in the development of RS4, aiming to extend the same participatory approach that shaped its earlier games. The announcement underscores the studio’s intention to expand the RuneScape universe—without taking away from the games players already love.
