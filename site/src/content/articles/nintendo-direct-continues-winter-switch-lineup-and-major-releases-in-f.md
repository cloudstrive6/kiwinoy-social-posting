Nintendo’s September Direct continues into its second day, with the spotlight now on Switch games arriving this winter. The broadcast kicked off at 3pm UK time, promising a flurry of updates on key franchises and a handful of hotly anticipated launches coming in the next few months.

## What to Expect

Fans hoping for more Zelda after the detailed "The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time" remake reveal yesterday will need to look elsewhere, with the showcase opting to clear the way for other franchises. There’s growing speculation around Mario, as it’s been nearly a decade since the last mainline 3D Mario—though development resources have been busy on "Donkey Kong Bananza" in the interim.

While a sneak peek at a distant Mario game might be overly optimistic, the show’s "winter" focus ensures we’re in for more concrete release windows. One near-certainty is an official release date for **FromSoftware’s The Duskbloods**, still poised to arrive this year, though Nintendo fans are watching closely to see if it’ll launch near heavyweight competitors like GTA 6. There’s also hope for news on a long-awaited **Switch 2 version of Diablo 4**, arriving just ahead of BlizzCon, plus fresh footage of **Witcher 3 Remastered on Switch 2** following its recent Gamescom appearance.

## The Big Picture

Nintendo is expected to showcase more of **Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave**, set to drop next week for Switch 2, with the main Direct followed by a mammoth 110-minute Nintendo Treehouse deep-dive. With "winter" covering the calendar through late February, Nintendo still has plenty of room to surprise — whether with fresh reveals or updates on long-rumored projects.

As the showcase unfolds, the main question remains: Will Nintendo keep some big reveals tucked away until later, or will today’s Direct pull back the curtain on every major Switch title heading into 2027?
