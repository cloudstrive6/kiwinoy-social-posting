Marvel Studios pulled back the curtain on **Avengers: Doomsday** at Disney's **D23** fan event on Friday, debuting the film's **second trailer** — and it wastes no time putting **Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom** at the heart of the story.

Per Variety, the trailer shows Doom squaring off against a staggering lineup: **Mister Fantastic and the Fantastic Four**, the **X-Men**, and the **Avengers**, with Doom commanding **an army of Sentinels**. It's the clearest look yet at the scale Marvel is aiming for as the Multiverse Saga barrels toward its climax.

## The biggest crossover on the board

Doomsday is stacking its cast unlike anything the MCU has attempted. Downey Jr. leads as Doom — reportedly speaking with a Latverian accent — opposite the new Fantastic Four (Pedro Pascal, Vanessa Kirby, Joseph Quinn and Ebon Moss-Bachrach) and a legacy **X-Men** contingent that includes Patrick Stewart, Ian McKellen, James Marsden, Rebecca Romijn, Alan Cumming and Kelsey Grammer, alongside returning Avengers.

## When you can see it

**Avengers: Doomsday** is directed by **Joe and Anthony Russo** and is set to hit theaters on **December 18, 2026**. With the D23 trailer now out in the world, expect the theory machine to run at full speed until release.
