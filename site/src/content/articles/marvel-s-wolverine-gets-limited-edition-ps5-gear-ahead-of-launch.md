Marvel’s Wolverine arrives on **September 15**, and Sony has unveiled a full lineup of exclusive hardware to celebrate. Fans can get their hands on a **limited-edition DualSense Wireless Controller**, available in both classic Wolverine "Battle Yellow" and striking "Adamantium" silver colorways, as well as a corresponding **PS5 Digital Edition bundle** and themed **console covers**. These accessories are all available to preorder now—though supplies are going fast.

## Wolverine Accessories Up for Grabs
The new Wolverine hardware collection includes:
- **Limited-edition DualSense controllers** in black with either "Battle Yellow" or "Adamantium" silver accents.
- **PS5 Digital Edition bundles** and **console covers** featuring distinctive Wolverine designs.

Preorders have launched at various retailers, including **PS Direct** and **Smyths Toys UK**. Notably, some items are already selling out—such as the PlayStation 5 Pro covers at PS Direct and the standard yellow DualSense controller at both PS Direct and Smyths Toys (now only available in the UK).

Smyths Toys is also the exclusive supplier for the "Adamantium" DualSense in Ireland, priced at €84.99, and offers free click and collect (with shipping charges for delivery). Other major UK retailers like Amazon, Argos, and Currys are not offering stock at the moment. Meanwhile, PS Direct provides free shipping for orders.

## Game, Gear, and Launch Details
All these special-edition accessories drop on the **same day as Marvel’s Wolverine** for PS5. And if you’re looking to pick up the game at launch, preorders are open now. The game is currently listed at a reduced price of **£62.99 at Smyths Toys in the UK** (down from its usual RRP of £69.99). These Wolverine accessories and the game itself are poised to be hot items leading up to launch, so fans hoping to snag them will want to move quickly.
