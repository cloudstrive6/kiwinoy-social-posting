The legendary stealth classic Thief is making its return in Thief Remastered, and Nightdive Studios is celebrating with special physical editions. **Preorders for the remaster's physical release are now open**, featuring an homage to Thief’s iconic original '90s big box packaging—the unmistakable trapezoidal shape beloved by collectors. But there’s a catch: **these collector's editions are only available for Switch, Switch 2, and PS5**, with PC players left out of the equation.

## A Nod to PC Gaming History
The original Thief set itself apart not only with gameplay but with its unforgettable packaging—a quirky trapezoidal box that remains a sought-after relic among fans. Nightdive and publisher Clear River Games are saluting that legacy with the remaster's special edition, but only for consoles. While skipping a PC version isn’t uncommon for boutique physical releases these days, it stings a bit more for a franchise so deeply rooted in PC gaming’s golden era. Even recent indie releases have made similar moves, but seeing a PC icon like Thief miss out feels especially bittersweet for collectors still hunting for physical treasures.

## The Future of Physical PC Editions
There’s a growing desire among PC gamers for physical releases, fueled by nostalgia and the ongoing appeal of unique packaging. Although some studios continue to embrace physical PC editions—as seen with titles like Baldur’s Gate 3—gaps persist. Interestingly, GOG has teased the possibility of a boutique service that could allow fans to buy physical versions of their digital libraries, a potential game-changer for collectors eager to see big box magic return. But for now, Thief’s iconic packaging will remain out of reach for those on PC when the remaster launches on December 3.
