A team packed with **former Disco Elysium talent** has reemerged under the banner of **Summer Eternal**, finally revealing their long-awaited new project: an "incendiary, physics-driven RPG" called **Red Rooster**. The announcement comes alongside the launch of the **Summer Eternal Anthology**, a hefty, 500-page physical book offering both a deep-dive into modern game development and a detailed look at Red Rooster's creative journey.

## What We Know About Red Rooster

In **Red Rooster**, players take on the role of **Redrick Roost**, a white-collar inmate-turned-arson investigator. Partnered with an opiate-dependent mathematician, you’ll investigate a complex web of fires that seem to be linked to Redrick's own brother — the city's fire chief. The setting is described as a "fractalised border archipelago" in the city of **Arta-Tantor**, promising a blend of political alternative history with a strong science-fiction edge. Gameplay is set to break boundaries, with the team aiming for "fully physics-driven" mechanics that let players move, manipulate, and burn objects throughout a highly reactive world built in Unreal Engine. The studio says this goes beyond static RPG worlds, delivering **dynamic camera movement** and rich, evolving stories powered by both scripted and emergent events.

## Inspiration, Anthology, and Player Ownership

The team behind Summer Eternal draws inspiration from the likes of **Half-Life: Alyx**, **Vampire: The Masquerade: Bloodlines**, and **Planescape: Torment**, as well as TV and literary touchstones like **Fringe** and the works of **Ted Chiang**. The **Summer Eternal Anthology** features exclusive interviews with notable names such as **Yanis Varoufakis, Richard Wolff, Ted Chiang**, and Bioware veterans, plus content from worker-run organizations. It's available now, with purchase also granting membership in the Summer Eternal Players Non-Profit organization—allowing fans to influence the studio and share in future profits from Red Rooster.
