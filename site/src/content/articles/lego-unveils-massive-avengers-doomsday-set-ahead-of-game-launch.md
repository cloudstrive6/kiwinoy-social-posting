Marvel fans have even more to look forward to as LEGO and Marvel team up for a brand-new set inspired by the highly anticipated video game, Avengers: Doomsday. According to exclusive reporting from Collider, the LEGO Avengers: Doomsday set captures the high-stakes energy of the upcoming game and features both classic and new minifigures, including some of Earth's Mightiest Heroes as well as the formidable villain, Doomsday.

Collider confirms that the set showcases a dynamic battle diorama, complete with destructible scenery and various accessories that mirror the action-packed world of the game. Fans can expect minifigures of favorites like Iron Man, Captain America, and Thor, with Doomsday making his LEGO minifigure debut. Early images show detailed builds inspired by some of the game’s environments and vehicles, offering Marvel aficionados a fresh way to dive into the game's narrative before its official release.

While the exact release date for the Avengers: Doomsday LEGO set hasn't been announced yet, Collider notes that it's expected to hit shelves ahead of or alongside the game’s launch. This synergy promises to keep hype high for both the playset and the title, ensuring that collectors and gamers alike can embrace the Doomsday storyline in both digital and brick-built form.

As always, LEGO’s collaboration with Marvel bridges the gap between gaming and collectibles, delivering a product that appeals to both dedicated builders and Marvel fans. For now, fans can enjoy the first look at these heroic new minifigures and prepare for the inevitable showdown when the set becomes available later this year.
