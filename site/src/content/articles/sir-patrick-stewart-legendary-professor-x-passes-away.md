The entertainment world is in shock following the confirmed death of **Sir Patrick Stewart**, the iconic actor whose legacy stretched from the Shakespearean stage to the science fiction frontier—and, for a generation of Marvel fans, defined the live-action portrayal of **Professor Charles Xavier** across the X-Men film franchise.

## A Lasting Legacy as Xavier

Making his live-action debut as Professor X in the original **2000 X-Men movie**, Stewart reprised the role in six more 20th Century Fox movies, including sequels, Wolverine solo outings, and the time-hopping "X-Men: Days of Future Past." His commanding presence and warmth brought gravitas to the beloved telepath, setting the standard for future adaptations and making Professor X a household name.

His death has drawn heartfelt remembrances from across the industry. **Hugh Jackman**, Stewart's longtime X-Men co-star, described him as "one of the greats of stage and screen," hailing his impact as both a performer and friend. **Marvel Studios President Kevin Feige** recalled his first awe-inspiring encounter with Stewart on set, praising his brilliance, charm, and generosity, while **Famke Janssen** called him a "legend" and celebrated the honor of working alongside him. Other tributes poured in from Dafne Keen, David Hayter, James Mangold, and more—all recognizing Stewart's unique talent and enduring mentorship.

## Stewart’s Marvel Impact Continues

Sir Patrick Stewart's career ended with one more Marvel appearance, as the **Russo brothers** confirmed "Avengers: Doomsday" will be his final on-screen role. The directing pair praised Stewart as a legendary figure and thanked him for his contributions both on and off the screen. Stewart’s influence is felt far beyond his roles; he is remembered for his humanity, integrity, and generosity to fans and colleagues alike.
