**Phantom Blade Zero’s** debut at the end of October was met with enthusiasm from both critics and players, and now it’s received its first post-launch update on **PS5 and PC**. Developer S-Game Studio has started to address early feedback with a new patch, giving fans their first taste of the game’s evolving roadmap since release.

## Key Updates and Adjustments

The most significant change in the **latest version 1.0302256** comes in the form of enemy and boss behavior tweaks. One highlight is a **nerf to Hua, Deadly Betrothed**, whose grab attacks have been made less frequent—likely a relief for players who found her particularly challenging. The patch isn’t a major overhaul, but it demonstrates S-Game Studio’s commitment to smoothing out gameplay from the start.

For PC users, this initial update brings additional improvements tailored to the platform, though specifics remain light. Across both platforms, players will notice the introduction of **new audio options**, as well as enhancements to weapon feedback and item descriptions—small touches that aim to improve the overall experience.

## What This Means for Players

Though this inaugural patch doesn’t radically alter Phantom Blade Zero, it’s a promising sign that S-Game Studio is listening closely to their community. The continued balancing of boss encounters and attention to quality-of-life features sets the tone for future support. Expect further tweaks as the player base continues to provide feedback.
