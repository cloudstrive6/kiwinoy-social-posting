The PC Gaming Show: Most Wanted, sponsored by Pot Noodle, is almost here. The showcase airs on **December 3** and will count down the **20 Most Wanted** games, with award-winning presenter **Frankie Ward** returning to host the main show.

The pre-show will be hosted by Future's own presenters, **Midas** and **Jules Gil**, who will be interviewing guests as the countdown gets underway.

## Who is hosting

Frankie Ward is a long-time host of the show, and she brings charm and passion to every game she talks about. This will be her **third show of the year**. She is an award-winning presenter with years of experience, as well as plenty of esports knowledge.

Last year, Ward played Commander Ward, piloting a ship through outer space. **Amelia Tyler**, the BAFTA-nominee and Baldur's Gate 3 narrator, played her shipboard AI as a disembodied floating head. This year's theme is being kept under wraps, and the organizers aren't revealing who Ward will become, so viewers will have to tune in to find out.

On the pre-show side, PC Gamer viewers will be used to seeing Midas in the outlet's videos. Jules Gil is usually found on the Future Games Show, so the pairing is something of a crossover.

## How to watch

The show will stream on **December 3** through PC Gamer's **YouTube**, **Twitch**, and **Steam** pages, or anywhere else that hosts video. The source does not list a start time or the specific games in the countdown, so those details remain to be announced.
