The seventh episode of VisionQuest has raised the stakes yet again, serving up a game-changing moment for AI favorites in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. **After already bidding farewell to Jarvis earlier in the season**, episode 7 leaves fans stunned as another beloved AI, Friday, faces a harrowing encounter at Ultron’s hands. The episode doesn't just drop bombshells about character fates—it also dives deep into Vision’s identity and introduces fresh mysteries about the Client’s origins.

## Did Ultron Really Kill Friday?

The closing scenes of VisionQuest episode 7 have left viewers holding their breath. **Ultron picks up Friday and hurls her off a balcony, her code seemingly crashing to the ground below**. But is this the end for Tony Stark’s digital ally? The show leaves the moment ambiguous. There’s no clear evidence that a physical fall is fatal for an AI—especially when, as the episode clarifies, Friday was inhabiting Jocasta at the time. It’s possible that Ultron simply expelled Friday’s consciousness from Jocasta, perhaps shunting her back into Vision’s Mindspace rather than destroying her entirely. This makes a crucial difference in understanding the event, casting doubt on whether Ultron acted as a villain or had other motives.

## What Does This Mean for Ultron?

Ultron’s actions this episode have left his path more uncertain than ever. While this could represent a classic return to his villainous streak, there are signs the move might have been protective—especially after his earlier, surprisingly heartfelt conversation with Friday, in which he acknowledged seeing Vision as his son. The possibility that Ultron actually saved Friday, instead of killing her, suggests a more complex trajectory for the character, though it’s still unlikely he’ll turn outright heroic any time soon. For now, with just one episode left in the season, fans are left speculating about Friday’s fate and what Ultron’s decision really means for the future of Marvel’s AI characters.
