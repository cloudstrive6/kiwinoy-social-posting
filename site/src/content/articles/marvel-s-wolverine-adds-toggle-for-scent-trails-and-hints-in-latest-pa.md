Insomniac has rolled out a major new update for **Marvel’s Wolverine**, finally allowing players to toggle on or off the game’s contentious scent trails and other exploration hints. The update, which went live on **October 6**, introduces a new ‘Hint’ button mapped to the R3 stick on PlayStation 5 controllers, letting players decide in real time if they want the extra guidance or prefer to find their own way. 

## More Player Control After Community Feedback

This change arrives after months of **persistent community feedback**. Many fans felt that the constantly visible scent trails made exploration too obvious and detracted from the thrill of discovery. Insomniac responded directly to these concerns, announcing on social media that the new toggle option was a direct result of players’ input. The update doesn’t stop at scent trails; it gives players similar control over other in-game hints like **audio cues and nightmare roots**. Now, those looking to immerse themselves and navigate to locations such as the Nightmare Cabin without visual guidance can do just that.

## Additional Fixes and Ongoing Refinements

Insomniac’s latest patch also addresses other community-reported issues, targeting various **bugs and stability problems** to improve the overall experience. This follows an earlier tweak in September that allowed players to turn off whiskey bottle scent trails, material crate audio cues, and nightmare door roots, as well as a reduction in the default visibility of scent trails by lowering their opacity. The studio’s ongoing willingness to adapt shines through, with Insomniac reaffirming its commitment last month to listening closely to player responses – a necessary approach for what may be their most hotly debated game. Even design choices such as the casting of Liam McIntyre have sparked lively community discussions, but the team remains engaged and receptive to feedback.
