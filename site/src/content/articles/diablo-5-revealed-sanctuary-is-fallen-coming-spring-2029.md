Blizzard has officially unveiled **Diablo 5**, confirming the next chapter in its legendary action-RPG franchise will arrive in **spring 2029**. The announcement, made during the opening ceremony of **BlizzCon 2026**, teases a dramatic new direction for the series—this time, evil has already won and Sanctuary is in ruins.

## A Dark New Era for Sanctuary

For the first time in franchise history, the story of **Diablo 5** picks up not with a desperate struggle to avert disaster, but in a world where Diablo himself has already claimed victory. Set a century after the events of Diablo 4, players will explore a fallen Sanctuary—"Sanctuary has fallen. Diablo won," Blizzard revealed. At BlizzCon, Blizzard President **Johanna Faries** and the Diablo 5 development team offered fans an early glimpse at the game's new setting, its grim tone, and its class roster.

The panel confirmed the return of two fan-favorite classes, **Demon Hunter** and **Monk**, alongside the debut of a new class: **Plague Knight**. Beyond that, details on the game’s core systems, progression, and gameplay changes remain under wraps.

## Expanding the Universe: Animated Series and Milestones

That's not all Blizzard had in store. The company has also announced a collaboration with **Netflix** to bring the Diablo universe to the small screen in an upcoming animated series. Though specific storyline details are still unknown, Blizzard teased, "Sanctuary's darkness is spreading," emphasizing a new chapter for the franchise in animation.

With the franchise celebrating its **30th anniversary** and having surpassed **130 million players worldwide** since its debut in 1996, Diablo continues to leave a massive mark on gaming culture. With Diablo 5 and the Netflix animated series on the horizon, the world of Sanctuary is about to get even darker—and much bigger.
