**Daredevil: Born Again** is ending. As first reported by **The Hollywood Reporter**, **Season 3** will be the final chapter of the Disney+ revival series starring **Charlie Cox** as Matt Murdock. The third season finished filming roughly **two months** before the announcement and is now deep in post-production, with a **2027** premiere window targeted — though no specific date has been announced.

## The Big Picture

The decision fits a clear pattern: Marvel is actively pulling back on the number of streaming series it develops. Disney+ recently reversed a **Season 2** renewal for **Wonder Man**, and *Born Again* — despite being well-regarded by fans and critics — reportedly never became a major ratings hit for Marvel Television. In the current environment, that combination wasn't enough to keep it going.

Complicating matters further, showrunner **Dario Scardapane** has exited the project. His contract was up and was not renewed, leaving it unclear who will shepherd the rest of post-production. Scardapane was central to the show's identity as it exists today: during the **2023 writers' and actors' strikes**, Marvel scrapped the original version developed by **Matt Corman** and **Chris Ord** and brought in Scardapane alongside directors and producers **Justin Benson** and **Aaron Moorhead**, reorienting the series toward the grittier tone that made the original **Netflix Daredevil** — cancelled back in **2018** — so beloved.

## What to Expect

**Season 3** has real dramatic weight to carry. **Season 2** left things on a loaded setup: **Wilson Fisk** (**Vincent D'Onofrio**) is mayor of New York and hunting Daredevil, a storyline that demands a payoff. Whether the final season sticks the landing now depends on a post-production stretch navigating a leadership vacuum.

Beyond the show itself, there is **no confirmed MCU future** for either **Charlie Cox** or **Vincent D'Onofrio** after *Born Again* wraps. Any big-screen appearances remain fan speculation, not announced plans. Marvel Television still has projects in development — among them a spy thriller reportedly codenamed **"Zodiac"** — but for the Devil of Hell's Kitchen, at least in this chapter, the curtain is coming down.
