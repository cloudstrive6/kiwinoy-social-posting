MobyGames, the well-known video game database, is stepping up its offerings with **MobyGames Professional**, a new **premium tier that enables developers to claim their game credits and customize personal portfolio pages**. Available now after a beta phase earlier this year, the full launch is scheduled for this autumn. 

## Verified Credits, But No Editing

With the Professional tier, **developers can claim game credits via a verification process** and build portfolio pages to showcase their work. However, MobyGames emphasizes that this **does not allow modification of actual credits information**. Instead, verified users can update personal details that are then marked as verified, but **the structure and content of official credit listings remain unchanged**. This policy continues under Atari's ownership, maintaining the site’s longstanding credit verification procedures.

## The Ongoing Challenge of Missing Credits

One catch: the new feature **does not let developers add themselves back into credits if they've been removed from a game’s official listing**. Over the years, the practice of omitting developers from credits after their departure from projects has frustrated many in the industry, leaving some longstanding contributors unrecognized. Recent releases, like Donkey Kong Country Returns HD and Metroid Prime Remastered, have highlighted these ongoing crediting issues for both developers and translators contracted externally.

## Pricing and Availability

**MobyGames Professional** is priced at **$14.99 per month**, with an annual plan available for **$11.99 per month**. For those looking to try the service, **a 45-day free trial** is currently on offer, giving potential subscribers a chance to test the new portfolio features firsthand.
