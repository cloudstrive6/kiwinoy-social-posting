Brand-new footage from **Avengers: Doomsday** debuted during the 2026 World Series, and immediately set the Marvel fandom abuzz. As fans dove into the latest clips, debates erupted over a couple of intriguing moments—most notably a scene where Scott Lang calls Steve Rogers "Captain America" in front of Sam Wilson, and another charged exchange between Steve and Thor that has viewers raising their eyebrows.

## Is That Really Steve Rogers?
The biggest talking point centers on the identity of 'Steve Rogers' in these new scenes. Fans have pointed out that something feels off about Steve's appearance and mannerisms, sparking a wave of theories that suggest this isn't the hero we remember. Instead, many are speculating that it's actually **Loki**—the God of Mischief—posing as Steve, a move Marvel might be using to mislead audiences in early promos. Side-by-side comparisons of Steve’s expressions from these clips with Tom Hiddleston’s Loki have further fueled the speculation, with fans insisting the smiles and delivery are eerily similar.

## The Hammer Dilemma and Fan Reactions
Another focal point from the footage is a scene where 'Steve' asks Thor if it bothers him that he can lift Mjölnir. Thor’s response—admitting that it does—doesn’t quite align with his reaction in previous films, like **Avengers: Endgame**, where Thor was nothing but supportive when Steve wielded the hammer. This discrepancy is leading many to double down on the Loki theory, with fans suggesting that such a conversation would make a lot more sense between Thor and his trickster brother. With Marvel known for keeping secrets close to the vest, some argue that intentionally editing these teasers to conceal Loki’s true identity fits perfectly with their usual playbook.

While the debate has only grown more heated, there’s still no official word on what’s really going on—and it seems that Marvel fans will just have to wait until the movie's release for answers.
