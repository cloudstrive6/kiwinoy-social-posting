Valve has unveiled a sweeping update for Steam users with the beta launch of **Steam Achievement Sets**, a feature that lets game developers group achievements into customizable collections. As modern games continue to grow through ongoing DLC, expansions, and seasonal updates, Valve’s move aims to make achievement hunting more rewarding and far less confusing for both completionists and casual players alike.

## Achievement Sets: What’s New

Achievement Sets give developers the flexibility to organize their achievements in ways that best suit their games, such as separate groupings for single-player and multiplayer objectives or by character class. **Players will still unlock achievements as they have before**, but now Steam will automatically slot these into designated sets, each complete with its own progress bar and a 'set complete' trophy for your Steam profile. If new achievements are added to an existing set, your previous completion will remain intact until you decide to jump into the new content.

Achievement Sets come in two main types: Base Game (shown in blue), which includes achievements available to everyone who owns the game, and **DLC sets (shown in purple)**, which are only relevant after a player has purchased the extra content. Notably, DLC sets don’t affect your overall game progress unless you own the associated content, making it easier to track your achievements without losing your hard-earned completions.

Developers now have the option to archive achievements or entire sets—ideal for games with time-limited seasonal or holiday content. Archived achievements remain visible for those who unlocked them, while they no longer block new players from achieving 100% completion.

## Updates to Achievement Tracking and Comparison

Coinciding with Achievement Sets, Valve has rolled out a revamped achievements page accessible from the Steam library and on the web, alongside new filters to help you find locked, unlocked, or specific set achievements faster. Enhanced comparison tools now let you stack your progress up against up to three friends at a time—including those who’ve used **Family Sharing**—with visibility into rare achievements and completed sets.

The 'Perfect Games' tab has been renamed to 'Completed', broadening its scope to show games with completed sets alongside fully finished titles. Games already supporting Achievement Sets in beta include **Against the Storm, Age of Wonders 4, American Truck Simulator, Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, Crusader Kings 3, Dredge, Euro Truck Simulator 2, Forza Horizon 5, LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight, Left 4 Dead 2, Portal 2, PowerWash Simulator, PowerWash Simulator 2, Team Fortress 2,** and **Victoria 3**.
