Marvel’s Wolverine is set to hit PlayStation 5 on **September 15**, and anticipation is at a fever pitch. Insomniac Games is bringing its signature single-player superhero style to Logan, promising fans an original action-adventure packed with exactly the kind of close-quarters ferocity you’d expect. But recent gameplay reveals have introduced a twist that’s splitting the fanbase—an unexpected projectile attack.

## New Combat Options Stir Controversy

Traditionally, Wolverine is all about raw, up-close devastation, ripping through enemies and obstacles with his adamantium claws. Insomniac’s game firmly embraces this legacy, showcasing visceral melee combat, intricate special techniques, and even a rage system. Players will be able to equip four Special Techniques at once, with moves like Tornado Spin, Knee Breaker, Spiral Strike, and Bull Rush—each designed to give players greater combat flexibility.

What’s driving debate is one recently unveiled ability: **a projectile attack launched by Wolverine slashing his claws together**. This ranged move puts Logan’s claws to use from a distance, a gameplay addition standard for video games but unexpected for longtime fans of the character’s close-range focus. Some argue that this freedom enriches the experience, while others feel it strays too far from Logan’s roots.

## A Debate With Deep Roots

Discussions among fans are heating up online, but the idea of Wolverine using projectiles isn’t entirely without precedent. Comics have occasionally experimented with his claws, including versions where Logan heats up his claws or delivers massive slashes. Video games—like classic fighting and beat ‘em up titles—have also given Wolverine ranged claw attacks before. 

The introduction of new abilities reflects a long tradition of creative liberty with superhero characters, especially in video games. Whether players want pure comic book accuracy or welcome these gameplay enhancements, Insomniac’s approach is certain to spark conversation. In just over a week, fans will get to decide for themselves whether these bold choices make for the ultimate Wolverine experience.
