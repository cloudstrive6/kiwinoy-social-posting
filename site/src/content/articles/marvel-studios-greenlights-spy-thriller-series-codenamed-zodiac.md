Marvel Studios is taking another swing at the spy genre with a newly revealed project codenamed **'Zodiac.'** The series, confirmed to be in development, marks a return to the shadowy espionage corners of the MCU—a space that has been largely missing since S.H.I.E.L.D.'s disbandment in Captain America: The Winter Soldier. With the project officially greenlit, fans are starting to piece together what this mysterious endeavor might mean for the Marvel universe.

## The Secret History of 'Zodiac' in Marvel

Marvel Comics has long intertwined spycraft with superhero action, and 'Zodiac' is a particularly layered reference. In the comics, Zodiac has been both a villainous collective themed around the twelve astrological signs and, in another iteration, a group comprised entirely of Life Model Decoys—a synthetic technology developed by Nick Fury and his brother, Jake. The name could also reference the 'Great Wheel of the Zodiac' from Secret Warriors: a hidden oversight board composed of members from powerful clandestine organizations, including Hydra, the Hand, and S.H.I.E.L.D. Specifically, this shadow group operates as a kind of Illuminati for Marvel's secret societies, setting the stage for potential power struggles.

## Ties to VisionQuest and the MCU's Spy Foundations

The timing of 'Zodiac' hints at deeper MCU connections. With the upcoming 'VisionQuest' series set to explore artificial intelligence and synthetic beings, there could be build-up to introducing Life Model Decoys into the MCU, possibly connected to the original Human Torch—teased as J.I.M. Hammond at D23. Such technology has deep ties to S.H.I.E.L.D.’s comic history, potentially paving the way for a new era of Marvel espionage stories.

There's also unconfirmed speculation that 'Zodiac' could be a cover for a story featuring Brett Goldstein's Hercules, whose links to the astrological Zodiac in mythology and the Marvel comics as a S.H.I.E.L.D. agent may come into play—though nothing concrete has been announced.
