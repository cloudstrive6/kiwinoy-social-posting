Marvel Rivals' cutest hero is getting the brick treatment. The LEGO Group has revealed **Marvel Rivals: Jeff the Land Shark**, a **495-piece** set that recreates the cutesy, bitey Marvel hero as a fully articulated build.

The set is aimed at collectors **12 and older**, so it's pitched at fans rather than younger builders.

## Size, price and release date

When fully assembled, Jeff the Land Shark measures **5 inches tall, 8.5 inches long and 3 inches wide**. The set is fully articulated, so the figure can be posed once it's built.

Marvel Rivals: Jeff the Land Shark is priced at **$49.99** and will be released on **January 1, 2027**. It's available to **preorder now** on the LEGO site.

## The big picture

Jeff is one of the most recognizable characters in Marvel Rivals, and this set gives fans of the game a way to put the land shark on a shelf. At 495 pieces and under $50, it sits at an approachable price for a collector-focused set.
