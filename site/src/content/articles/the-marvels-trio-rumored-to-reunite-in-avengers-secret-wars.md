After a challenging period for the MCU since Avengers: Endgame, some projects have drawn particular criticism—including **2023's The Marvels**. That film brought together **Captain Marvel, Ms. Marvel, and Monica Rambeau**, hinting at a powerful new superhero team-up. Despite the exciting setup, fans haven't seen these characters appear again, and none are currently confirmed for **Avengers: Doomsday**. But new rumors suggest their story might not be over just yet.

## Secret Wars Reunion in the Works?
Multiple reports now indicate that **The Marvels' trio may finally reunite in Avengers: Secret Wars**, which is scheduled for release next year. This rumored comeback would see Captain Marvel, Ms. Marvel, and Monica Rambeau rallying alongside other Avengers to take on the franchise’s next big threat. With **Nick Fury already confirmed for Secret Wars**, the return of the Marvels could bring closure to their arc and satisfy fans who felt the first movie left things unresolved.

## Monica Rambeau’s Key Role in Upcoming Films
Interestingly, while rumors circulate about all three characters, **Monica Rambeau seems almost certain to appear before Secret Wars—in Avengers: Doomsday.** This speculation is grounded in the post-credits scene from The Marvels, which showed Monica stranded in another universe—specifically, one connected to the X-Men. With the X-Men set for a significant role in Doomsday, Monica’s appearance feels almost inevitable, though Marvel has yet to confirm her involvement. If this holds, it’s possible Monica will reappear in Doomsday, paving the way for the full Marvels team’s reunion in Secret Wars.
