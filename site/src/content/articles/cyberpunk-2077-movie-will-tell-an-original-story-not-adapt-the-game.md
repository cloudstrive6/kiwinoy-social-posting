CD Projekt Red has clarified its approach to the long-anticipated live-action **Cyberpunk 2077 movie**, revealing that the film will explore an original story set within the iconic universe. The studio emphasized that a **direct adaptation of the video game's plot is off the table**, ensuring this cinematic project will forge its own narrative path.

## Exploring Night City From a New Angle

Rather than retelling the journey of **V and Keanu Reeves’ Johnny Silverhand** from the acclaimed game, the creative team aims to present a fresh story in the world of Cyberpunk. The company stated a 1:1 translation isn't the goal, signaling an intention to build on the universe created by tabletop RPG designer Mike Pondsmith and further developed in CD Projekt's games. The Cyberpunk 2077 film is being produced in collaboration with **Paramount (now Skydance)** and brings veteran producer **Lorenzo di Bonaventura** (known for the Transformers series) onboard.

## Expanding the World Beyond the Game

CD Projekt Red has experience telling new stories set in familiar spaces. The popular anime series **Cyberpunk: Edgerunners** won praise for expanding the world of Night City with original characters and plots while staying true to the game's spirit — a strategy now being echoed with the upcoming movie. With the anime returning to Netflix for its second season, the universe continues to grow across multiple mediums.

The developer is also nurturing other creative ventures, such as officially kicking off work on **Project Hadar**, its first game set in an entirely original universe, breaking its tradition of adapting pre-existing worlds like The Witcher and Cyberpunk. Meanwhile, titles like the recently remastered **Witcher 3** and the in-development **Cyberpunk sequel** show CD Projekt's ongoing commitment to expanding its flagship franchises.
