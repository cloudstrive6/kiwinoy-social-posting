Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls has just rolled out a **major new update** on both **PS5 and PC**, bringing much-needed performance improvements and extensive bug fixes after a launch period plagued by technical issues. Developer Arc System Works previously addressed some day-one concerns, but this fresh patch aims to tackle a broader range of player complaints, especially on PC.

## What’s New in the Latest Patch
The biggest highlight for PC users is the fix for errors that caused **heavy processing loads during matches**, which should result in smoother gameplay and fewer interruptions. In addition to this fix, the update includes **a wide array of smaller bug resolutions**—addressing specific character move glitches and squashing issues found only on the PS5 build. The improvements aren’t isolated to just a few areas: nearly all game modes, from **Character Lessons to Online Matches and Arcadia Rumble**, have seen some level of refinement.

A tweak to the **replay functionality** means that previously saved replays will no longer be accessible after updating. PC players will also notice a bigger patch size due to additional optimizations, and are encouraged to complete the game’s shader warm-up before diving back in for a smoother experience. On PS5, new players may still encounter a known issue during the initial setup, but waiting until the full game install completes should resolve the problem, with Arc promising a permanent fix in a future update.

## The Big Picture
This major patch comes as a relief to those who felt Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls didn’t hit its stride out of the gate, especially on PC. With game-wide stability improvements and fixes spanning almost every mode, players can expect a much more polished experience going forward. For those who had to set aside the game due to performance headaches or replay hiccups, now is a solid time to jump back in and test the changes for themselves.
