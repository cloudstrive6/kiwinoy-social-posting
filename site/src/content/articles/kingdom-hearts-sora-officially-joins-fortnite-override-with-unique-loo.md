The worlds of Fortnite and Kingdom Hearts are finally colliding, as **Sora from Kingdom Hearts officially joins Fortnite Override this week**. Epic Games has lifted the curtain on Sora’s Fortnite appearance, giving the fan-favorite hero a new look that blends the signature Kingdom Hearts aesthetic with Fortnite’s vibrant and playful art direction.

## What Sora Brings to Fortnite

Sora enters the game **wielding his iconic keyblade**, which now features a llama keychain nodding to Fortnite’s signature style. The reveal comes with an official video showing off his Fortnite-adapted design, which is getting fans of both franchises talking. Alongside the Sora skin, official Fortnite channels have begun posting more **Kingdom Hearts-themed content**, including character quotations, further fueling excitement. However, as of now, Sora is the only Kingdom Hearts character confirmed for the season.

## Could More Kingdom Hearts Characters Arrive?

There’s significant speculation within the community that **other Kingdom Hearts favorites like Kairi, Riku, and Roxas could join Sora** as new skins. While nothing is confirmed yet, the themed social media activity has fans hopeful the entire Kingdom Hearts gang may soon be available to add to their collection. For now, the spotlight is firmly on Sora as he becomes a featured player in Fortnite’s ongoing crossovers for the Override season.
