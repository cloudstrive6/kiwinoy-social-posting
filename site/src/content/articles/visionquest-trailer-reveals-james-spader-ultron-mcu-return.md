Marvel used **D23** to drop the first real trailer for its Disney+ series **VisionQuest** — and the biggest reveal is a returning villain: **James Spader is back as Ultron**, marking his first MCU appearance since **2015's Avengers: Age of Ultron**.

## Ultron is back — inside Vision's mind

The trailer offers the first look at Spader reprising the role, with **Ultron appearing in human form inside Vision's mind**. It's a striking way to bring the character back, tying his return directly to Vision's own story rather than a straightforward resurrection.

## What VisionQuest is about

The series follows Paul Bettany's **White Vision** on an existential quest to reclaim his lost memories and humanity after the events of WandaVision, while on the run with a mysterious teenager linked to his past. VisionQuest is billed as the **final chapter in the trilogy** that began with **WandaVision** and continued with **Agatha All Along**. It runs **eight episodes**, with **Terry Matalas** as showrunner.

## A stacked cast

Alongside **Paul Bettany** (Vision) and **James Spader** (Ultron), the series features Todd Stashwick as Paladin, Ruaridh Mollica as Tommy Maximoff, T'Nia Miller as Jocasta, Emily Hampshire as E.D.I.T.H., Orla Brady as F.R.I.D.A.Y., Henry Lewis as D.U.M.-E, Jonathan Sayer as U, and James D'Arcy returning as J.A.R.V.I.S.

## When you can watch

**VisionQuest premieres Wednesday, October 14, 2026, on Disney+.**
