**Discord** has launched a **McDonald's app**, letting users order food through one of the most popular communication platforms around. Powered by **Uber Eats**, the integration comes with its own lofi-inspired interface unique to Discord, and it lands just as gamers are getting their hands on **GTA 6**.

## How it works

The **'Order McDonald's'** feature lives in the Apps section of any Discord DM, channel, or server. You can launch it in a server, which notifies everyone that you're ordering McDonald's, or open it privately through its own DM. Once inside, you can choose **Order Food** or **Play Game**.

Discord says the point of the integration is to let you order without tabbing out of a game or call. In practice, Order Food sends you to an **external Uber Eats link**. If you have the Uber Eats app, Discord on mobile takes you straight to your account, while the desktop version opens your default browser. Either way, you'll need an **Uber Eats account** to order, and you'll still have to pause whatever you're playing. At the very least, you get a neat little animation of approaching the Drive-Thru window.

## The minigame

The game is called **Fast Lane Feast**, and it's pitched for when you're waiting for your order to arrive, though you can play it any time. You drive along a small street, collect **burgers and fries for fuel**, fly off ramps, and try to avoid traffic. Controls are minimal: **A and D** move you left and right. If you get competitive, you can save your score to a **global leaderboard**.

## The bigger picture

McDonald's isn't the only fast-food chain courting gamers. **Wendy's** just launched an entire **RPG**, one of the very few game releases in the month of GTA 6, and has also teased a collaboration with the Rockstar game. McDonald's, meanwhile, may be the only company that hasn't. For now, it's offering the **Pokémon Happy Meal**, with a **Home Alone** collaboration planned for the holiday season.
