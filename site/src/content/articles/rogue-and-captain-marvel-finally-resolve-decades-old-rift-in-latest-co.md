In the latest chapter of Marvel Comics, a pivotal moment unfolds between two of the franchise’s most complex heroines. **Captain Marvel #5 (2026)** sees Carol Danvers and Rogue confront the emotional fallout of their historic clash—a rift that began decades ago when Rogue, then a villain, stole Carol’s powers and memories during their infamous encounter.

## The Legacy of Rogue and Carol’s Turbulent Past

Rogue made her debut in **Avengers Annual #10 (1981)**, launching her Marvel career with a controversial attack on the Avengers alongside Mystique and Destiny. Her assault on Ms. Marvel—Carol’s former alias—not only left Carol physically and mentally shattered, but also left Rogue permanently changed after absorbing both Carol’s powers and memories. Despite later becoming a beloved X-Men member, Rogue’s act left a near-irreparable wedge between the two, with both women haunted by the pain of that fateful day.

## A Confession Years in the Making

In **Captain Marvel #5**, Carol Danvers continues to untangle her own memories, still grappling with traumatic gaps—most notably, the emotionally charged recollection of her father wishing she had died instead of her brother. This fragmented memory, shaped by Rogue’s absorption years ago, resurfaces as Carol confronts personal loss and unresolved anger. At her father’s gravesite, Carol is surprised by the arrival of Rogue and Emma Frost. Rogue, wrestling with her own sense of longing for family, finally confesses that she returned all of Carol’s memories except one—holding onto a core memory about Carol’s father.

Emma Frost helps reconnect this lost moment for Carol, revealing that her father’s last words were not only harsh, but also included an expression of love and regret. Carol, having found her own closure, chooses not to reclaim the memory—allowing Rogue to keep it for herself. This deeply personal exchange culminates in a much-needed forgiveness between the two, closing one of Marvel’s most enduring character feuds.
