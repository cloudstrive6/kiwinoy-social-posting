Insomniac's long-awaited **Wolverine** game is finally out, and early impressions make it clear: it's not the smash hit many hoped for. While the game has its strengths—particularly in the portrayal of Logan—it ultimately doesn't reach the greatness expected from one of **Sony's most high-profile PlayStation 5 exclusives**. For fans and for PlayStation, which has faced a run of troubling headlines recently, this lukewarm reception comes at a tough moment.

## Critical Reception: Mixed Results

Critical response from major outlets points to a game that's "good, even, at least in some ways," but struggles to rise above mediocrity. **GamesRadar** offered a 3/5, noting that while parts of the game capture an energetic, layered vibe, much of it is let down by "thin" level design—repetitive corridors and shallow combat segments that fail to keep things fresh. 

**IGN** gave Wolverine a 6/10, highlighting Liam McIntyre's performance as a particular bright spot. McIntyre brings depth to Logan's character, balancing the hero's animal instincts and compassion. Yet, reviewers found that the gameplay itself "wears thin," ultimately holding the overall experience back.

## The Bigger Picture for PlayStation

This underwhelming debut comes at a time when **PlayStation** can hardly afford another stumble. Fresh off a series of controversies—including the troubled live service scene, high-profile project losses, and the contentious decision to end physical disc production in 2028—the underperformance of Wolverine adds to a growing list of headaches. Especially as a platform-exclusive, fans and critics alike expected more from Insomniac's latest Marvel outing.
