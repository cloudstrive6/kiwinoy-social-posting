**No Man's Sky** continues its ambitious expansion as it officially launches on **iPhone and iPad** today, adding Apple's mobile devices to the game's impressive roster of supported platforms. Developer Hello Games is marking the occasion with the rollout of its latest update, **Forsaken**, bringing seasonally spooky content and major feature enhancements to the sprawling space adventure across all systems.

## Forsaken: A Darker Side of the Universe

Forsaken invites players to explore the "darker side" of No Man's Sky just in time for Halloween. The update introduces mysterious traders who are on the hunt for bizarre samples—ranging from haunted technology to unsettling biological oddities. This content encourages players to seek out the game's more chilling locations, including **derelict hulks, abandoned freighters, and infested outposts**. Successful explorers will unlock new, creepily organic ship modules, cockpits, and interior sets inspired by far-off galaxies, as well as new themed gear like the **larva tank helmet** and the **wriggling grub cannon** for their multi-tool arsenal.

## Big Changes for All Players and a Major Mobile Debut

While the Forsaken content leans into limited-time, seasonal offerings, the update also introduces more permanent gameplay additions and quality-of-life changes. Players will find **new postal missions** that turn them into intergalactic delivery drivers, along with a streamlined UI featuring a **radial weapon selection menu**, a minimal HUD mode, and dozens of new accessibility options. Travel gets a major upgrade too, with "seamless warping"—allowing players to lock onto a star and fly there directly, skipping the traditional galactic map navigation.

Today’s launch makes No Man’s Sky officially available on **iPhone 11 and up**, as well as 3rd generation iPad Air and newer models. The mobile version supports **custom touch controls**, grants access to all previous updates (over 40 in total), and keeps things efficient with a **4GB download size**. The initial portion of the game is even available for free, giving mobile players a risk-free chance to chart their own cosmic journey.
