Funcom is bringing a chilling twist to its survival MMO, **Dune: Awakening**, with the upcoming **Blood and Ice expansion landing December 1st**. This free update takes players far from the scorching deserts of Arrakis and into the frostbitten reaches of a brand new map, the Polar Sink. The expansion will be available on **PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X/S**.

## Breaking the Heat: A New Survival Challenge

With Blood and Ice, Dune: Awakening introduces **around 35 hours of new progression** alongside new vehicle modules, augments, and specialization traits. The expansion’s Polar Sink map fundamentally changes the survival gameplay loop, forcing players to contend with cold instead of heat. Players will have to upgrade their gear using resources unique to this environment to stay warm — a sharp contrast to Arrakis’ signature sweat-inducing heat. The Polar Sink is split into two biomes: the tundra, where opulent strongholds rise, and the heavily fortified ice cap. Once players penetrate these frosty strongholds, they'll be able to harvest ice for chilling their base equipment and speeding up production.

## More Events, Cosmetics, and Features Incoming

Blood and Ice isn’t just about new scenery. The update adds a new Events tab to the main menu, with the Water Crisis event set to follow the current Scourge event after launch. Funcom notes that these events are playable in both single-player and multiplayer modes, with increased frequency, new stories, and free rewards on the way. The expansion also marks the debut of a cosmetics store stocked with rotating items, including designs inspired by the Dune films — timed to coincide with the release of Dune: Part Three in cinemas. Eventually, Funcom aims to make the full cosmetics catalog permanently available.

## Looking Ahead: Offline Play and Cross-Play

Several community requests are also being answered. **Offline single-player mode arrives this month**, with the ability to transfer characters between multiplayer, single-player, and select servers on PC starting January 2027. Full cross-play support will also roll out in January 2027. Funcom says the player base continues to grow, with over a million new players joining since the console launch, and teases further major free updates for next year.
