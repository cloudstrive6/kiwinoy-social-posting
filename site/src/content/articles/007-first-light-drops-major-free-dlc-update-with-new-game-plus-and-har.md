**007 First Light is getting a substantial free DLC update today, bringing a slew of new features and gameplay enhancements to IO Interactive’s critically acclaimed Bond title.** The Extended Operations update answers player requests by introducing New Game Plus, a new difficult 00 mode, a robust photo mode, two new TacSim mission challenges, refined weapon handling, and notable enemy AI tweaks—all at no extra cost.

## New Game Plus and Tougher 00 Mode Arrive

Players who’ve already solved the campaign’s mysteries now have fresh incentive to return: beating 007 First Light once unlocks **New Game Plus** and the newly added “00 difficulty.” Designed to push veterans to their limits, 00 difficulty ramps up the challenge across combat, stealth, and social encounters. Enemies react faster to suspicious sights and sounds, certain guard types are replaced with tougher variants, and players will have to contend with stricter gadget usage rules and far less forgiving combat—expect two-hit kills and less leeway in parrying and dodging.

## More Missions, Smarter Combat, and Photo Ops

TacSim challenges also get an upgrade. One mission puts Bond behind the wheel of the Aston Martin Valhalla again, charging through Slovakian mountains at various times of day while facing new timed objectives (complete with mountable rocket launchers). Another scenario sends 007 sneaking through a redesigned Vietnamese resort, retrieving classified documents before making a rooftop escape—stealth fans, take note.

Weapon handling fidelity is also refined, enhancing the precision of both movement and gunplay. For those seeking a greater challenge, enemies can now throw Bond off ledges, flipping the script on a previously player-only maneuver.

And for the creative, **photo mode** lets players pause the action at any moment—mid-battle included—to adjust lighting, filters, poses, and composition, capturing heroic or chaotic moments in style.

## First Light’s Ongoing Success

Since its release, **007 First Light has earned a reputation among fans as one of the franchise’s best games in years**. Following robust sales and positive reception, IO Interactive looks set to keep the content coming, even as other company projects face turbulence.
