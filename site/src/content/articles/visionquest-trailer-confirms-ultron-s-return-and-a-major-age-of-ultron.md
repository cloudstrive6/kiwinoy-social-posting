Marvel fans expecting **VisionQuest** to simply pick up where WandaVision left off are in for a much bigger story. The first trailer for the Disney+ series confirms that the show will tackle not only what happened to White Vision after WandaVision, but also dig into profound questions about his identity, his memories, and his emotional ties to Wanda, Billy, and Tommy. These fan theories were spot on to some extent—especially as Tommy plays a key role and Vision is shown wrestling with who he really is and whether he ever truly had a family.

## Ultron’s Unexpected Comeback

However, that's far from the only twist. The trailer shocks with the return of **Ultron**, the villain from Avengers: Age of Ultron, appearing alongside a host of other AI characters—each in human form. It turns out these appearances are part of Vision’s "Mindscape," but it’s still a surprise to see Ultron depicted as a grandfatherly figure to Tommy, flipping the script on his classic MCU role.

## A Game-Changing Retcon for Vision

The real bombshell is that **VisionQuest is officially changing the end of Age of Ultron**. Instead of Vision destroying Ultron, as fans believed, showrunner Terry Matalas explains that Vision actually backed Ultron up—showing him mercy and keeping him alive, albeit in an unconventional prison within his mind. This shift does more than revive a fan-favorite villain; it thrusts Vision into new moral territory, making him directly responsible for anything Ultron does after the events of Age of Ultron. While it appears this version of Ultron may have mellowed, this retcon deepens Vision’s character and adds ripple effects to the MCU’s timeline. Some longtime fans may not love the twist, but it’s a bold move that opens up fresh drama between hero, villain, and family.
