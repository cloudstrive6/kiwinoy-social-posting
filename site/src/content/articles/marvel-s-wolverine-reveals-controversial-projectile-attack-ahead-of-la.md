Marvel’s Wolverine from Insomniac Games is almost here, and anticipation is running high among PlayStation 5 players. Launching on **September 15**, the game promises an original single-player action-adventure focused on the ferocious combat style that defines Wolverine. Early footage has highlighted **brutal close-quarters battles** using Logan’s signature claws, but a recent reveal of a new combat ability has sparked a strong reaction in the fanbase.

## A New Move for Logan

Insomniac is introducing additional combat freedom to Marvel’s Wolverine. Alongside the traditional melee attacks, the studio has implemented unique Special Techniques—such as **Tornado Spin, Knee Breaker, Spiral Strike, and Bull Rush**—allowing players to equip four at a time. Most notably, one Special Technique now lets Wolverine unleash a **ranged projectile by slashing his claws together**, enabling him to strike foes from a distance. This marks a significant departure from Logan’s longstanding, up-close-and-personal fighting style, prompting a heated discussion among fans about the authenticity of such an ability for the character.

## Comics vs. Video Game Logic

While some fans argue that projectiles don’t fit Wolverine’s traditional skill set, there is precedent for this kind of attack in the character’s extended history. Comic book arcs have experimented with unique ways for Logan to use his claws—including **heating them up** to set objects ablaze or making unusually long slashes. Video games have also taken creative liberties, with older titles like **Marvel vs. Capcom** featuring slash projectiles, and recent games such as Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls allowing for ranged attacks. This history shows that while the new projectile move isn’t strictly comic canonical, it’s not alien to Wolverine’s digital incarnations.

Whether players want strict fidelity to the comics or are willing to embrace new mechanics for fun’s sake, the debate underscores the passion surrounding Marvel’s Wolverine. With the release just days away, fans are anxious to see whether Insomniac’s bold choices pay off.
