Marvel Studios released the final trailer for **Avengers: Doomsday** on Monday, November 23 (US time), about three and a half weeks before the film opens in cinemas on **Friday, December 18, 2026**.

The big reveal is Steve Rogers. The trailer shows the life he chose at the end of Avengers: Endgame: Chris Evans as Steve, with Peggy Carter and their son, played by Wesley Holloway. That quiet life does not last. The Fantastic Four turn up for Steve, and Pedro Pascal's Reed Richards is among those who come to recruit him.

## Cyclops rallies the X-Men

The trailer also leans into the X-Men. James Marsden is back as Cyclops, calling the X-Men into the fight, a moment fans of the old X-Men films have waited a long time to see in the same story as the Avengers.

## Who is in it

Robert Downey Jr. plays the villain, Victor von Doom. The Russo brothers, Anthony and Joe, direct from a script by Stephen McFeely and Michael Waldron. The returning cast includes Chris Hemsworth, Anthony Mackie, Sebastian Stan, Paul Rudd, Simu Liu, Florence Pugh and Patrick Stewart. Marvel Studios also confirmed Hailee Steinfeld's return as Kate Bishop on Sunday, the day before the trailer arrived.

## When you can see it

Avengers: Doomsday opens on December 18, 2026, the same day as Dune: Part Three. The full trailer, "Avengers: Doomsday | Final Trailer | In Theaters December 18", is on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wb8Tq_HD4E8).
