Nightdive Studios has officially confirmed **The Tarnished Mirror**, a brand new campaign for its upcoming **Thief: The Dark Project Remastered**. This announcement comes shortly after the campaign’s name was leaked through a set of Steam achievements, prompting Nightdive to drop a clever, self-aware teaser acknowledging the widespread speculation.

## The Road to the Reveal

The teaser trailer, which opens by nodding to the recent leaks, features the iconic thief Garrett and showcases artwork by illustrator **Alexandru "Dominus" Negoita**. The video wraps up by revealing the campaign’s logo and teases that a **full reveal** is planned for **November 2nd**. For now, details about what exactly The Tarnished Mirror will include remain under wraps, with Nightdive confirming only its existence and name—more concrete information is promised at the November event.

## What’s New in the Remaster?

Nightdive first announced Thief: The Dark Project Remastered back in June, promising a suite of fan-requested features and upgrades. Players can expect **visual enhancements**, a **weapon wheel**, a **mission selector**, and support for **4K/120fps**. The remaster is heading to **Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PC, PS4, PS5, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X/S**. With The Tarnished Mirror campaign now confirmed, long-time fans and newcomers alike have even more reasons to revisit this stealth classic.
