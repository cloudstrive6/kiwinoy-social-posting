A major chapter opens for Minecraft as Mojang officially reveals **The Sift**, a brand-new dimension joining the Overworld, Nether, and The End. This is the first time a new dimension has been introduced to the iconic sandbox game since The End arrived back in 2011, marking a milestone that fans have been waiting over **14 years** to see. Mojang made the announcement during Minecraft Live following a sneak peek at Minecraft Dungeons 2, where The Sift will make its debut.

## New Ways to Play, New Worlds to Explore

While Mojang is keeping most details under wraps for now, the studio has confirmed that The Sift will bring **fresh biomes, new mobs, and unique methods of exploration** to the Minecraft universe. Players got a brief look at this mysterious environment—and even a glimpse of one of its inhabitants—during the recent livestream. The Sift will first be available in **Minecraft Dungeons 2**, which launches on September 29th, before rolling out to Minecraft’s Java and Bedrock Editions in **2027**. No exact release date has been set, but anticipation is already building for how this new dimension will reshape player adventures.

## A Blockbuster Year for Minecraft

The announcements didn't end with The Sift. Mojang also revealed that **Minecraft World**, a massive new attraction, is set to open at Chessington World of Adventures Resort in London in 2027. The theme park will offer experiences for builders and adventurers alike, including a dedicated Minecraft World Hotel, themed dining, and an ambitious indoor-outdoor rollercoaster. These updates come as Minecraft continues to attract new players daily, underscoring its enduring popularity nearly two decades after launch.
