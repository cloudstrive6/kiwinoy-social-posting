Microsoft has rolled out a surprise incentive for Call of Duty fans looking to pre-order **Modern Warfare 4**, with a limited-time offer of **store credit** for those who secure their copies on **Xbox Series X/S or PC via the Microsoft Store or Xbox app**. Those who pre-order are eligible for **$5 / £4 / €5** in store credit, directly usable across the Microsoft and Xbox digital storefronts.

## How to Claim and Key Details
The promotion wraps up today, **September 1**, closing at **8:59pm PST / 11:59pm EST / 4:59am BST (September 2)**. Although the offer was only recently announced, it actually covers all **pre-orders placed since May 28th**. Already locked in your pre-order after that date? You’re likely eligible. Upon release, customers will receive a **notification on Xbox or PC within 35 days** of launch confirming their store credit, which can also be located under the Offers & Credits section in your user profile. You’ll need to redeem the credit **by December 15**, after which it remains valid for **90 days**. This deal is available in numerous territories, including the US, UK, Canada, Australia, much of Europe, and several other regions worldwide.

## Loyalty Discounts and Platform Differences
The pre-order credit is available for both the **Standard and Vault Editions** of Modern Warfare 4. The higher-end Vault Edition, typically **$100 / £100**, can be further discounted by **10%** for players who own and have played any recent Call of Duty titles—whether purchased or claimed via PlayStation Plus. This loyalty discount only applies if you’re pre-ordering on the same platform as your qualifying Call of Duty title, excluding Nintendo platforms, as previous eligible games are not available there.

On a related note, **Modern Warfare 4 marks the first Call of Duty title in over a decade to launch on a Nintendo platform**, debuting on **PC, PS5, Xbox Series X/S, and Switch 2** on October 23.
