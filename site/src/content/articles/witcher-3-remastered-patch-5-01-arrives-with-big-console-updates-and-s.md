**CD Projekt Red has just pushed out a major update for The Witcher 3 Remastered, delivering a suite of new visual options and key fixes across PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S.** Players can now disable lighting features like Bloom on consoles, addressing feedback about over-bright visuals since the remaster’s launch. The patch also straightens out some quirky bugs and improves performance for returning fans of the beloved RPG.

## What’s New in Patch 5.01

The newly released **patch 5.01** introduces much-requested graphical settings on consoles, letting players tweak lighting effects to better suit their tastes. On the technical side, the update resolves annoying issues like Geralt’s stamina draining unexpectedly while sprinting and dodging, and boats veering out of control. Ladder usage has been polished up, and Vesemir will no longer repeat his Quen Sign advice as often, streamlining gameplay flow. 

**PC users** benefit from fixes to graphics settings application and the occasional mis-selection of GPUs, while voiceover volume and framerate limit problems have been patched on consoles. The remaster’s launch brought millions back to The Witcher 3, but also surfaced new bugs and complaints about brightness changes, making this patch a welcome update.

## Switch 2 Parity on the Horizon

Looking ahead, **CD Projekt Red is already working on an update for the Switch 2 version** of The Witcher 3 Remastered. The goal: bring it in line with other platforms in terms of “clarity and sharpness.” More details are promised in the coming days. All these efforts come ahead of the game's next big chapter, with the expansion “Songs of the Past” set to introduce players to a new region and story content.
