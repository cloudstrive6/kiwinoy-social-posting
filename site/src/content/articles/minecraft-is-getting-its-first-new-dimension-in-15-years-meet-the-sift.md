For the first time since its original release, **Minecraft** is expanding beyond its classic three dimensions. **Mojang has unveiled The Sift**, a colorful new realm set to arrive in both the Bedrock and Java editions of Minecraft, adding to the Overworld, Nether, and The End as the game's latest dimension.

## New Dimension, New Adventures

The Sift is described as **riotously pink**, with a turquoise sky and distinctive terrain populated by unusual bunny-like creatures that walk instead of hop. While the exact gameplay features and how players will enter The Sift in Minecraft remain under wraps, Mojang promises the usual haul of **new mobs, blocks, and biomes**. Expect a world that looks—and likely behaves—quite unlike anything previously seen in the franchise.

## Early Access in Minecraft Dungeons 2

Minecraft fans won't have to wait for the 2027 update to get a glimpse of The Sift. This strange new dimension will debut sooner in **Minecraft Dungeons 2**, launching next week, where it brings some intriguing mechanics. In the action-RPG sequel, The Sift is affected by **"shifting tides"** that influence player characters and includes hazardous new block types as well as both friendly and hostile mobs. Two of the named biomes are **Singer's Meadow**, a verdant area with red sculk flora, and **Carapace**, a desert filled with blue-walled structures and massive fossils. Notably, Minecraft Dungeons 2's version of The Sift might differ in form and access from the main Minecraft experience, as Mojang isn't revealing all the details yet.

## More Biomes on the Horizon

The major content reveal at Minecraft Live didn't stop with dimensions: **a new ice caves biome** is also on the way. These underground areas, found beneath cold surface locations in the Overworld, feature a unique twist—icicles will fall from the ceiling if a torch is placed beneath them, creating a new light-driven hazard for explorers.
