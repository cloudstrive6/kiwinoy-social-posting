Insomniac has confirmed that **Marvel's Wolverine** will run at 60 frames per second **with ray tracing** in its performance mode — and, crucially, that this is the game's **default** presentation on a standard **PlayStation 5**, not something reserved for the PS5 Pro. According to the studio, it's the first time a 60fps ray-traced mode has been the default in one of its games.

## Why this matters

Ray tracing is usually one of the first things sacrificed when a game chases a higher frame rate, because it's so computationally expensive. Shipping both together on base PS5 hardware — and making it the mode most players land in by default — is a meaningfully higher bar than Insomniac has set for itself before.

Senior project director Jess Reiner-Reed told Push Square the target was locked in early: the team wanted 60fps running on the base PS5 in performance mode from the start, and Reiner-Reed said "the game feels super smooth." Head of technology Mike Fitzgerald framed it as delivering the caliber of fidelity the PS5 was always meant to have.

## What to expect

Insomniac notes that pre-release footage has been captured on PS5 Pro, where owners can expect even higher visual fidelity. The team also describes Wolverine as more enclosed in scale than the open-world Spider-Man titles — a design choice that likely gives them more headroom to hit that 60fps-with-ray-tracing target consistently.

**Marvel's Wolverine** launches on **September 15, 2026**, exclusively on PS5.
