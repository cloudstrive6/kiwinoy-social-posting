**Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man** isn't swinging away anytime soon. While Marvel Television's future is a bit uncertain, series creator **Jeff Trammell** confirmed at New York Comic Con that the show’s team is already deep into work not just on the upcoming third season, but also preparing for a possible fourth. Though **Marvel Studios** hasn’t made an official announcement regarding Season 4, fans can look forward to more of Peter Parker’s animated adventures for at least another couple of years.

## Shorter Waits Between Web-Slinging Seasons

Unlike the long gap between the show’s debut in **2025** and its return with Season 2, which doesn’t hit Disney+ until **January 13, 2027**, Trammell noted that Season 3 was greenlit as Season 1 premiered. This head start should mean a shorter wait between the second and third seasons. The series team has wasted no time jumping into both Seasons 3 and 4, aiming to keep Spidey’s animated presence alive and well. However, it’s worth noting that Season 4 isn’t officially confirmed, particularly with Marvel TV’s history of sudden shifts—as seen recently when another project was canceled after renewal.

## What to Expect from Spider-Man’s Alternate Universe

Set in an alternate reality within the Marvel Cinematic Universe, **Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man** puts a unique spin on Peter Parker’s early superhero days. Instead of being mentored by Tony Stark, this version of the webhead is recruited by Norman Osborn, a move that sets the show apart from Tom Holland’s film counterpart. The story draws fresh parallels and differences through Peter’s evolving relationship with Norman and fellow student **Lonnie Lincoln**, who heads toward his own transformation as the villain Tombstone.

Season 2 promises even bigger storylines, centering on the iconic Venom arc and introducing new faces like **Gwen Stacy/Spider-Gwen**. The show’s first season earned significant praise for blending classic coming-of-age Spider-Man themes with inventive new crossovers—including the animated debut of Matt Murdock/Daredevil. With multiple new seasons potentially on the way, Spidey fans have plenty to look forward to.
