In an era where near-photorealistic graphics have become standard, **Etre** stands out by embracing a retro-futuristic visual identity. Developed by LA-based **Studio Lifeforms**, Etre immediately catches the eye thanks to its vibrant, graphic novel-inspired art direction reminiscent of the '70s and '80s. While many games chase realism, Etre’s debut footage reveals a daringly expressive world that’s both distinct and mesmerizing.

## Unique World and Narrative

Set on **Orbis**, a planet-sized alien consciousness teetering on the brink of awakening, Etre drops players into the shoes of **Xyle**. The protagonist’s journey begins as he searches the sentient cityscape for his soulmate, Atel. Studio Lifeforms has rooted the game’s style in the legacy of vintage comics, producing an environment that feels both otherworldly and alive. Visually, Etre draws comparisons to titles like Rez and Tetris Effect, but it applies this trippy aesthetic to a more grounded, narrative-driven action formula.

## Gameplay Influences and Early Impressions

Etre isn't just about looks. Its gameplay is influenced heavily by **Remedy's Control**, promising **semi-open exploration** and a story that adapts to player choices. The combat system mirrors Control’s kinetic, movement-based fights, with enemies that range from bizarre environmental anomalies to sentient geometric shapes. However, early clips suggest the action may not yet match the tactile thrill of its inspirations—a challenge for Studio Lifeforms as they push the experience from visually impressive to truly immersive. 

As of now, there’s no confirmed release date for Etre, but anticipation is mounting as more details surface.
