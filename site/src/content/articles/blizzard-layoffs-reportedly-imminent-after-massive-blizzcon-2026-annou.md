Just days after BlizzCon 2026 wowed fans with a string of high-profile game reveals, reports now suggest that **Blizzard layoffs are 'imminent'** under the Xbox umbrella. The word comes via Bloomberg's Jason Schreier, who discussed the situation on a recent episode of the 8-4 podcast, noting that the cuts could arrive any day now. This follows Blizzard's recent highly successful beta for **World of Warcraft: Forever** and comes as Microsoft is preparing a new round of job reductions across its Xbox division.

## BlizzCon Announcements Planned Ahead of Cuts

At this year's BlizzCon, Blizzard unveiled **Diablo 5**, a new open-world **StarCraft shooter**, a new **World of Warcraft** title, an updated **Warcraft 3 campaign**, and more. Despite the lineup's scale — and some release dates stretching as far as 2030 — Schreier pushed back on the suggestion that these reveals were tied to looming layoffs. He clarified the show's headline titles were in the works long before rumors of cuts surfaced, noting that such events are typically mapped out a year ahead.

Schreier further explained that Blizzard's decision to share far-reaching release targets was meant as a sign of renewed discipline after previous public delays for major projects like **Diablo 4** and **Overwatch 2**. Internally, these long-term plans were standard, but had rarely been communicated to fans until now.

## Not Just a Microsoft Move

The BlizzCon lineup and announcements, according to Schreier, were not motivated by the current wave of layoffs nor were they mandated by Microsoft leadership. Reports indicate that most of the upcoming Xbox cuts have been in motion since mid-year, while BlizzCon content was prepared independently, suggesting Blizzard maintained significant autonomy.

While Blizzard appeared to avoid direct impact from earlier Xbox layoffs this year and was celebrated as a top-performing studio, it now faces the reality of another round of cuts that could affect its staff soon.
