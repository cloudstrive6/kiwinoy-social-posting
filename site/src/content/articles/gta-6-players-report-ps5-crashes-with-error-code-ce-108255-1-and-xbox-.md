Grand Theft Auto 6 has been out for only a few days, and some players are already running into a stubborn problem. Some **PS5** players report the game crashing with error code **CE-108255-1**, and in some cases the crashes are **stopping them from progressing through the story**.

## What players are reporting

One Reddit user said the game "runs perfectly fine for a while," letting them complete missions and chapters, but then crashes after **around 40 minutes** and sends them back to the PS5 home screen. They added that other games, including **GTA 5** and **For Honor**, run without crashing.

Another player told Dexerto that GTA 6 worked fine until **Pipework**, an early mission that introduces Mandy. Now it crashes "every time I use the plunger or stand around the sink with it," which means they can't progress.

Some **PS5 Pro** owners say the game crashes during the opening prologue, either at the same point every time or a few seconds in, and they haven't been able to play since launch. Others say they've cleared their console's cache, rebuilt the database, factory reset their PS5, and even downloaded the game **three times**, with no luck.

The problem isn't limited to Sony's console. Some **Xbox Series X** players have reported crashes too, with one saying the game sometimes crashes after just **15 minutes**.

## Is there a fix?

Not a firm one yet. **Rockstar hasn't acknowledged the crashes** or said whether a patch is on the way, and its support is sending players with console errors to PlayStation or Xbox directly.

PlayStation's support page describes CE-108255-1 simply as "an error occurred with the game/application," and its only advice is to "try updating your game." To do that, highlight GTA 6 on your home screen, press **Options**, and choose **Check for Update**. Beyond that, players can try the general crash advice from Sony.

## The bigger picture

GTA 6 launched on **Thursday** for PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. It's one of the best-reviewed games of all time, though its Metacritic user score has been hit by review bombing over its limited FPS and a purported "woke" agenda. Eurogamer gave it five stars, calling it an open world of "awe, minute detail and impossible scale."
