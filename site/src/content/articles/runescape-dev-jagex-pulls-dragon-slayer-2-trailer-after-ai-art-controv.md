RuneScape developer **Jagex has taken down the trailer for its upcoming Dragon Slayer 2 quest** after eagle-eyed fans noticed something unusual: a player character sporting six fingers and oddly shaped, sneaker-like armour shoes—the latest red flag pointing to the use of generative AI art in official materials. The trailer, showcased during this year's RuneFest in Birmingham, quickly became the center of online discussion as fans flagged these telltale signs across social media.

## Jagex Responds to AI Claims

Shortly after the incident gained traction, **Alec 'Mod Sween' Sween addressed the RuneScape community on Reddit**. Sween confirmed that the trailer was created by an external partner who had "explicitly confirmed that GenAI would not be used". Despite these assurances, the unmistakable six fingers and other visual quirks led Jagex to apologize and remove the video. It's the second generative AI controversy to hit Jagex in quick succession, coming hot on the heels of an earlier issue involving a clothing collaboration. Jagex reiterated that they do not use generative AI for their games or assets, and do not knowingly allow its use.

## Major Overhaul of Review Processes

In response to the latest slip, Jagex said it is **"overhauling" its content review process** to prevent similar incidents in the future. The team admitted that errors were made and accepted responsibility, emphasizing that they "should have caught these things" and cannot simply blame third parties. As an interim step, Jagex plans to work with and commission creators from within the RuneScape community to ensure the next iteration of the Dragon Slayer 2 story lives up to expectations.
