Arc Raiders is back in the spotlight as **Update 1.45.0 goes live**, delivering a slew of improvements and fresh content to keep players engaged in the run-up to the much-anticipated Frozen Trails expansion. With this update, Embark Studios adds a new Raider Project, kicks off the fifth Expedition, introduces the Hornet Hunter Set, and tweaks the game's currency system—making this a must-play patch for active raiders.

## Fresh Content and Final Update Before Frozen Trails

This latest update signals the **final major content drop before the Frozen Trails expansion lands in October**. Players can dive into a brand-new Raider Project helping Apollo investigate "phantom arc signals" that could lead beyond the mountain range, hinting at new territories to come with the next expansion. The update also marks the start of the fifth Expedition, giving veterans and newcomers alike new objectives and rewards to chase.

Plus, as a lighthearted touch, a batch of new community-made rubber ducks—including a confetti duck, a bowtie-wearing penguin duck, and an X-ray duck—have been added to Bird City, letting players brighten up their dens with some cheeky collectibles.

## Big Changes to Cred and Rewards

One of the most critical changes announced is the **retirement of Cred as an in-game currency**. Cred will remain active until October 8, but players are strongly encouraged to spend any remaining currency before then. The reason? Arc Raiders is rolling out a "more expansive reward system" with the Frozen Trails update, making all Raider Deck rewards accessible through this new system. Any rewards you’ve already claimed with Cred will stay in your possession following the update, so nothing is locked behind the old currency wall.

A smaller follow-up update is also planned before October, but Update 1.45.0 is the last content-heavy patch before new adventures open up past the mountains.
