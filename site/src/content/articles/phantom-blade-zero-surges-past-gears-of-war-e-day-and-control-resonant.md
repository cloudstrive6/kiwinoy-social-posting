Chinese action title **Phantom Blade Zero** is making waves before its official release, staking a claim as one of the year's most promising action games. According to newly scrutinized market data, the game has already sold **500,000 copies through Steam pre-orders**, generating an estimated **$24 million in revenue** prior to launch, and is currently outperforming heavyweight competitors like **Gears of War: E-Day** and **Control Resonant** on the platform.

## Phantom Blade Zero Breaks Out

The data, analyzed by Alinea Analytics' Rhys Elliott, highlights how strongly Phantom Blade Zero is resonating with PC players—especially in **China, which leads as its top market**. The game’s popularity extends beyond sales: it has amassed an impressive **2.5 million Steam wishlists** ahead of its debut. Analysis of its audience revealed that a significant portion of those wishlisting the game have also played other action heavyweights, with **41% having played Black Myth Wukong**, **38% Elden Ring**, and **28% Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice**. These stats suggest Phantom Blade Zero is attracting a core audience primed for hardcore action combat.

## Setting Up for a Huge Launch

All pre-launch signals indicate Phantom Blade Zero might not just be a PC phenomenon. Interest appears high for the PlayStation 5 version as well, with market watchers pointing to the possibility of the game becoming **one of the breakout single-player hits of the year**. Its surge comes amid a historic boom in the Chinese games industry, with more Chinese-developed titles finding both critical and commercial success worldwide. Phantom Blade Zero is set to launch for **PS5 and PC on October 28th**, aiming to cement its place among 2026’s biggest releases.
