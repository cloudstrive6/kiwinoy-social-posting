World of Warcraft: Forever’s beta is already making waves among fans—**drawing in 'crazy' player numbers and sparking buzz for its new character naming features**. The update introduces a two-name system, meaning characters can now have both a forename and a surname, giving players way more room for creativity than ever before. As a result, the beta’s servers have quickly filled with a blend of inspired fantasy names and, predictably, a tidal wave of cheeky and risqué monikers.

## Double Names, Double Mischief
The fresh naming format in **WoW: Forever** isn’t just about fun roleplay (though that’s a big plus): it also generates race-appropriate surnames on random, like the skyborne elves sporting wind-related last names. But with newfound freedom comes inevitable mischief—screenshots of questionably-named characters have been popping up across the internet, showcasing everything from nods to bodily functions to outright crude puns. Some standouts shared by players include names like "Hemorrhoid Man," "TBCisgone Forthisshit," and the especially on-the-nose "Bigpeniss Cok."

## Blizzard Responds to the Name Game
As the floodgates open, **Blizzard has reaffirmed that its longstanding World of Warcraft naming policies still apply**—even in beta. Players caught with names that cross the line risk losing access, with suspensions already landing for a handful of reported offenders. Player reports remain the main tool for enforcing these rules, though with community creativity running rampant, keeping the mischief in check may prove an ongoing challenge once the game officially launches in November.
