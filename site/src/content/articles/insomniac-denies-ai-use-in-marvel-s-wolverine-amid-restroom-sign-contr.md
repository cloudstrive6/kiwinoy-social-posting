**Insomniac has responded to fan concerns about the use of AI-generated assets in Marvel’s Wolverine**, after a peculiar restroom sign in the game made waves on social media. The studio’s community director James Stevenson stepped in to dispel the rumors, firmly stating that the asset in question was not produced using AI tools, but was instead the result of a UV map bug. Stevenson assured players that a fix for the sign is on the way and reinforced that there are “no AI generated assets in Marvel’s Wolverine.”

## Developers Clarify: No AI Here

The dispute began when players noticed the odd bathroom sign and quickly speculated that generative AI might be the culprit. Insomniac, however, was quick to clarify that the issue was purely technical, stemming from how the asset’s textures were mapped, rather than from any use of AI creation tools. Some fans challenged the explanation online, but Stevenson reiterated the team’s stance and commitment to resolving the graphical bug.

## Recent Glitches and Studio Transparency

Beyond the sign, Stevenson addressed additional complaints about launch issues, including problems with Wolverine’s Scent Trailers and Audio Sense indicators, which some players reported had caused game crashes. Insomniac acknowledged these concerns, emphasizing the importance of careful testing to prevent introducing further bugs as solutions are deployed.

Amidst wider industry debates over game studios adopting generative AI, Insomniac is standing apart—at least for Marvel’s Wolverine. While some developers, such as Level-5, have confirmed using AI in their recent projects, and others like Krafton, Square Enix, and EA are investing heavily in the technology, Insomniac is making it clear that their mutant hero’s adventure remains an AI-free zone.
