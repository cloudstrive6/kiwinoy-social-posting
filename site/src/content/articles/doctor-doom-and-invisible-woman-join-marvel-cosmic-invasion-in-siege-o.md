**Marvel Cosmic Invasion** fans, get ready: publisher Dotemu has officially detailed the game's second DLC, with the upcoming add-on titled **The Siege of Castle Doom** set for release on **November 10, 2026**. The new content not only expands the roster with two legendary Marvel characters but promises to shake up gameplay with a mysterious new mode.

## New Faces Enter the Fray
A fresh trailer spotlights the two headlining fighters joining the cast: **Doctor Doom** and the **Invisible Woman** from the Fantastic Four. Both will be fully playable and can team up with the existing heroes, expanding your combat options as you battle through the cosmic threats in this action-packed beat-'em-up. With the addition of these heavyweights, players will be able to put together some truly unique super-powered squads.

## What's Next for Marvel Cosmic Invasion
Alongside the new characters, **The Siege of Castle Doom DLC** promises to introduce a brand new game mode—though details are still tightly guarded for now. Whether it’s a boss rush, survival challenge, or something entirely different, it’s clear Dotemu is aiming to inject new replayability into the game. More information on this mysterious mode is expected in the coming weeks, keeping anticipation high among fans eager for fresh challenges.

With two iconic Marvel characters joining the roster and more gameplay variety on the way, The Siege of Castle Doom looks primed to give Marvel Cosmic Invasion a fresh lease on life this fall.
