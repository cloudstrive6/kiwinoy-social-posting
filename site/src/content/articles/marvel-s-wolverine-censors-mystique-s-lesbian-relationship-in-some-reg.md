Reports have emerged that **Marvel's Wolverine** has censored depictions of Mystique's lesbian relationship and marriage to Tyger in certain regions, most notably in the Middle East. Players on Reddit and social media have shared images showing that in these versions of the game, references to the couple are altered or hidden, sparking conversation about regional content modification in major AAA titles.

## How Mystique’s Relationship Was Changed

Screenshots circulating online reveal a photo of Mystique and Tyger on their wedding day has been blurred out, leaving the image entirely blank, although the player can still pick it up. In another example, a letter between the couple is similarly censored, with its contents made unreadable. Dialogue clips have surfaced showing audio and subtitles being censored any time Mystique’s relationship—specifically the word “wife”—is referenced, replacing it with asterisks or muting the line.

These changes appear to be specific to the **Middle Eastern version** of the game. Such tactics are common in media released in the region, where local regulations lead to the removal or alteration of references to LGBTQ+ themes, religious terminology, or other culturally sensitive content. Despite these edits, Mystique’s relationship is not entirely erased; instead, it is censored in references and assets throughout the campaign.

## Broader Impact and Industry Context

This isn’t the first time a **Marvel game from Insomniac and Sony** has faced censorship in certain markets. Marvel’s Spider-Man 2 was previously modified ahead of its release in Saudi Arabia, removing elements such as Pride flags and a side mission involving a gay couple. Similar editing practices are seen in film and television, with words or scenes muted or visually changed in regional releases.

As conversations around ownership and global standards continue—especially following the acquisition of some publishers by Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund—concerns have grown about the future of LGBTQ+ representation in games. Both Marvel’s Wolverine and other major titles are at the center of this debate, balancing creative intent with local regulations.
