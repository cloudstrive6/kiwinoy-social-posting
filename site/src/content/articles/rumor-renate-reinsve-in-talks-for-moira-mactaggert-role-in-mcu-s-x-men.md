The upcoming **Marvel Cinematic Universe X-Men reboot** continues to build its roster, and now new rumors suggest that **Renate Reinsve**, star of Backrooms, could be the next major addition. Reports indicate the actress has met with Marvel about potentially taking on the part of Moira MacTaggert, a central figure from the X-Men comics whose mutant status was revealed in recent years.

## Reinsve’s Potential MCU Jump
If Reinsve joins the project, she would follow other recent casting news, including **Peter Claffey as Beast** and earlier official reveals at D23 for actors playing Professor X, Cyclops, Emma Frost, and Mister Sinister. Interestingly, Reinsve had previously stated in an interview that joining a Marvel movie didn't appeal to her, yet her recent career choices—including her role in the horror film Backrooms—suggest she may be open to branching out. This rumor emerges as another breakout horror star, **Inde Navarrette from Obsession**, was cast as Rogue, making this potential casting all the more notable.

## Moira MacTaggert’s Evolving Role
Moira MacTaggert has had a long history within X-Men media, with appearances in the Fox X-Men movies by Olivia Williams and Rose Byrne. In Marvel Comics, Moira was initially a human ally to the X-Men, but later stories—especially during the Krakoan Era—revealed her to be a mutant with the power to resurrect and relive her life with the knowledge of past experiences. How this will play out in the MCU reboot remains unclear, but it could offer Reinsve a deeply personal, story-driven role without committing heavily to superhero action.
