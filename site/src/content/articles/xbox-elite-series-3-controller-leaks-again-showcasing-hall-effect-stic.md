The **unannounced Xbox Elite Series 3 controller** appears to be all but confirmed following another significant leak—this time thanks to details unearthed in Xbox’s own Design Lab website. The datamined info, which recently made its way to Reddit, offers up a clearer look at Microsoft’s next premium controller and the features it may be packing.

## Major Hardware Upgrades Leak Out
Among the most notable new details: the controller is set to offer **Hall Effect and TMR (Tunnel Magnetoresistance) analogue sticks**, a major step up over traditional sticks. These rely on magnetic tech to reduce wear and eliminate stick drift—one of the most persistent gripes gamers have with analog controls. The leak suggests customers might get to choose between the two technologies, although the default option remains unclear for now.

The Elite Series 3 also looks set to introduce **advanced haptics**, possibly inspired by the PlayStation 5’s DualSense, giving players more immersive feedback. The beloved **impulse triggers** are expected to return, this time with their own multi-stage presses and haptics. There’s also mention of a **dedicated audio scroll wheel**—previously rumored in earlier leaks—and customizable, replaceable faceplates with several potential d-pad options.

## Different Versions Incoming?
According to the leak, Xbox plans to offer the Elite Series 3 in two main editions: **Base** and **Complete**, both with access to optional haptics motors. This would effectively create four possible product variations, giving players a degree of choice that matches the controller’s premium reputation. The Elite brand has long been aimed at dedicated and competitive gamers, with the last two versions spaced a few years apart and offering various price points and features.

While these details aren't official yet, the depth of the leak and its presence in Xbox's own design pipeline suggest a reveal may be on the horizon. All of this comes as the wider Xbox business sees major shifts, with internal changes, layoffs, and franchise moves shaping the future of the platform.
