Grand Theft Auto has long been home to some of gaming's most spectacular community stunts, and **GTA 6** is already carrying that tradition forward. In the game's release week, players have begun posting daring feats in the brand-new world, from tight-squeeze flying to high-risk vehicle jumps.

## The first stunts of GTA 6

One standout is a TikTok video from user **Gstitz**, recorded early in the game. It claims to be the **"world's first"** demonstration of a **Mallard** flying sideways through a narrow gap in a large building. The attempt takes several tries, but the player eventually pulls it off without crashing or breaking any parts.

A more grounded approach comes from YouTuber **Hazardous**, who flings **motorbikes and cars** around the map while scouting promising high-risk jumps. Some of these locations are tied to in-game challenges, which suggests **Rockstar** has deliberately left breadcrumbs to encourage this kind of experimentation.

These clips are still a long way from the truly absurd stunts seen in earlier Grand Theft Auto games. Even so, they make a strong start as a firing gun for the wider community, and they are among the most impressive things seen in the game's opening week.

## A busy launch week

Stunts are only part of the story. Players have been entranced by the game's **slapstick violence** and its **in-game social media**. **UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham** has even praised the game, despite not having had time to actually play it. Eurogamer editor-in-chief **Chris Tapsell** came away impressed too, lauding the game in a **five-star review**.

GTA 6 is available on **PS5** and **Xbox Series X/S**. If you're playing and need a hand, Eurogamer has published a walkthrough and a cheat list for the game.

## The big picture

If the first few days are any guide, the community is only warming up. With challenge-linked jump spots already in the world, expect more creative stunt clips to follow as players learn the map and its vehicles.
