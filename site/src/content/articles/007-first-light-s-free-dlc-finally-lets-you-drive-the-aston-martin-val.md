**007: First Light** fans have something big to celebrate—IO Interactive has released a free DLC pack called 'Extended Operations' that finally lets players take the coveted Aston Martin Valhalla out on the open road. While the base game teased players with glimpses of Bond’s legendary ride, actual driving sequences were disappointingly brief. Now, players can finally experience what it's like to command one of the world’s most famous spy cars in an all-new mission. 

## Taking the Valhalla for a Spin

The highlight of the 'Extended Operations' DLC is the 'Valhalla Protocol,' a mission set in First Light’s TacSim mode. Designed to feel like a big-budget twist on classic VR missions, this standalone operation puts players behind the wheel of Bond’s heavily armed Aston Martin, racing through Slovakian mountains, evading drones, and unleashing rockets and machine guns on enemy vehicles. It’s a long-awaited payoff for fans eager to see the Valhalla in action after it was confined mostly to a garage through the main campaign.

## More Than Just a Joyride

Alongside Valhalla Protocol, the DLC introduces the 'Jungle Heist' TacSim operation, reimagining the Vietnamese hotel location into a randomized challenge where Bond must crack a safe in a luxurious villa. The update also brings several quality-of-life additions, including a Photo Mode, New Game+ with customizable suits and gadgets, and a tough new '00' difficulty setting unlocked after finishing the main story. As an extra thank you to fans, players can now even pet a bulldog in-game—a quirky nod to the game’s impressive milestone of reaching 7 million wishlists.
