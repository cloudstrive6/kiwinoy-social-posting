**Grand Theft Auto 6** has added another record to its growing list. Rockstar's open-world crime game has reportedly pulled in more than **420 million hours watched** across livestreaming platforms in its first seven days on sale, according to Streams Charts.

That is nearly **three times** the previous record, held by Valorant's closed beta in April 2020. It also tops the combined first-week watch time of Valorant, Elden Ring, Diablo 4 and Lost Ark.

## Twitch, YouTube and Kick

GTA 6 reportedly dominated on Twitch, YouTube and Kick. After attracting more viewers on launch day than any game ever, YouTube led in average audience numbers during the first **six hours** of launch. Twitch then took over in content volume over the following days. Twitch's GTA 6 watch time at launch was higher than any other game in the platform's history.

Streams Charts also noted that **1,056 channels** hit new all-time peak viewership while streaming the game, in some cases multiplying their previous records tenfold. Big names like **IShowSpeed, IlloJuan and TheBurntPeanut** stole the spotlight, but many smaller channels also set personal bests.

## A record-setting launch all round

Livestreaming is only one part of the picture. Publisher Take-Two called GTA 6 the **biggest entertainment launch in history**, with **$3.79bn (£2.89bn)** in revenue from **32.5 million copies** sold on day one.

The wider industry has felt the effect too. Anticipation for the game boosted US hardware sales for both PlayStation and Xbox, and GTA 6 became each console brand's most pre-ordered title ever. According to Sony, more than **39 million people** played GTA 6 on PS5 at launch, and the game recorded the highest number of concurrent players ever on any PlayStation console, beating even the most popular free-to-play titles.

GTA 6 is available on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S.
