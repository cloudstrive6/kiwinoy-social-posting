The MCU is preparing to dive into darker territory, as a **Midnight Sons movie is in development**. The groundwork for this supernatural-focused team appears to be building up, especially with the upcoming **Ghost Rider movie featuring Ryan Gosling as Johnny Blaze**. However, Marvel’s exploration of the supernatural is anything but new, with multiple properties introducing mysterious and monstrous characters over the years.

## How the Midnight Sons Are Forming
From early experiments with Hulu’s now-scrapped supernatural series like Helstrom and a planned Ghost Rider show, the MCU shifted gears by weaving occult storylines directly into its films and special presentations. Both Doctor Strange movies, Disney+’s **Werewolf by Night special**, and Blade’s tease in Eternals have steadily introduced key characters. Now, with Ghost Rider joining the universe, all eyes are on which supernatural heroes (and monsters) will be chosen for the Midnight Sons lineup.

## Key Characters Already Hiding in the MCU
Several members from the comics’ Midnight Sons have already made appearances. **Wong**, who has risen in prominence as Sorcerer Supreme following the Snap, stands out as a natural team leader—he assembled the squad in the comics’ “Damnation” arc, summoning the likes of Blade, Moon Knight, Elsa Bloodstone, Man-Thing, Iron Fist, Scarlet Spider, and Ghost Rider against a supernatural threat.

Fans of the **Werewolf by Night** special will recognize **Man-Thing**, the unforgettable creature who alternates between a gentle personality (when called "Ted") and a terrifying powerhouse. Elsa Bloodstone, another key character, was introduced as a monster hunter wielding a mystical relic granting supernatural abilities—making her an ideal candidate for the new team. And of course, Jack Russell, the Werewolf by Night himself, showcased his loyalty and ferocity, hinting at major potential as a Midnight Son. With so many of these characters already established, the stage is set for a unique and monstrous ensemble.
