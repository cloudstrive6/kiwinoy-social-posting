A **Grand Theft Auto 6** player says they have been hit with a **30-day PlayStation Network ban** after sending a friend a photo taken in the game's nudist resort.

Posting on Twitter/X on Friday evening, user **DerrickGott007** said they had "just got a 30-day PSN social features ban" for sharing the image, which was captured with the in-game camera and sent through PlayStation's messaging service. They warned other players against doing the same, writing: "Do not, I repeat do not send any photos taken with the in-game camera, across the PSN messaging network that contain nudity."

## What the notice says

In a screenshot of the notice, **PlayStation Safety** states that content sent from the player's account via Message on PlayStation "breached the Code of Conduct". The account has been "temporarily suspended from using PlayStation social features for 30 days" due to "pornographic or obscene content".

The notice also carries a warning: "further offenses may result in temporary or permanent suspension from the whole of PlayStation, not just social features."

PlayStation's Code of Conduct asks players not to "share, solicit, access, distribute or store material that is obscene, pornographic or illegal". It also says Sony "may take any action as reasonably necessary to protect PlayStation and our players". As Dexerto points out, the code does not make an exception for content taken from games, even those rated for adults.

## Why this matters

The case is a useful reminder for anyone using GTA 6's in-game camera. Whatever the game itself allows, images you share outside it through platform services are covered by the platform's own rules. Anyone who has snapped something cheeky in Leonida, such as full-frontal nudity in the nudist resort, may want to keep it to themselves rather than send it over PSN messaging.

It's worth noting that this is one player's account of events, based on their own post and the notice they shared.

**GTA 6** launched on **Thursday, 19th November**, for **PS5 and Xbox Series X/S**. It is one of the best-reviewed games of all time, though its Metacritic user score tells a different story, as players review bomb it over its limited FPS and purported "woke" agenda.
