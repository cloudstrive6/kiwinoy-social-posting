Tom Holland's wall-crawler just rewrote the MCU record books. **Spider-Man: Brand New Day** is now officially the **highest-grossing Marvel Cinematic Universe film in U.S. history**, overtaking **Avengers: Endgame** at the domestic box office earlier this week.

Per GamesRadar and multiple box-office trades, Brand New Day pushed past Endgame's **$858 million** domestic lifetime total — a mark the 2019 Avengers finale had held for more than six years — to claim the domestic crown for the franchise.

## A record-smashing run

Brand New Day didn't just squeak past the finish line — it got here faster than any Marvel movie before it:

- **Biggest domestic opening ever:** roughly **$360 million**, edging out Endgame's $357.1 million from 2019.
- **Fastest to $500 million domestic:** seven days, one better than Endgame's eight.
- **$1 billion worldwide in six days**, and it has already overtaken *Toy Story 5* as the year's highest-grossing film.

## One important asterisk

Here's the part the viral posts tend to skip: this is a **domestic** record. Globally, **Avengers: Endgame still wears the crown** with roughly **$2.7 billion** worldwide, and Brand New Day — sitting near **$2.2 billion** — trails it by about half a billion. So Spidey is the new king of the *U.S.* box office, not (yet) the world.

## Why it matters

It's a statement about star power. A solo Spider-Man film out-earning a culminating, decade-in-the-making Avengers event on home turf suggests the wall-crawler may be the single biggest draw Marvel has. With **Avengers: Doomsday** still on the way, the studio's box-office ceiling just got a whole lot higher.
