**Marvel Studios has officially shifted the release date for Avengers: Doomsday in the UK from December 18 to December 16, generating immediate uproar among the international fanbase about early spoilers and leaks.** The date change was confirmed ahead of the still-upcoming Avengers: Endgame Encore re-release, which itself has been affected by leaked footage undermining its rollout.

## UK Gets Doomsday Two Days Early—And Fans Aren’t Happy

The news comes as **Disney confirmed UK theaters will premiere Avengers: Doomsday ahead of other territories**, a move that’s already drawing criticism from fans online. Many are voicing concerns that the **early UK release could lead to widespread spoilers**, upsetting the global MCU community. This isn’t the first time Marvel has staggered its premiere dates—previous films like Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame also released slightly earlier in the UK—but the backlash appears especially fierce this time around, in part due to fresh frustration over recent leaks for Endgame Encore.

## More Leak Fears Amid Already-Controversial Movie Rollouts

Worries about spoilers are compounded by recent **leaked footage from Avengers: Endgame Encore**, with unconfirmed reports suggesting it stemmed from early press or influencer screenings. If Doomsday follows industry standard practice, similar early screenings could make leaks a foregone conclusion, regardless of the adjusted UK release. Meanwhile, Marvel faces ongoing complaints over differences in theater experiences—such as only IMAX and Infinity Vision screens reportedly getting extra minutes in Endgame Encore—as well as general discontent with casting, character decisions, and late-stage MCU movie tweaks.

It’s uncertain whether the UK release shuffle will heighten these frustrations, but for now, fans worldwide are bracing for possible spoilers and a heated wait.
