X-Men '97 is still riding high after its acclaimed run, and Marvel just dropped fresh details at New York Comic Con that have fans buzzing. **Season 3 footage for X-Men '97 was unveiled, confirming that Marvel's animated revival isn’t slowing down, with new faces and classic mutants joining the roster.** The show's first two seasons set a high bar with near-perfect review scores, and it looks like the next chapter is ready to keep that streak alive.

## What’s New for Season 3

The newly-revealed footage from NYCC locked in a handful of big Marvel names set to appear in Season 3. **Namor, She-Hulk, and Deadpool will all be joining the action.** These additions land alongside the confirmed focus on the Brotherhood of Mutants, now led by Mystique, following Magneto's death in the previous season. Returning Brotherhood members will include Blob, Pyro, Toad, Avalanche, and Surge, while producers are also hinting at more deep-cut cameos still to come.

## Mutant Weddings and Marvel Cameos

Perhaps the biggest moment in Marvel’s preview was the tease of **Gambit and Rogue’s wedding**, an event that promises to shake up the series. Both Deadpool and She-Hulk were spotted in the wedding footage, raising questions about whether their appearances are genuine cameos or another one of Morph’s tricks. Either way, fans know from X-Men history that no mutant wedding comes without chaos, so expect drama when the episode lands.

Marvel also confirmed that Season 3 is slated to premiere in 2027, with work on a fourth season already underway—further proof that X-Men '97 has become a flagship for Marvel Studios’ animation efforts.
