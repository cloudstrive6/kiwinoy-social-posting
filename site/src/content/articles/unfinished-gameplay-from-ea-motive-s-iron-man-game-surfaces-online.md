Early gameplay footage from what appears to be **EA Motive Studio's Iron Man game** has leaked online, stirring up new excitement among Marvel fans. The footage, which began circulating across social platforms today, offers an extended look at an extremely unfinished build, featuring Tony Stark in full Iron Man mode. Notably, the video includes glimpses of what looks like a **S.H.I.E.L.D. helicarrier** and other recognizable Marvel characters, pointing to a project with an ambitious scope.

## What the Leaked Footage Shows

The gameplay includes sequences of Iron Man soaring beneath bridges and weaving through city environments, taking on armored foes with signature weapons. Players can expect classic Tony Stark moments too, such as **upgrading suit technology** and tinkering in his lab, hinting at a globe-trotting adventure. Combat appears explosive and frantic, with Iron Man firing missiles, unleashing arm-mounted lasers, and hurling projectiles at enemy tanks. While the leaked video is clearly from an early development stage, it leans heavily into the fantasy of suiting up as Iron Man and taking the fight worldwide.

## Development Still On Track

The leak comes amid speculation about the fate of EA Motive’s project following a long period of silence. Motive originally revealed its plans for an Iron Man game years ago but offered few updates since, leading some to question whether the game was still in production. The last official update came with reassurances that the title remains a priority for the studio—even as Motive contributed to other projects. The presence of today’s gameplay leak suggests that, contrary to recent rumors, **Iron Man is still very much in development** at the studio. However, EA declined to comment on the footage, and the actual release timeline remains unconfirmed. Notably, this isn’t the first Iron Man game footage to emerge online recently, but the Motive-developed title is the only one still moving forward.
